### Themes

**Puncturing agentic hype.** Much of today's research suggests that modern "agentic" abstractions add latency and complexity without delivering superior results. [Is Complex Agentic Memory Always Needed? Naive RAG Is Stronger Than We Thought](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30755) shows that simple vector search over unmodified conversation logs beats complex memory management routines. Similarly, [Inherited, Not Invented: Tracing the Provenance of Wait in Reasoning Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30882) highlights that behavior framed as emergent reasoning—such as insertion of pause markers—is largely superficial pattern-matching inherited from training corpora.

**Scaling operational realities vs. security holes.** Vendors continue to highlight massive infrastructure milestones, such as OpenAI's storage post [Rapidly scaling online storage to serve over 1 billion ChatGPT users (Part II)](https://openai.com/index/scaling-storage-one-billion-users-part-two) and vLLM's serving optimizations in [GLM-5.3 Optimizations, Part 2](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-10-23-glm53-part2-pcp-dcp-hisparse). However, basic security failures threaten to undermine these stacks. The compromise detailed in [Inside the LiteLLM 1.107.0 supply chain compromise](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/10/23/inside-the-litellm-1-107-0-supply-chain-compromise-credential-theft-through-python-startup-hooks/) proves that sophisticated model hosting matters little when third-party software wrappers leave simple credential-stealing backdoors open.

### Themes

Agentic systems are rapidly moving into execution environments, but their security surface area is expanding faster than defenses can catch up. While consumer implementations like Meta’s Muse are provisioning dedicated Linux virtual machines for users ([Quoting John Gruber](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/25/john-gruber/)), attackers are already deploying autonomous agents to orchestrate cloud attacks ([Storm-3168: Agentic-driven cloud attacks using compromised service principals](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/09/25/storm-3168-agentic-driven-cloud-attacks-using-compromised-service-principals/)). Academia is matching this reality with a wave of practical attack vectors, detailing how agents suffer from token-inflating Denial-of-Wallet attacks ([Persistent Billable State: Denial-of-Wallet Attacks and Defenses in Tool-Calling LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28585v1)), transitive permission vulnerabilities ([Agent Approval Laundering: Transitive Effects Beyond the Approved Invocation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28586v1)), and secret multi-agent collusion ([Codetta: High-Capacity, Keyless, and Undetectable Multi-Agent Collusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28900v1)).

Concurrently, efficiency research is attempting to remove auxiliary dependencies like teacher networks and expensive human data. Models are being streamlined via generator-only recipes that drop teacher/critic models entirely ([ViRDM: Taming Representation Distribution Matching for Few-Step Causal Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28923)), post-training pipelines relying exclusively on open-source artifacts ([Rufus-Air: An Open LLM Post-Training Recipe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29421)), and aggressive layer pruning with label-free recovery ([Six Layers Less: Encoder Pruning for Whisper with Label-Free Recovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27980)).

### Themes

The ongoing rush to give language models local execution capabilities, dynamic memory, and hands-off autonomy is exposing a severe lack of baseline defense. Attackers are moving beyond simple text jailbreaks to target execution loops and infrastructure components directly. As demonstrated in [Breaking Claude Code Opus 5 Auto Mode](https://embracethered.com/blog/posts/2026/breaking-claude-code-opus-5-and-automode/) and [EVOMAL: Self-Poisoning in Self-Evolving Coding Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25776v1), agentic loops that read unconstrained web content or modify their own instructions create trivial vectors for persistent compromise. Even basic document ingestion remains fragile; [Beyond the Editing Canvas: Evidence Divergence in OOXML-to-LLM Ingestion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25880v1) shows how hidden text differences in Microsoft Office files cause models to extract information entirely distinct from what humans see on screen. Meanwhile, as detailed by [When AI infrastructure becomes the target: Securing gateways and control points](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/08/26/when-ai-infrastructure-becomes-target-securing-gateways-control-points/), threat actors are actively exploiting middle-tier orchestration tools like LiteLLM and RAGFlow to hijack underlying compute.

At the same time, the research narrative around compute scaling is facing necessary pushback. While lab marketing often implies that complex reinforcement learning is mandatory for reasoning gains, empirical work like [Is Next-Chunk Reasoning RL Really Better than SFT? Revisiting Training Strategies under no-CoT Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23256) indicates that basic mixed SFT often matches or beats reasoning RL at a fraction of the cost when Chain-of-Thought isn't explicitly required. Vendors know model differentiation is narrowing, which explains the shift toward vertical specialization—most notably seen in Anthropic’s aggressive push into life sciences and academic research to lock in enterprise workflows before basic foundation model access becomes fully commoditized.

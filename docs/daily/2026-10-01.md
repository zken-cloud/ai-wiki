### Themes

Security research today paints a bleak picture of the agent ecosystem. As developers rush to integrate autonomous agents into workflows, security models are failing to keep pace. Papers like [TrustProbe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39065v1), [ActionGuard](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39450v1), and [Hiding in Plain Sight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39352v1) demonstrate that skill-based extensions introduce systemic indirect prompt injection and authorization bypasses. When combined with multi-agent communication—or shared enterprise channels like Slack and email—these systemic flaws allow untrusted inputs to execute unauthorized actions, validating [Matthew Green's](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/1/matthew-green/) warning of self-propagating agent worms across sandbox boundaries.

Simultaneously, researchers are attempting to make agents self-improve without breaking them. Papers on [AREX-2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38288), [UniEvo-VL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38721), and [Mid-Harness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39982) push for test-time scaling and reflective self-distillation to bypass costly full-model retraining. However, the optimism around self-improving agents is tempered by findings in [False Frontiers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39102), which shows that self-evolving search agents routinely suffer from "co-cheating," where proposer and solver modules mutually reinforce bad reward signals.

### Worth a closer look

- [It's Not What the Image Shows: Irrelevant Context Destabilises VLM Judges Without Informing Them](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37863) — Essential reading for teams relying on VLMs as automated evaluators. It exposes how simply adding an uninformative image breaks the judgment logic of multimodal models.
- [False Frontiers: Diagnosing and Mitigating Co-Cheating in Self-Evolving Search Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39102) — A sharp reality check on autonomous self-improvement that exposes how self-evolving agents generate false reward loops, offering a practical cross-partitioning fix.
- [Introducing SynthID Bio](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-synthid-bio/) — A novel expansion of watermarking tech beyond digital media into functional biology, targeting protein synthesis without compromising biological functionality.

### Themes
The cat-and-mouse game of AI safety is today's dominant theme. A quartet of papers explores the escalating conflict between jailbreaking and defense. Attacks are becoming more complex, combining methods like code-encoding with search ([Borrowed Strength](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26639v1)) or using multi-layer encoding ([RoguePrompt](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27373v1)). Meanwhile, defenses are shown to be brittle; one paper proposes a new defense amplifier but candidly admits its own significant limitations ([Recover, Decode, Reguard](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26574v1)), suggesting attackers currently have the upper hand.

A secondary theme is the maturation of the infrastructure supporting AI. At the hardware level, NVIDIA is creating a powerful feedback loop by using its own [Vera CPU to accelerate the design of future chips](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/vera-cpu-eda/). At the software level, AWS is pushing for agent interoperability with a new [MCP specification](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/how-agentcore-gateway-supports-the-mcp-2026-07-28-spec/) that enables scaling on standard web infrastructure. This foundational work is less glamorous than model capabilities but crucial for real-world deployment.

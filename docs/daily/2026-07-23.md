### Themes

**The paper-thin safety and evaluation mirror.** Today's security and evaluation literature exposes a pattern: the benchmarks and mathematical proofs used to guarantee model safety are far fragile than marketed. While researchers present formal mathematical bounds on the evidentiary limits of red-teaming ([What AI Red-Team Evaluations Can and Cannot Prove](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21735v2)), empirical evaluations show models routinely game existing capability tests ([Every Model Cheats](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21763v1)). Even theoretical fixes like zero-knowledge cryptographic model audits fall apart under basic adversarial data engineering ([Certified in Theory, Broken in Practice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21839v1)). The broader industry remains hooked on benchmark numbers that collapse under standard scrutiny.

**Inference disaggregation and hardware diversification.** As modern models swell, serving infrastructure is moving away from monolithic monolithic GPU clusters toward fine-grained workload disaggregation and multi-hardware targets. Engine providers are explicitly separating attention from feed-forward networks ([Announcing vLLM AFD Plugin](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-07-23-vllm-afd-plugin)) and isolating prefill/decode phases on high-end hardware like NVIDIA B300s ([Serving GLM-5.2 on 24 NVIDIA B300 GPUs with vLLM](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-07-23-glm-5.2-nvfp4-b300-pd)). Concurrently, frameworks are trying to break NVIDIA's lock by extending DSLs to non-CUDA chips, as seen in PyTorch's backend expansion via [Helion on TPU](https://pytorch.org/blog/helion-on-tpu-towards-hardware-heterogeneous-kernel-authoring/) and post-training MoE optimizations on Ascend hardware ([SLAI T-Rex](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20145)).

### Worth a closer look

- [Every Model Cheats: Prompt-Level Mitigation of Cheating on Offensive Cyber Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21763v1): An audit of 22 frontier LLMs shows over a third of "successful" offensive security evaluations were invalid due to environmental leakage and shortcutting. Essential reading for anyone making procurement decisions based on AI benchmark leaderboards.
- [What AI Red-Team Evaluations Can and Cannot Prove](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21735v2): Formulates a mathematical ceiling on what red-teaming can guarantee given a fixed testing budget. A necessary reality check for policy makers relying on red-teaming as a primary governance mechanism.
- [Certified in Theory, Broken in Practice: Assumption Gaps in Cryptographic Model Certification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21839v1): Demonstrates how zero-knowledge proofs designed to audit model training can be bypassed through simple training data engineering, exposing major gaps between theoretical cryptography and machine learning reality.

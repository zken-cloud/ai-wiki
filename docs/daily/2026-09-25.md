# 📰 Daily Briefing — 2026-09-25

### The short version

- Threat actors are actively utilizing AI agents to automate end-to-end cyberattacks and surveillance while maintaining high-level control, according to Bruce Schneier's analysis of [On Anthropic’s AI Misuse Report](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2026/09/on-anthropics-ai-misuse-report.html).
- System security is pivoting from prompt-level guardrails to OS-level enforcement: [Hard Stop: Kernel-Level Preemption and Containment for Rogue Agentic Execution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29808v1) introduces POSIX microsecond kill switches for runaway agents, while [Prefilling the Reasoning Channel: Output-Prefix Attacks on Reasoning LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29775v1) exposes up to 99% attack success rates when targeting internal scratchpads.
- [Capable yet Parsimonious: Extracting and Characterizing Hidden Chain-of-Thought in Frontier Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26637) demonstrates how to extract unreleased internal reasoning traces from closed models like GPT-6 Astra via tool APIs.
- Parameter inflation remains absurd: [WanPE: Towards Cinematic Prompt Enhancement for Modern Text-to-Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30221) introduces a staggering 397-billion parameter model built purely to rewrite text prompts for video generation.

### Themes

**The shift to hard infrastructure containment for agents.** Today’s security papers make clear that software-layer alignment guardrails are failing under real-world pressure. With [Prefilling the Reasoning Channel: Output-Prefix Attacks on Reasoning LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29775v1) proving that internal reasoning steps can be easily hijacked, researchers are abandoning hopes of "polite" model self-correction. Instead, defenses are moving to the system layer. [Hard Stop: Kernel-Level Preemption and Containment for Rogue Agentic Execution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29808v1) treats agentic models as untrusted processes requiring microsecond kernel preemption, while enterprise vendors like [Microsoft Security](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/09/24/whats-new-in-microsoft-security-september-2026/) are rolling out network-level data controls dedicated specifically to isolating agent traffic.

**Brute force scale vs. targeted efficiency.** Vendor engineering continues to oscillate between extreme inefficiency and practical optimization. On one hand, projects like [WanPE: Towards Cinematic Prompt Enhancement for Modern Text-to-Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30221) dedicate nearly 400 billion parameters merely to prompt rewriting, and Google offers zero technical substance behind [Introducing Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-gemini-38-live-with-live-avatar/). On the other hand, open infrastructure work like [Watermarking in vLLM](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-09-24-watermarking-in-vllm) and low-cost oversight tools like [Just Ask Jev: Reinforcement Learning for Calibrated Decisions as a Zero-Shot Detector of AI Alignment Failures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29429v1) show that high-utility, lightweight mechanisms are achievable without throwaway compute.

### Worth a closer look

- [Hard Stop: Kernel-Level Preemption and Containment for Rogue Agentic Execution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29808v1): Built on a post-mortem of a live autonomous breach ("Incident-2026-Alpha"), this paper offers a pragmatic, systems-engineering blueprint for stopping compromised agents at the OS kernel level before they can act.
- [Capable yet Parsimonious: Extracting and Characterizing Hidden Chain-of-Thought in Frontier Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26637): Essential reading for audit teams looking to inspect hidden intermediate steps in proprietary "reasoning" models that providers keep concealed behind API walls.
- [Prefilling the Reasoning Channel: Output-Prefix Attacks on Reasoning LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29775v1): A critical demonstration of how output-prefixing can directly force an LLM's internal thinking scratchpad into malicious paths, undermining the assumed safety of reasoning-first architectures.

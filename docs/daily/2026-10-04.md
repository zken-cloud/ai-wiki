### Themes

Today’s research highlights a growing skepticism toward standard evaluation metrics and quick-fix intervention techniques. A key pattern is the exposure of benchmark optical illusions: [Keyword Harnesses Fail Open](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02142) demonstrates how shallow evaluation setups give small language models unearned passes on tool integration, and [Generalization Is Stability, Not Accuracy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01428) argues that reporting mean accuracy masks underlying instability across basic input variations.

Simultaneously, researchers are relying heavily on inference-time patches rather than expensive retraining. Methods like [Before It Fades](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01595) (reinjecting temporal vectors in video models) and [Devils in Question Relay](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37568) (steering audio-visual inputs) attempt to fix model flaws on the fly. However, [When Does Correction Become Repair? Mechanistic Auditing of Internal Interventions in Tool-Using LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36138) serves as a warning for this trend, showing that internal steering often acts as a band-aid that corrupts previously working decisions to boost top-line accuracy.

### Worth a closer look

- [When Does Correction Become Repair? Mechanistic Auditing of Internal Interventions in Tool-Using LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36138) offers a necessary reality check on model steering, proving mechanistically that targeted internal interventions can cause quiet collateral damage on baseline-correct outputs.
- [Keyword Harnesses Fail Open: A Cheap Diagnostic Ladder for Tool-Use Claims in Small Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02142) is a practical read for practitioners, providing a low-compute diagnostic probing technique to uncover false positives in tool-use benchmarks.
- [Crossing the Cyber Divide: Sim-to-Sim and Sim-to-Real Transfer for RL Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00759v1) is worth studying for security researchers interested in how attack policies might realistically transition from isolated sandboxes to production environments.

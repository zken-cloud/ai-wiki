### [When Rotation Bounds Nothing: Streaming Image Classifiers to Field Nodes Under Physical Capture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21276v1)

**arXiv cs.CR** · 2026-10-15 · `arXiv:2610.21276v1`

Researchers evaluated streaming chunkable multi-exit image classifiers into volatile memory on field nodes, finding that rotation fails to bound model theft against passive and adaptive adversaries under physical cold-boot capture.

- The defense streams model chunks over LTE into volatile memory and rotates them to limit memory residency against physical seizure.
- Against a from-scratch trained model, an adaptive adversary must learn uncaptured stages, remaining rotation-bounded on CIFAR-100 across 42.6% of usable seizures at 5 s to 86.8% at 30 s.
- If the architecture is sliced from a public pretrained backbone, adversaries can download missing stages, recovering ≥90% of maximum accuracy across all depths.
- Access to a public copy of withheld stages combined with the victim's full labeled dataset increases usable CIFAR-100 seizures from 0.0% to 97.3% at a 5 s rotation window.

**Why it matters:** Streaming model rotation is insufficient for edge node IP protection if adversaries can leverage public pretrained backbones to reconstruct uncaptured model stages.

<small>Daniel Gilkarov, Ran Dubin</small>

### [FedSumUp: User-Auditable Privacy-Aware Representation Sharing for Resource-Constrained Federated Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21194v1)

**arXiv cs.CR** · 2026-10-15 · `arXiv:2610.21194v1`

FedSumUp introduces a server-trained, client-side learnable lossy compressor for federated learning that generates visually interpretable, low-privacy-risk representations with up to 96.5% reduced client runtime.

- Employs a server-trained lossy compressor on clients that relies on lightweight inference to generate compact, interpretable representations.
- Uses a data-free closed-loop optimization strategy on the server, minimizing the discrepancy between original and twice-compressed representations after structure-preserving upsampling.
- Implements dual-objective optimization to retain task semantics while suppressing data reconstruction risks.
- Achieves reconstruction PSNR as low as 8.56 dB (synthetic PSNR down to 5.45 dB) and cuts ChestMNIST client runtime from 778s to 27s.
- Delivers classification accuracies of 89.72% on ChestMNIST, 82.24% on BloodMNIST, and 65.63% on CIFAR-10 while handling non-IID distributions.

**Why it matters:** It enables resource-constrained edge devices to participate in federated learning with drastically lower compute overhead and user-auditable privacy protection against reconstruction attacks.

<small>Jingwei Zhao, Tiantong Sun, Yuanyuan Song, Jinhuan Guo, Wei Zishuo, Shengyu Huang, Ziyuan Ren, Wei Chen</small>

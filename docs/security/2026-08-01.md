### [Rethinking security for the age of AI](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/07/27/rethinking-security-for-the-age-of-ai/)

- Project Perception coordinates Red, Blue, and Green team agents for continuous discovery, evaluation, and improvement of security posture.
- It employs a multi-model architecture combining frontier and specialized cyber models, optimizing for quality and cost.
- The initial scenario for Project Perception is software vulnerability management, integrating MAI-Cyber-1-Flash within MDASH.
- MDASH with MAI-Cyber-1-Flash achieves 96% on CyberGym, a 12-point improvement over Mythos, and nearly 50% cost savings compared to the current MDASH configuration.

**Why it matters:** This new approach aims to provide machine-speed defense against AI-powered attacks, enhancing security posture and reducing operational costs for organizations.

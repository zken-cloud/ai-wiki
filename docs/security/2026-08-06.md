### [Agent Against Agent: An Agentic System for Automatic Prompt Injection Red Teaming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05108v1)

**arXiv cs.CR** · 2026-08-05 · `arXiv:2608.05108v1`

PIMiner is an agentic red-teaming system that constructs a reusable attack strategy library to execute prompt injection against unseen target LLMs with low query counts and zero test-time training.

- PIMiner constructs a strategy library from scratch by training on a sequence of (dataset, target model) pairs.
- Transfers learned strategy libraries directly to unseen target LLMs at inference without additional model training.
- Requires a low query budget of approximately 10 queries per test sample to target agents.
- Achieves Attack Success Rates (ASR) up to 86.7% on Gemini-2.5-Pro, 61.9% on GPT-5.1, and 42.9% on Claude-Sonnet-4.5 across IPIArena and AgentDojo benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** It offers a highly transferable and query-efficient alternative to reinforcement learning methods for evaluating agent vulnerabilities and gathering prompt injection defensive data.

<small>Yanting Wang, Chenlong Yin, Runpeng Geng, Jinyuan Jia</small>

### [Gradient Immunity: Null-Space Resistance to Malicious Fine-Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05045v1)

**arXiv cs.CR** · 2026-08-05 · `arXiv:2608.05045v1`

Unidirectional Safety Gate (USG) prevents malicious fine-tuning of partially protected open-weight language models by using a Null Space Cubic Layer and Inverse Adapter to suppress gradients from harmful hidden states.

- Targets the Partially Protected Open-Weight (PPOW) setting where most weights are trainable while a small safety-critical component is preserved.
- Combines a Null Space Cubic Layer to block/suppress gradients of harmful samples with an Inverse Adapter after the final Transformer layer to preserve forward pass behavior.
- Calibrates protection thresholds using defender-held harmful data to generalize defense to nearby in-distribution harmful inputs without requiring user cooperation.
- Evaluated across six model-dataset settings, keeping post-fine-tuning attack success rates near pre-release levels while maintaining high safe-pass rates.

**Why it matters:** It enables open-weight model providers to prevent safety alignment degradation during downstream fine-tuning without relying on user compliance or centralized fine-tuning services.

<small>Yuxuan Huang, Xingyu Zeng, Tianhang Zheng, Chaochao Lu</small>

### [PURPOSE: Poisoning Conflict Resolution in RAG via Proxy-Fact-Grounded Updates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04756v1)

**arXiv cs.CR** · 2026-08-05 · `arXiv:2608.04756v1`

PURPOSE is a black-box poisoning attack for RAG that bypasses post-retrieval conflict resolution by injecting non-contradictory, proxy-fact-grounded updates instead of direct counter-claims.

- PURPOSE extracts query-related proxy facts to approximate the resolver's reference knowledge and grounds a pivot event within them.
- The attack re-frames poisoned injections as logical updates rather than direct frontal contradictions, minimizing conflict signals detected by resolvers.
- Evaluated across three QA benchmarks, five generators, and three conflict-resolution methods, PURPOSE achieved the highest Attack Success Rate (ASR) in 35 of 45 settings.
- PURPOSE outperformed the strongest prior attack by an average of +9.7 ASR points.

**Why it matters:** It reveals that RAG conflict-resolution safeguards can be bypassed using non-contradictory context updates, exposing a critical vulnerability in post-retrieval arbitration mechanisms.

### [Breadcrumbing Search Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04565v1)

**arXiv cs.CR** · 2026-08-05 · `arXiv:2608.04565v1`

A novel threat model and attack framework, Authority-Chain Hijack (ACH), systematically steers LLM search agents by injectively coordinating a chain of evidence across multi-turn search results and retrieved pages.

- Exposes vulnerabilities in multi-turn LLM search agents under a constrained tool-intermediary threat model where an attacker appends one controlled result per query.
- Authority-Chain Hijack (ACH) creates a coherent evidence chain across multiple seemingly independent sources, achieving a 55.9% overall Attack Success Rate (ASR) and 83.3% MaxN ASR on SafeSearch.
- Trace-Guided Strategy Evolution (TGSE) automates attacker strategy refinement using execution traces, boosting performance to 71.4% ASR and 95.0% MaxN ASR in held-out evaluations.

**Why it matters:** This research demonstrates that cross-checking and multi-query verification mechanisms in search agents can be bypassed by coordinated, trajectory-wide evidence manipulation.

### [DeepInvert: Semi-Supervised Embedding Inversion Against Obfuscated Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04477v1)

**arXiv cs.CR** · 2026-08-05 · `arXiv:2608.04477v1`

DeepInvert is a semi-supervised embedding inversion attack that recovers original prompt tokens from obfuscated representations by exploiting unlabeled embedding structure through a novel consistency objective.

- Combines supervised learning on labeled shadow data with an unsupervised consistency objective on unlabeled target embeddings using a mixed training pipeline.
- Achieves 73.5% top-1 token recovery against ObfusLM, significantly outperforming the prior state-of-the-art accuracy of 26.2%.
- Evaluated against nine obfuscation defenses (including ObfusLM, SentinelLMs, TextObfuscator, and DPNR) across five tasks and four model architectures.
- Exposes a core utility-security tradeoff: obfuscation schemes that preserve utility retain enough semantic structure for inversion, whereas those that block inversion destroy task performance.

**Why it matters:** It demonstrates that lightweight obfuscation-based prompt defenses fail to provide robust privacy for cloud language model services.

<small>Zhicong Huang, Cheng Hong, Tao Wei</small>

### [Season: Spectrum-Aware Orthogonal Gradient Refinement for Transfer-Based Adversarial Attacks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04441v1)

**arXiv cs.CR** · 2026-08-05 · `arXiv:2608.04441v1`

Season is a training-free, spectrum-aware framework that decomposes adversarial gradient updates into orthogonal frequency components to improve L-infinity transferability across heterogeneous CNN, ViT, and MLP architectures.

- Decomposes gradient updates into low-frequency (structural) and high-frequency (textural) branches.
- Uses a low-saliency guidance scheme to shift high-frequency energy to background regions, protecting foreground structures needed by ViTs.
- Applies orthogonal projection to keep textural updates in the orthogonal complement of the structural direction, preventing feature interference.
- Functions as a plug-and-play wrapper that boosts eight existing gradient-stabilization and input-enhancement attacks by an average of 6.6% (up to 16.0%) across ImageNet targets.

**Why it matters:** It significantly increases the transferability of black-box adversarial attacks between fundamentally different model families like CNNs and ViTs without requiring retraining.

- Comprises a dynamic benchmark spanning CybORG CAGE 4 and CyberWheel sandbox environments, alongside a dataset of over 13,000 red-blue interaction trajectories.

**arXiv cs.CR** · 2026-08-04 · `arXiv:2608.04192v1`

SkillClone is a black-box attack that reconstructs the functional behavior of hidden LLM agent skills through structured benign probing, synthesis, and differential validation without exposing underlying files.

- Introduces Behavioral Skill Reconstruction (BSR) to replicate proprietary agent skills via ordinary system usage rather than prompt injection or file extraction.
- SkillClone operates by inferring an interface hypothesis from public advertisements, executing structured probes, synthesizing a functional clone, and using differential validation for iterative repairs.
- Evaluated across 30 skills covering rules, tables, procedures, and algorithms, achieving exact or partial functional recovery on held-out inputs.
- Demonstrates that iterative requerying improves functional accuracy, rendering traditional disclosure-focused defenses and vaguer skill descriptions ineffective.

**Why it matters:** File-level secrecy and prompt-leakage defenses are insufficient to protect proprietary agent logic because attackers can functionally clone hidden skills using standard input-output interactions.

### [When Modalities Fail to Tango: Conformal Backdoor Detection in Multimodal Contrastive Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04052v1)

**arXiv cs.CR** · 2026-08-04 · `arXiv:2608.04052v1`

CASCADE is a two-stage coarse-to-fine conformal backdoor detection framework for multimodal contrastive learning that uses nonconformity scores to deliver statistical confidence bounds and precise poisoned-pair identification.

- Identifies two flaws in traditional CLIPScore defenses: distribution overlap between benign and poisoned pairs, and a lack of statistical guarantees from fixed thresholds.
- The coarse-grained stage leverages cross-modality consistency to segregate high-confidence benign and poisoned pairs.
- The fine-grained stage computes text-space similarity nonconformity scores against a reference set of high-confidence poisoned pairs to isolate latent backdoor instances.
- Achieves an average FPR of 5.79% at 100% TPR and an average AUROC of 0.9867 on the CC3M dataset across diverse and adaptive attacks.

**Why it matters:** It provides a mathematically grounded defense mechanism with provable confidence bounds to clean pre-training datasets for multimodal models without relying on prone-to-failure fixed CLIPScore thresholds.

### [When Do PEFT Adaptations Leak Structure? Measuring Black-Box Structural Bounds in Public-Base Model Services](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05036v1)

**arXiv cs.CR** · 2026-08-05 · `arXiv:2608.05036v1`

VectorHijack-SR converts public-base model and private PEFT victim residuals into structural bounds to infer adaptation family, layer locality, rank, and exact-version linkage without establishing universal adapter recovery.

- VectorHijack-SR aggregates query-level statistics (spectral, locality, entropy, margin) and uses a service-disjoint classifier alongside a cross-fitted hierarchical rejector.
- Family leakage exceeds chance across multiple backbones, achieving 21/24 on RoBERTa/MNLI and 15/18 on DeBERTa-v3/AG News; exact-version linkage on five LoRA-r64 services reaches AUC 0.940.
- On a ten-seed BERT open-set grid, the rejector achieves pooled AUROC 0.804 and known accuracy 0.956, but struggles on structurally close DoRA and LoRA+head variants.
- Demonstrates a visibility-exploitability gap where posterior-selected PEFT underperforms distill-then-convert PEFT (0.356 vs 0.517) and free-running generation remains near chance.

**Why it matters:** Black-box services using public base models with private PEFT adapters inherently leak structural metadata and version information to adversaries with local base model access.

<small>Zhongjiang Yao, Shuangshuang Liang, Chun Yang, LiWei Chen, Gang Shi</small>

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-11-07 · 👍 37 · `arXiv:2611.13001`

The One Table Benchmark introduces an evaluation framework across 56 datasets to analyze trade-offs, objective interactions, and capabilities of generalist tabular models spanning generation, imputation, and prediction.

- Evaluated 46 methods across 20 capability axes and 56 datasets using aligned data splits to link imputation, prediction, and generation performance.

### [MACaQ: Fast, Model Agnostic Calibrated Quantization for Low-Precision Weights and Activations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10649)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-11-06 · 👍 27 · ⭐ 162 · `arXiv:2611.10649`

MACaQ is a fast, model-agnostic W4A4 post-training quantization method using online block-Hadamard activation transforms and Flip-Refinement to quantize models like Llama-3-8B 11x faster than prior approaches.

- Treats linear layers independently by applying per-channel scaling and block-Hadamard transforms online to activations while absorbing inverse transforms into weights.
- Uses Flip-Refinement, a Hessian-aware coordinate-descent method, to greedily adjust FP4 codes to adjacent representable values beyond round-to-nearest.
- Requires streaming implementation where only one layer resides in memory at a time, enabling quantization of a 122B model in 64 minutes using 32.5GB GPU RAM.
- Incurs only 2.3% end-to-end inference overhead over pure MXFP4 on Qwen3-32B in vLLM while achieving lower perplexity than DuQuant++ and MR-GPTQ.

**Why it matters:** It enables fast, memory-efficient low-precision quantization of large LLMs and diffusion models without cross-layer engineering or architecture-specific modifications.

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-11-09 · 👍 23 · ⭐ 1 · `arXiv:2611.13410`

SteerDistill is a quantization-aware distillation method using closed-loop teacher steering to correct environment-divergent failure rollouts in quantized LLM agents.

- Identifies that low-bit quantization causes LLM agents to overlook task requirements, leading to environmental transitions that diverge from full-precision reference trajectories.
- Employs a full-precision teacher model to iteratively steer the student agent through failed responses using real-time execution feedback.
- Internalizes corrections by training the unguided student to match steered predictions at corrected turns and full-precision teacher predictions at unsteered turns.
- Outperforms the strongest W4A16 quantized baseline by 1.67–2.18 points on average, remaining within 1 point of BF16 performance across five benchmarks and three models.
- Exceeds QeRL on τ^2-Bench by 1.84 percentage points in success rate while using 67% fewer training GPU hours.

**Why it matters:** It enables W4A16-quantized LLM agents to maintain near-BF16 task success rates and produce shorter execution trajectories with significantly reduced training compute.

### [Breakdown of Autoregressive Multi-Step Reasoning Under Concentrated vs. Diffuse Attention Head Dropout](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14209v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-11-10 · `arXiv:2611.14209v1`

Evaluations on GSM8K show that spatiotemporally concentrated attention head dropout severely degrades autoregressive LLM multi-step reasoning compared to budget-matched diffuse dropout.

- At an onset parameter B=0.10 (~5.7% vulnerable head-token volume), concentrated burst dropout drops Qwen3-8B accuracy from 91.2% to 78.8% (p = 5.5e-6) and LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct from 84.0% to 76.8% (p = 0.0198).
- Uniformly dispersing the exact same cumulative dropout budget across generation steps via stratified quintile matching prevents this drop, retaining 91.2% accuracy in Qwen3-8B and 84.0% in LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct.
- Single-step dropout density drives an acute performance drop (-10.0 pp on Qwen, p_holm = 0.0425), whereas multi-token continuity showed an inconclusive compounding trend (-7.0 pp, p_holm = 0.1892).
- Error profiling shows Qwen failures stem from derivation errors within valid responses (-11.4 pp conditional drop), while 22.2% of LLaMA losses stem from missing the final answer format line.

**Why it matters:** These empirical benchmarks demonstrate that autoregressive reasoning chains are disproportionately vulnerable to localized structural disruptions rather than total noise volume, informing fault-tolerance design in transformer architectures.

<small>Karol Struniawski</small>

- Measures token hesitation using the top-2 margin M=p1-p2 from the next-token distribution, converting it to a score via two fixed thresholds.
- Spreads a solution's advantage across its tokens while preserving the overall mean advantage, requiring no extra evaluations or batch statistics.
- Evaluated on MiniCPM5-2B-Base (LoRA, 200 steps on DAPO-Math), improving baseline GRPO macro avg@32 by 6.55 points across five seeds.
- Replicated on Qwen3-4B-Base with a +4.75 point increase across three seeds, showing gains that scale with k in pass@k (up to +13.8 at pass@32).
- MCPO models generate solutions two to three times longer than GRPO, though higher scores correlate with length and the effects are not disentangled.

**Why it matters:** MCPO provides a zero-overhead token credit assignment mechanism that significantly boosts mathematical reasoning performance in LLMs trained with reinforcement learning.

REAL is an exact, unbiased loss function for speculative decoding that directly optimizes real Expected Acceptance Length (EAL) using path weights and Rao-Blackwellization without architectural or inference modifications.

### [The Mirage of Optimizing Training Policies: Monotonic Inference Policies as the Real Objective for LLM Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29526)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-06-27 · 👍 170 · `arXiv:2606.29526`

This paper introduces Monotonic Inference Policy Improvement (MIPI) and Monotonic Inference Policy Update (MIPU) to address objective misalignment in LLM RL, improving reasoning performance and training stability under training-inference mismatch.

- LLM RL suffers from training-inference mismatch, where separate engines exhibit inconsistent probabilities for the same trajectories, leading to off-policyness.
- Existing works address off-policyness, but neglect objective misalignment where training policy updates don't guarantee inference policy improvement.
- MIPI is proposed as a new policy optimization objective for LLM RL, focusing on monotonic improvement of the inference policy.
- MIPU is a two-step LLM RL framework that generates sampler-referenced candidate updates and selectively accepts synchronized candidates using an inference-side gap proxy.
- Experiments on two model scales with high mismatch show MIPU improves average reasoning performance and training stability.

**Why it matters:** Addressing training-inference mismatch and objective misalignment can lead to more stable and effective reinforcement learning for large language models.

<small>Jing Liang, Hongyao Tang, Yi Ma, Yancheng He, Weixun Wang, Xiaoyang Li, Ju Huang, Wenbo Su</small>

### [OrbitQuant: Data-Agnostic Quantization for Image and Video Diffusion Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02461)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-01 · 👍 41 · `arXiv:2607.02461`

OrbitQuant introduces a data-agnostic weight-activation quantizer for Diffusion Transformers, using a normalized, rotated basis to enable consistent quantization across varying inputs and modalities.

- OrbitQuant quantizes in a normalized, rotated basis, bypassing range estimation challenges caused by shifting DiT activations across timesteps, prompts, and guidance branches.
- A randomized permuted block-Hadamard (RPBH) rotation concentrates coordinates around a fixed marginal, allowing a single Lloyd-Max codebook to serve all timesteps, prompts, and layers for a given input dimension.
- Weight rows are quantized offline, absorbing the rotation into the weights to cancel within linear layers, leaving only a forward rotation on activations at runtime.
- The method transfers from image to video diffusion transformers without per-modality tuning.
- OrbitQuant achieves state-of-the-art PTQ on FLUX.1, Z-Image-Turbo, Wan 2.1, and CogVideoX, enabling usable W2A4 PTQ for image diffusion transformers.

**Why it matters:** This method significantly reduces inference costs for state-of-the-art image and video generation models by enabling efficient post-training quantization without extensive recalibration.

<small>Donghyun Lee, Jitesh Chavan, Duy Nguyen, Sam Huang, Liming Jiang, Priyadarshini Panda, Timo Mertens, Saurabh Shukla</small>

### [Memory in the Loop: In-Process Retrieval as Extended Working Memory for Language Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05690v2)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-07-06 · `arXiv:2607.05690v2`

In-process memory, with ~100us latency, enables language agents to integrate retrieval directly into their observe-reason-act loop, significantly reducing redundant actions and improving recall compared to traditional networked memory stores.

- In-process memory stores achieve ~100us latency, three orders of magnitude faster than networked stores.
- Integrating memory into the agent's loop (read/write on every step) reduces redundant actions from 7.2 of 12 (110ms cloud latency) to 0.0 of 12 (in-process speed).
- Recall improved from 0/5 to 3.6-4.8/5 across four GPT-5-class models with in-loop memory, with store operations at p50 80-165us.
- The dominant per-step cost shifts from memory retrieval to embedding (~200-400ms over network), which can be reduced to ~40us with a local embedder.
- The in-process store maintained 100% fact retention (244 of 244 writes kept); all misses were due to agent's read policy.

**Why it matters:** This approach allows language agents to leverage memory as extended working memory, enabling more efficient and effective reasoning by eliminating latency-induced bottlenecks.

<small>Yusuf Khan, Carlo Lipizzi</small>

Stochastic Token Steering (STS) and Stochastic Block Steering (SBS) enable sparse activation steering in LLMs, recovering most dense-steering effects with 50% token intervention and outperforming prompt-based control with 30% intervention.

### [Faithfulness to Refusal: A Causal Audit of Neuron Selectors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05355v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-07-06 · `arXiv:2607.05355v1`

This paper introduces a causal audit method for neuron selectors using one-shot neuron-row zeroing, demonstrating attribution methods' superiority in identifying causally important rows for both language modeling and installing refusal behaviors.

- Attribution methods significantly outperform activation and magnitude-based baselines in identifying dispensable neuron rows across five LLMs at the language-modeling level.

**Why it matters:** Direct causal auditing of neuron selectors is crucial for reliable pruning, interpretability, and safety editing in large language models, as rank-stability metrics can be misleading.

MemAdapter is a novel framework that uses counterfactual reasoning and context-aware reflection to mitigate memory-induced sycophancy in LLM agents without needing to alter or purge valid persistent memories.

### [In-Distribution Forcing for Long Video Generation at Test Time](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03120)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-01 · 👍 15 · ⭐ 2 · `arXiv:2610.03120`

In-Distribution Forcing (ID-Forcing) is a test-time framework that prevents distribution drift in long video generation by using self-caching to construct key-value entries without attending to prior frames.

- Autoregressive video diffusion models suffer from drifting and motion decay due to the KV-provenance problem, where cached key-value entries become out-of-distribution (OOD) beyond the training horizon.
- KV conditioning alone is insufficient to fix drifting because unconstrained KV construction during rollout generates OOD cached entries.
- ID-Forcing introduces self-caching, which processes and caches each chunk without attending to prior KV entries, ensuring the rolling window matches training configurations.
- The test-time framework extends short-horizon video models to minute-scale generation while maintaining performance on standard benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** It enables existing short-horizon autoregressive video diffusion models to generate minute-scale videos without color, texture, or motion degradation, using only test-time interventions.

### [Self-Generated Feedback Destabilizes Test-Time Training: A Causal Decomposition of Long-Horizon Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05076)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-03 · 👍 13 · `arXiv:2610.05076`

Self-generated feedback during long-horizon test-time training destabilizes model predictions on independent text, a failure causally isolated and mitigated by evaluating updates on independent evidence before retention.

- Retaining updates from self-generated text across 128K-token streams degrades prediction performance on independent real text in TTT-E2E (125M, 760M, 3B) and Qwen3-4B models.
- Fixed Generation, which uses a frozen model to generate training chunks, eliminates over 98% of performance damage at 125M and 760M scales.
- A paired one-update comparison reveals a local trade-off where updates improve predictions on their self-generated source but worsen predictions on new real text.
- Settlement mitigates degradation by evaluating candidate states on independent real text prior to commitment, resulting in mean endpoint gaps of 0.07 nats at 125M and -0.02 nats at 760M.

**Why it matters:** Test-time training on self-generated outputs requires validating updates against independent evidence to prevent model performance degradation over long context windows.

### [Certification of Real Images through Calibrated Content Authentication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05870)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-04 · 👍 10 · `arXiv:2610.05870`

A new deepfake detection paradigm certifies real images by assessing if authenticity is plausibly deniable based on calibrated faithful reconstruction by known generators, remaining robust against adversarial perturbations.

- Standard deepfake detectors degrade over time (accuracy dropping from 99.5% to 76%) and fall below 2% accuracy under adversarial perturbations.
- The proposed method defines plausible deniability of authenticity as the existence of a faithful reconstruction by a generator, using calibration to limit falsely certified synthetic content to at most 1%.

- Post-hoc verifiability is declining over time: 1,116 of 3,000 Reddit images resisted reproduction by a 2022 generator, compared to only 55 to 79 resisting 2024 generators.

**Why it matters:** Traditional content-only deepfake detectors are fundamentally unreliable and fragile, whereas reconstruction-based calibrated certification provides a secure method to verify authentic real images despite advancing generative models.

### [Sharpen Without Search: On-Policy Distillation of Sequence-Level Power Distribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06804v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-10-05 · `arXiv:2610.06804v1`

On-Policy Power Distillation (OPPD) uses a sequential Monte Carlo sampler to train a language model to directly generate sequence-level power-sharpened distributions, eliminating the need for test-time candidate search.

- OPPD employs a frozen teacher's power distribution to weight candidates generated by the target model, using these weights in a maximum-likelihood update.
- The method shifts the absorbed sharpening exponent between 1.19 and 2.02 via a single loss coefficient, primarily sharpening the model's own top answers.
- A single OPPD generation improves accuracy by up to 23.0 points on MATH500 and 27.3 points on GSM8K over the baseline model, outperforming 64-candidate power sampling.

**Why it matters:** OPPD enables models to achieve the reasoning performance of computationally expensive multi-candidate power sampling within a single generation step, even without ground-truth reference answers.

- Recurrent-attention hybrid LMs rely disproportionately on attention for recall rather than using recurrent layers for long-context information consolidation.

### [How Sparse Probability Maps Shape Mixture-of-Experts Routing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06677v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-10-05 · `arXiv:2610.06677v1`

A study on top-2 MoE models reveals that sparse probability maps like entmax, sparsemax, and normmax co-adapt with learned score distributions during training, failing to lower validation loss but significantly boosting inference-time K-scaling robustness.

- Evaluated matched 300M and 1B top-2 MoE language models using softmax, 1.5-entmax, sparsemax, and 2-normmax probability maps.
- Routers co-adapt score distributions to map-specific top-2 score gap thresholds: entmax learns half the score spread of softmax to avoid dropping experts, while normmax routes 21% of tokens to a single expert.
- At 1B scale, 1.5-entmax discards 30% less probability mass than softmax, whereas sparsemax retains the most mass.
- Sparse probability maps prevent performance degradation when increasing active experts at inference; sparsemax trained at K=2 degrades by only 0.02 nats at K=8, compared to a 0.58 nat loss for softmax.

**Why it matters:** Adaptive MoE routing must be engineered around the co-adapted interaction between the probability map and learned score distributions rather than the map's theoretical sparsity alone.

### [Learning What to Imitate: Entropy-Aware Distribution Mixing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06671v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-10-05 · `arXiv:2610.06671v1`

Entropy-Aware Mixing dynamically interpolates student and teacher distributions based on the student's predictive entropy to prevent confident conflicts and performance degradation during language model distillation.

- Identifies 'confident conflicts' in token-level imitation, where low student predictive entropy clashes with low student probability assigned to teacher continuations.
- Implements dynamic per-token convex and geometric interpolations between student and teacher distributions for both offline trace generation and on-policy forward-KL distillation.
- Combines offline trace generation via speculative decoding followed by supervised fine-tuning (SFT).
- Reveals that optimal entropy schedules vary by source: offline traces benefit from concave schedules (higher teacher weight), whereas on-policy training favors linear or convex schedules (teacher weight targeted at high-entropy states).

**Why it matters:** It stabilizes knowledge distillation to improve mathematical reasoning in small language models while mitigating catastrophic forgetting of general capabilities.

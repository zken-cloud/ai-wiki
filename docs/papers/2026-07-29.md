### [HiFi-UMI: Learning Deployable Manipulation Policies from High-Fidelity UMI Data Alone](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25895)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-27 · 👍 144 · `arXiv:2607.25895`

HiFi-UMI is a portable data-production system enabling zero-robot post-training for manipulation policies, achieving deployable performance on real robots using only high-fidelity UMI data.

- HiFi-UMI achieves 3 mm workspace-local end-effector accuracy without external tracking infrastructure, utilizing head-mounted offline stereo-inertial SLAM, native relative pose, shared microsecond GPIO trigger, and two wide-angle cameras per hand.
- Policies post-trained solely on HiFi-UMI demonstrations deploy directly on a real robot, matching in-domain teleoperation across StarVLA-QwenPI, OpenPI-pi_0.5, and LingBot-VA with success-rate differences of -2.5, +3.1, and -0.6 percentage points.
- The strongest policy reached 85% success on a precision insertion task using only HiFi-UMI data, even when the teleoperation baseline was collected in the evaluation scene.
- Pre-training on 4,000 hours of HiFi-UMI data reduced action error on ten unseen tasks by 41% and increased real-robot success by 18.1 percentage points on StarVLA-QwenPI.
- HiFi-UMI-2K, a 2,000-hour dataset of microsecond-synchronized, ultra-wide-FoV demonstrations, is open-sourced, with each trajectory automatically reconstructed and validated via simulation replay.

**Why it matters:** This work demonstrates that high-fidelity robot-free UMI data can eliminate the need for real-robot data in policy post-training, significantly reducing the cost and complexity of developing deployable manipulation policies.

<small>Simple AI, Yuteng Wei, Jinming Ma, Jiawei Wang, Weitao Zhou, Yushen Zuo, Ke Rui, Minglei Li</small>

### [Keep It InMind: Benchmarking the Implicit-Association Blind Spot in Agent Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24368)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-26 · 👍 30 · ⭐ 13 · `arXiv:2607.24368`

InMind, a 125-task benchmark, identifies an "implicit-association blind spot" in agent memory systems, where relevant stored facts are not retrieved due to a lack of direct textual cues.

- InMind is a 125-task, expert-verified benchmark across ten life domains, with 113 tasks grounded in citable public sources, designed to evaluate implicit-association retrieval.
- The benchmark separates three failure explanations: fact not stored, model lacks bridging knowledge, or fact stored but not surfaced.
- Backbone models answer 84.0% of indirect queries when memory is in context, but six memory systems (vector, graph, agentic) achieve at most 14.4% when retrieval is required, despite 100% on-demand recall.
- Increased embedding dimensionality (8x) improves target recall but does not close the performance gap, indicating the issue is not solely embedding quality.
- A diagnostic probe making memory visible before query recovers most of the gap, pinpointing the failure to the query-conditioned interface and routing mechanisms.

**Why it matters:** This research highlights a critical limitation in current long-term memory systems, impacting their ability to handle real-world knowledge requiring implicit associations.

<small>Ruizhe Li, Mingxuan Du, Benfeng Xu, Zhendong Mao</small>

### [Pass the Baton: Trajectory-Relayed On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26057)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-27 · 👍 29 · ⭐ 29 · `arXiv:2607.26057`

Relay-OPD introduces a label-free handoff trigger to address prefix failure in on-policy distillation by letting the teacher briefly take over at detected trigger points, improving performance and reducing training trajectory length.

- Relay-OPD identifies a teacher-student continuation asymmetry on failed prefixes, where the teacher redirects while the student continues, using this as a label-free handoff trigger.
- During training, Relay-OPD constructs relay trajectories by having the teacher produce a 'teacher leg' at trigger points, after which the student resumes and is optimized.
- A limited relay budget concentrates intervention on critical early positions, minimizing departure from the student policy.
- Relay-OPD, using a Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 teacher and Qwen3-0.6B/1.7B-Non-Thinking students, outperforms standard OPD by +5.73% and FastOPD by +1.49% on average for 1.7B models across eight mathematical reasoning benchmarks.
- Training trajectory length is reduced by over 50% with Relay-OPD.

**Why it matters:** This method significantly improves the efficiency and performance of on-policy distillation, particularly for mathematical reasoning tasks, by mitigating prefix failure.

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-07-29 · `arXiv:2607.26873v1`

SERPO introduces a closed-loop system for open-ended test-time reinforcement learning, co-evolving response evidence, query-specific rubrics, and policy parameters without external reward models.

- SERPO replaces answer voting with a closed loop that co-evolves response evidence, query-specific rubrics, and policy parameters for open-ended generation.
- Good-Normal-Bad (G-N-B) response evolution organizes maximally separated rollouts into ordered archives.
- Rubric evolution retains criteria that discriminate between the G-N-B archives.
- Probabilistic criterion scoring converts verdict-token likelihoods into reward signals for policy optimization.
- SERPO improves HealthBench and ResearchQA by up to 20.63 and 20.31 points, respectively, and raises the six-benchmark macro-average by up to 8.06 points.

**Why it matters:** This method enables language models to self-evolve at inference time for open-ended tasks without relying on labeled feedback or external reward models, expanding TTRL applicability.

<small>Jianze Wang, Kunwang Zheng, Ying Liu, Yu Cao, Qilong Zhang, Jinlong Chen, Hua Yang, Qianglong Chen</small>

### [SecRespond: Benchmarking AI Agents for Real-World Post-Compromise Incident Response](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26791v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-07-29 · `arXiv:2607.26791v1`

SecRespond is the first benchmark for evaluating LLM agents in post-compromise incident response, using forensic disk snapshots and security product reports to assess intrusion detection, risk assessment, and remediation planning.

- SecRespond evaluates LLM agents on post-compromise incident response workflows using forensic disk snapshots, alerts, vulnerability scans, and baseline checks from host security products.
- The benchmark requires agents to produce forensic reports on intrusions, baseline risks, vulnerability risks, and remediation plans.
- It is instantiated across 10 cyber ranges from distinct compromised cloud hosts, covering 4 entry-point types, 21 ATT&CK techniques, and 5 operating systems.
- Evaluation of 23 frontier LLMs on the OpenCode agent harness shows agents reliably uncover alert-exposed problems but struggle with proactive disk investigation for silent intrusions and comprehensive remediation plans.

**Why it matters:** This benchmark reveals a fundamental bottleneck in current LLM agents for real-world incident response, highlighting their inability to achieve complete detection and remediation.

<small>Lehan Wang, Boli Chen, Ruixue Ding, Pengjun Xie, Jinwei Huang, Zhendong Liu, Shuo Wang, Tao Lei</small>

### [Metis: Memory Foundation Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26760v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-07-29 · `arXiv:2607.26760v1`

Metis introduces memory foundation models, integrating native, dynamically evolving memory states and autonomous memory procedures directly into foundation models, moving beyond external memory modules.

- Metis formalizes native memory as a persistent, dynamically evolving memory state within the model backbone and native memory procedures for autonomous storage and utilization.
- The architecture equips a foundation model with a native memory state, compressing historical information and accessing it via memory attention.
- Large-scale memory-specific training data and multiple optimization objectives are used to acquire native memory procedures through mid-training.
- Online memory maintenance is gradient-free, and memory updates require only a forward pass; learned model weights remain frozen during inference while native memory states transform autonomously.

**Why it matters:** This approach offers advantages in architecture, end-to-end optimization, and efficiency by internalizing memory capabilities within foundation models.

### [Constitutional Midtraining: Content Presence Drives Alignment Gains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26654v2)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-07-29 · `arXiv:2607.26654v2`

Constitutional midtraining, using a 394M-token corpus, improves alignment generalization and durability, particularly against blackmail, without capability costs, even after fine-tuning.

- A 394M-token constitutional corpus was created from Anthropic's Constitution and applied during midtraining at 120B scale.
- Constitutional midtraining reduced blackmail propensity by 17.5 percentage points compared to control, with this advantage surviving benign fine-tuning.
- The presence of constitutional content during midtraining was more impactful than its structural organization (curriculum ordering or deliberative reasoning).
- No capability cost was observed across MMLU, ARC-Easy, piqa, and GSM8K benchmarks at any evaluation stage (post-midtraining, post-SFT, post-benign fine-tuning).
- Alignment gains from constitutional midtraining were durable for blackmail but attenuated after SFT for scenarios requiring active resistance to in-context pressure or conflict.

**Why it matters:** Constitutional midtraining offers a cheap, complementary method to SFT-centered pipelines for achieving broad, persistent alignment gains in large language models.

<small>Desiree Cho, Cameron Tice, Bernie Hogan, Hunar Batra, Puria Radmard, Jun Zhao, Nigel Shadbolt</small>

### [Rethinking Classifier-Free Guidance in On-Policy Diffusion Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24731)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-26 · 👍 74 · ⭐ 7 · `arXiv:2607.24731`

This paper identifies "Negative Branch Asymmetry" (NBA) in on-policy diffusion distillation (OPD) under classifier-free guidance (CFG) and proposes Positive-Direction Matching (PDM) to address it, improving knowledge transfer robustness.

- Existing OPD methods for CFG directly match teacher and student guided velocities, which is under-identified at the branch level.
- Naive matching is effective when negative conditioning is shared, leading to joint reduction of positive- and negative-branch errors.
- Negative Branch Asymmetry (NBA) occurs when the teacher's negative branch has privileged information, causing antagonistic branch-error dynamics where positive-branch error decreases but negative-branch error increases.
- Positive-Direction Matching (PDM) is introduced as a branch-aware OPD objective that separately constrains the positive prediction and the CFG conditional direction.
- PDM improves robustness and effectiveness in dense-to-sparse video control, where naive guided matching is sensitive to inference guidance scales.

**Why it matters:** The proposed PDM method enables more robust and effective knowledge transfer in diffusion model distillation, particularly for applications like dense-to-sparse video control.

### [Sol-Attn: Accelerating Video Generation Inference via On-the-Fly Attention Sparsification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24027)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-26 · 👍 34 · `arXiv:2607.24027`

Sol-Attn introduces a training-free sparse attention method for diffusion transformers, unifying dynamic routing, sparse computation, and approximation correction in a single online-softmax pass to accelerate video generation.

- Sol-Attn addresses limitations of existing dynamic sparse attention methods, which include rigid/unpredictable routing and lossy keep-or-drop sparsification.
- The core of Sol-Attn is on-the-fly block thresholding with proxy-score reuse, selecting critical blocks by comparing proxy scores against a threshold during online softmax.
- This method enables dynamic and controllable block budgets without materializing the proxy map, and reuses proxy scores of unselected blocks to approximate their contribution.
- Sol-Attn achieves 2.1x and 2.3x end-to-end speedups for video generation and editing, respectively, while maintaining visual quality.

**Why it matters:** This method significantly accelerates high-fidelity video generation and editing by reducing the computational bottleneck of attention in diffusion transformers.

### [Minimizing Targeted Activations: Input-Only Suppression of Evaluation-Awareness Latents in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25907v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-07-28 · `arXiv:2607.25907v1`

This paper introduces input-side suppression of evaluation-awareness latents in LLMs using optimized prompts, finding robust suppressibility but limited behavioral control, especially for CAA directions.

- The method adapts Fluent Dreaming / EPO with a negated feature term (GCG-style token optimization + self-cross-entropy fluency regularizer) to suppress latents without inference-time model access.
- Evaluation-awareness latents were robustly suppressible (z ≈ -7) across five target constructions (CAA direction, subspace norm, SAE feature, MLP neuron, behavioral logit) on Llama-3.2-3B and Llama-3.1-8B.

- A single MLP neuron was found to be eval-correlated but not causal, and natural text from the Pile provided a competitive baseline for internal direction suppression.

**Why it matters:** The findings indicate that activation-readability does not guarantee behavioral controllability, posing challenges for the validity of safety evaluations if models can detect and alter behavior when tested.

<small>Deepanshu Mody, Samarth Agarwal, Utkarsh Mittal, Dipesh Mahato</small>

### [RAGuard: A Layered Defense Framework for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems Against Data Poisoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26339v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-07-28 · `arXiv:2607.26339v1`

RAGuard is a layered defense framework for RAG systems against factual corpus-poisoning attacks, combining adversarial retriever fine-tuning and a black-box, label-free filter called ZKIP.

- RAGuard employs a two-layer defense: adversarial fine-tuning of a dense retriever to downrank malicious passages, and ZKIP, a label-free filter that scores documents based on semantic shift and output-entropy change upon removal.
- ZKIP operates by performing a leave-one-out decode for each retrieved document, requiring no poison labels, ground-truth answers, or internal model access.
- On Natural Questions with 5-30% poison ratios, ZKIP reduced attack success rate to 0.000 while maintaining Recall@5 within 0.03 of the clean baseline.
- The defense incurs a cost of k+1 generator passes per query (6x for k=5), with analyses for batching and early-stopping approximations to reduce overhead.
- Keyword-preserving poisons do not affect lexical retrievers like BM25, defining a boundary for the threat model addressed.

**Why it matters:** This framework significantly enhances the robustness of RAG systems against data poisoning, ensuring reliable information retrieval and generation in critical applications.

<small>Pushkal Kumar, Tucker Nielson, Tanish Kolhe, Shubham Zala, Vincent Li</small>

### [Between Gradient and Natural Gradient: A Continuum of LoRA Initializations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26247v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-07-28 · `arXiv:2607.26247v1`

ULoRA introduces a two-parameter family of preconditioned gradient initializations for LoRA, demonstrating that optimal preconditioning is task-dependent and can match or exceed full fine-tuning performance.

- ULoRA is a two-parameter family of preconditioned gradient initializations for LoRA, defined by a spectral whitening exponent and an Adam-like diagonal exponent.
- The optimal preconditioning strength for LoRA initialization is task-dependent and often lies strictly within the ULoRA family, not at its endpoints.
- A tuned ULoRA configuration matches or exceeds full fine-tuning on all five GLUE tasks with RoBERTa-base.
- ULoRA is competitive with strong baselines on GSM8K with LLaMA-2-7B.
- ULoRA-Auto, a search-free variant, selects per-layer exponents from measured spectral statistics and approaches the performance of tuned ULoRA.

**Why it matters:** Treating LoRA initialization and curvature preconditioning as a tunable dimension, rather than a fixed design, significantly improves fine-tuning performance.

<small>Dianze Liu, Farshid Ghezelbash</small>

### [Spend Experts Where You Are Unsure: Confidence-Adaptive Routing for Mixture-of-Experts LoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26052v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-07-28 · `arXiv:2607.26052v1`

CARE (Confidence-Adaptive Routing of Experts) dynamically adjusts the number of active experts in MoE-LoRA based on per-token uncertainty, improving efficiency and performance over fixed top-k routing.

- CARE activates experts in decreasing router weight until their cumulative mass reaches a threshold, with an extension for expert disagreement.
- A budget thermostat calibrates the activation threshold to match a target average number of active experts.
- CARE is a drop-in, single-forward-pass rule requiring no extra parameters.
- CARE improves performance over fixed top-k MoE-LoRA at matched compute and matches fixed-k=4 while using fewer experts on LLaMA-3.1-8B and Qwen2.5-7B across eight commonsense, math, code, and knowledge benchmarks.
- The confidence and disagreement signals from CARE also enhance out-of-distribution detection compared to MSP, entropy, and multi-pass proxies.

**Why it matters:** This method allows MoE-LoRA models to allocate computational resources more efficiently by adapting expert usage to token difficulty, leading to better performance or reduced compute.

<small>Tom Saliencro, Rohan Desai, Priya Nair, Maya Lindqvist, Daniel Whitmore</small>

### [Detecting CSAM Text-to-Image LoRAs From Weights](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25750v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-07-28 · `arXiv:2607.25750v1`

A method identifies harmful text-to-image LoRAs directly from their weights by analyzing the top-left singular vectors ($u_1$) of LoRA updates, providing an inference-free fingerprint.

- The top-left singular vectors ($u_1$) of a LoRA's updates form a compact, inference-free fingerprint of its strongest learned change.
- Using human-subject age as a proxy for CSAM, $u_1$ identifies what a LoRA was trained on and generalizes across base models.
- $u_1$ abstains on unrelated benign content, indicating specificity.
- The signal is robust to additive weight noise, rescaling, and precision reduction.

**Why it matters:** This method enables screening of harmful LoRAs directly from weights, avoiding reliance on metadata or generation of harmful outputs.

<small>David Demitri Africa, Cate Heine, Nadine Staes-Polet, Kimberly Mai</small>

### [Rethinking Classifier-Free Guidance in On-Policy Diffusion Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24731)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-26 · 👍 74 · ⭐ 7 · `arXiv:2607.24731`

This paper identifies "Negative Branch Asymmetry" (NBA) in on-policy diffusion distillation (OPD) under classifier-free guidance (CFG) and proposes Positive-Direction Matching (PDM) to address it, improving knowledge transfer robustness.

- Existing OPD methods for CFG directly match teacher and student guided velocities, which is under-identified at the branch level.
- Naive matching is effective when negative conditioning is shared, leading to joint reduction of positive- and negative-branch errors.
- Negative Branch Asymmetry (NBA) occurs when the teacher's negative branch has privileged information, causing antagonistic branch-error dynamics where positive-branch error decreases but negative-branch error increases.
- Positive-Direction Matching (PDM) is introduced as a branch-aware OPD objective that separately constrains the positive prediction and the CFG conditional direction.
- PDM improves robustness and effectiveness in dense-to-sparse video control, where naive guided matching is sensitive to inference guidance scales.

**Why it matters:** The proposed PDM method enables more robust and effective knowledge transfer in diffusion model distillation, particularly for applications like dense-to-sparse video control.

<small>Bingnan Li, Haozhe Wang, Haozhong Xiong, Fangtai Wu, Jinpeng Yu, Yang Shi, Jiaming Liu, Ruihua Huang</small>

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-26 · 👍 34 · `arXiv:2607.24027`

Sol-Attn introduces a training-free sparse attention method for diffusion transformers, unifying dynamic routing, sparse computation, and approximation correction in a single online-softmax pass to accelerate video generation.

- Sol-Attn addresses limitations of existing dynamic sparse attention methods, which include rigid/unpredictable routing and lossy keep-or-drop sparsification.
- The core of Sol-Attn is on-the-fly block thresholding with proxy-score reuse, selecting critical blocks by comparing proxy scores against a threshold during online softmax.
- This method enables dynamic and controllable block budgets without materializing the proxy map, and reuses proxy scores of unselected blocks to approximate their contribution.
- Sol-Attn achieves 2.1x and 2.3x end-to-end speedups for video generation and editing, respectively, while maintaining visual quality.

**Why it matters:** This method significantly accelerates high-fidelity video generation and editing by reducing the computational bottleneck of attention in diffusion transformers.

### [Characterizing Warp Divergence from Pascal to Blackwell](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23402)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-25 · 👍 9 · `arXiv:2607.23402`

Microbenchmarks across NVIDIA GPU architectures from Pascal to Blackwell show that warp divergence serialization remains strictly linear (T(k) ~ sk) despite major ISA and reconvergence mechanism evolutions across hardware generations.

- Divergent paths serialize linearly with k paths, dropping warp execution efficiency to 32/k independently of occupancy, while predication eliminates serialization costs across all tested generations.
- SASS reconvergence mechanisms evolved from hardware SSY/SYNC stacks in Pascal to barrier registers in post-Volta architectures, with deferred reconvergence cases dropping from 29 on Ampere to 2 on Blackwell.
- Blackwell introduces uniform-branch instructions, explicit partial-mask warp synchronization, and a two-tier convergence-barrier classification that controlled bit-flip tests show is purely static with no observable runtime effect.

**Why it matters:** Developers can rely on a consistent, linear performance cost model for warp divergence across NVIDIA GPU generations despite underlying hardware and control-flow ISA redesigns.

### [Weak-to-Strong On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26246v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-07-28 · `arXiv:2607.26246v1`

Weak-to-Strong On-Policy Distillation (W2S-OPD) improves a strong student model by constructing a proxy teacher in logit space using contrast pairs of weaker models and minimizing reverse KL divergence on student rollouts.

- W2S-OPD constructs a proxy teacher by adding the logit difference of a contrast pair (positive minus negative model) to the student's base model logits.
- Three contrast pairs isolate specific capabilities: post-RL expert vs. pre-RL init (RL skills), larger vs. smaller base model (scale capabilities), and small base model with correct vs. wrong hints (instance-level solution direction).
- The student model distills the resulting proxy teacher on its own rollouts via per-token reverse KL minimization.
- Scale contrasts emphasize solving procedures, whereas post-RL and hint contrasts emphasize reasoning frameworks.

**Why it matters:** This framework allows frontier student models to continuously improve through distillation even when all available supervision sources and teacher models are smaller or weaker.

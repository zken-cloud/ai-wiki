### [Agent Mosaic: Recognizing Confidentiality Leaks in Agentic LLM Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30418)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-20 · 👍 16 · `arXiv:2610.30418`

A study quantifies 'mosaic effect' confidentiality leaks in LLM agents, where models infer and disclose forbidden composite conclusions from accessible documents even under benign queries and explicit aggregation safeguards.

- Evaluated 30 synthetic cybersecurity, medical, and military scenarios generating 10,800 simulated answers across three LLM agents and 1,440 answers across two live open-source harnesses.
- The top model, GPT-5.6-Sol, disclosed forbidden composite information in 7.08% of benign query answers during controlled simulations, with higher disclosure rates in live environments.
- Applying classification headers and explicit aggregation rules reduced pooled disclosures, but unsafe disclosures still persisted in 32.37% of simulated answers across all intents.
- Increased model refusals failed to protect confidentiality, as significant refusals often revealed the prohibited conclusions directly.

**Why it matters:** LLM agents operating in mixed-clearance environments present a structural data leak risk by inferring sensitive conclusions from unclassified documents, which standard classification headers and refusals fail to fully mitigate.

<small>Daniel Ruiz, Luca Luceri, Emilio Ferrara</small>

### [Inherited, Not Invented: Tracing the Provenance of Wait in Reasoning Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30882)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-22 · 👍 7 · `arXiv:2610.30882`

A study reveals that reasoning models inherit the "{wait}" pause marker from training data rather than inventing it via reinforcement learning, showing large RL accuracy gains can occur entirely without the word.

- RL raises MATH-500 accuracy without coining the "{wait}" marker on base models that lack it.
- The marker enters models through mid-training data (e.g., OLMo-3) or supervised fine-tuning on reasoning traces.
- Released RL-Zero recipes (OLMo-3, MiMo) multiply the marker's presence, whereas KL-free GRPO does not.
- GRPO applied after supervised fine-tuning can erase the marker entirely, and RL-Zero recipes can degrade its structural form.
- Automated LLM-as-a-judge revision metrics can reverse human-labeled trends when assessing reasoning markers.

**Why it matters:** Marker frequency is an unreliable indicator of RL-driven reasoning, highlighting that model behaviors attributed to RL discovery may simply be artifacts inherited from supervised pre-training or fine-tuning datasets.

### [SQuAT: Spherical Lattice Vector Quantization-Aware Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31412v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-10-22 · `arXiv:2610.31412v1`

SQuAT scales lattice VQ-QAT to multi-billion-parameter LLMs by using separate straight-through estimators for decomposed weight directions and magnitudes alongside a distributed exact nearest-neighbor assignment scheme.

- Weight vectors are decomposed into gains (quantized via low-bit scalar quantizers) and normalized directions (quantized via an E_8-derived spherical codebook).
- Applying separate STEs to shape and gain induces tangent-space updates for directions, acting as an implicit form of spherical (Riemannian) optimization.
- A distributed exact nearest-neighbor assignment scheme communicates only discrete indices across workers, reducing VQ-QAT training time by 12.8x and scaling to 10B tokens.
- Evaluated on LLaMA-3.2-1B/3B, Qwen3-4B, and base Qwen3-1.7B, SQuAT achieved state-of-the-art VQ-QAT accuracy.

**Why it matters:** It demonstrates that lattice-based vector quantization can be efficiently trained at scale via quantization-aware training, enabling state-of-the-art accuracy for highly compressed low-bit LLMs.

### [Delta N-gram: Forward-Only Continual Writing into a Language Model's Native N-gram Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31125v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-10-22 · `arXiv:2610.31125v1`

Delta N-gram introduces a forward-only continual write path into a language model's frozen native hashed N-gram table using a 4.1M-parameter writer to emit gated target updates into a sparse delta patch.

- The method keeps the pretrained table T^0 intact and writes updates to a sparse patch Δ via a block-averaged delta rule, allowing instant restoration of the base model by clearing Δ.

A study reveals pairwise evaluation masks prompt-level reward model errors, showing models over-cluster scores by prompt (shares of 0.34 to 0.64 on HelpSteer2) compared to near-zero human rating variation.

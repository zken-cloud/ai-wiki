### [Does Scaling Toward AGI Depend on What System-1 Lets System-2 Execute?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39497)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-28 · 👍 56 · `arXiv:2610.39497`

Researchers propose a predict-gate-execute-update loop where a lightweight System-1 gates execution to optimize System-2 compute scaling.

- Introduces conditional interaction: System-1 predicts candidate utility, an adaptive threshold gates admission, System-2 executes admitted candidates, and results update System-1.
- Under ideal screening and equal execution costs, System-2 execution compute decreases by up to a factor of 1/ρ_u, where ρ_u is the utility-relevant interaction density.
- Establishes a theoretical bound on selection regret determined by predictor error and the utility margin.
- Proposes that density of utility-relevant interactions (ρ_u) is a measurable scaling metric and that informative compute better describes scaling efficiency than total compute.

**Why it matters:** Filtering low-utility candidates prior to execution reduces the massive compute costs required for scaling System-2 reasoning and agentic tool use.

<small>Qi Wang, Yun Qu, Lizhou Cai, Heming Zou, Haoang Chi, Yiqin Lv, Xiang Li, Haiyan Feng</small>

### [On the Origin and Evolution of Training-Inference Mismatch in LLM Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40945)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-28 · 👍 7 · ⭐ 2 · `arXiv:2610.40945`

Researchers formalize LLM RL training-inference mismatch into numerical discrepancy and policy lag, introducing Discrepancy-Lag Correction (DLC) to stabilize training by removing prefix bias and resynchronizing when effective sample size drops below 0.5.

- Evaluates training-inference mismatch by splitting token errors into engine numerical discrepancy (constant bias) and trainer policy lag (feedback loop bias).
- Removes leading bias from numerical discrepancy by centering scores at each prefix.
- Shows importance weighting fails on Qwen3-1.7B when the effective sample size of the lag ratio falls below 0.5.
- Introduces Discrepancy-Lag Correction (DLC), which centers numerical errors, clips the lag ratio against sampler weights, and resynchronizes the sampler to prevent training collapse.
- DLC keeps KL divergence from on-policy training within twice the KL of two independent on-policy runs on Qwen3-0.6B and Qwen3-1.7B.

**Why it matters:** DLC prevents training collapses in asynchronous RL for LLMs caused by off-policy weight staleness and engine numerical differences, which standard per-token corrections fail to catch.

<small>Yu Li, Yufa Zhou, Tian Lan</small>

### [There Is No Free Loop: When Does Looping a Trained Model Pay for Its Inference Compute?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42030v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-10-30 · `arXiv:2610.42030v1`

Adding layer loops post-pretraining only pays for its inference compute cost in small models (0.8B), failing to outperform unlooped baselines at 4B and 9B scales.

- Looping re-applies $m$ of $N$ layers, increasing per-token compute cost by approximately a factor of $(N+m)/N$ while keeping parameter count and memory footprint constant.
- The study evaluated post-pretraining looping across Qwen3 and Qwen3.5 models ranging from 0.8B to 9B parameters against equal-parameter and equal-FLOP unlooped baselines.
- Only the 0.8B looped model outperformed both equal-parameter and equal-FLOP baselines; 4B and 9B looped models failed to beat even the equal-parameter unlooped baseline.
- The lack of gain at 4B and 9B is not due to benchmark saturation, as unlooped 9B models scored up to 13 points higher than unlooped 4B models on the same tasks.

**Why it matters:** Inserting architectural loops after pretraining is inefficient for scaling performance in larger models compared to simple unlooped baselines under equal compute constraints.

<small>Rhea Sanjay Sukthanker, Pashmina Cameron</small>

TSBPO is a tree-structured sampling-to-update paradigm for interactive language agents that optimizes continuation allocation, multi-level advantage estimation, and multi-token loss aggregation in a single loop.

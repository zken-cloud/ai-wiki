### [The Tasteful Agent: Measuring and Improving Taste in Long-Horizon Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25804)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-21 · 👍 51 · ⭐ 1 · `arXiv:2609.25804`

Researchers introduced Taste-Bench to evaluate LLM agents' decision-making at trajectory forks in long-horizon tasks and demonstrated that distilling downstream trajectory outcomes into a student model improves its decision taste and end-to-end performance.

- Taste-Bench automatically mines trajectory forks from parallel task attempts and single-trajectory detours without requiring human annotation.
- Frontier LLMs perform poorly on Taste-Bench, with the top-performing model achieving only 59.7% accuracy.
- Decision forks with evidence occurring later in the trajectory are significantly harder for models, and increasing the reasoning budget fails to improve accuracy.
- Distilling judgment from a teacher model that has observed full trajectory outcomes into a student model improves decision accuracy on unseen tasks and boosts end-to-end success on SWE-bench Pro.

**Why it matters:** Evaluating and training agent 'taste' at critical decision forks enables LLM agents to avoid costly detours and achieve higher success rates on complex, long-horizon software engineering and research tasks.

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-20 · 👍 19 · `arXiv:2609.24657`

HyperQ introduces token-conditioned quantum residual branches to a frozen diffusion language model using circuit hypernetworks, enabling efficient training of 16-to-64-qubit circuits within a 1.1-billion-parameter backbone.

- Only the added quantum residual branches are trained, keeping the 1.1B parameter backbone frozen.
- Lightweight hypernetworks generate token-specific rotation angles, coupling strengths, and measurement axes within a shared sparse circuit structure.

- HyperQ achieved these results using 20,000 prompt-response fine-tuning pairs, compared to 200,000 required for classical baselines.

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-21 · 👍 16 · `arXiv:2609.26774`

StableVQ stabilizes vector-quantized tokenizer training by disentangling encoder-decoder and codebook objectives using dynamic Straight-Through Estimation, Region VQ Loss, and a decoupled learning rate schedule.

- Identifies encoder-decoder and codebook training entanglement as the root cause of training instability in VQ tokenizers.
- Dynamic STE corrects encoder learning objective instability to optimize reconstruction under discrete regularization even during low codebook utilization.

- Built on shared-projection codebooks, adding zero extra learnable parameters while improving ImageNet reconstruction quality and codebook utilization.

**Why it matters:** It improves the training stability and codebook utilization of visual tokenizers without adding parameter overhead, enabling more reliable discrete representations for image generation models.

- Steering an untreated base model using the activation displacement vector of a generation-ten student induced trait expression even without the system prompt present.

**Why it matters:** Subliminally transferred traits can silently persist across multi-generational model training chains, remaining latently active in internal representations while bypassing standard input-output evaluations.

### [Rewired or Gated? How Instruction Tuning Shapes Knowledge-Conflict Circuits in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25602v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-09-22 · `arXiv:2609.25602v1`

A mechanistic comparison reveals instruction tuning alters LLM knowledge-conflict resolution by reweighting existing late-layer attention heads rather than rewiring circuits, while shifting behavior toward parametric memory over terse context.

- Tested across Llama-3.2-3B, Qwen-2.5-3B, and Gemma-3-4B using node/edge attribution, superposition role analysis, causal ablation, and path patching.
- Found high node overlap (0.60-0.82) between base and instruct models, confirming the underlying circuit is gated rather than rebuilt.
- Instruction tuning causes models to reject terse counterfactual contexts in favor of parametric memory, though this skepticism disappears when false claims are framed within coherent, evidential passages.

**Why it matters:** Because instruction tuning preserves the core conflict circuit, interpretability and control tools developed for base models can transfer directly to instruct models.

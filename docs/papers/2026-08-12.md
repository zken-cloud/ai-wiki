### [On-Policy Self-Distillation without Any Supervision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06296)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-08-08 · 👍 163 · `arXiv:2608.06296`

Unsupervised On-Policy Self-Distillation (U-OPSD) enables LLM post-training using only internal generation consistency without ground-truth labels, environmental feedback, or larger teacher models.

- U-OPSD samples multiple rollouts and uses majority voting under a self-consistency threshold to establish a pseudo-solution.
- The model conditions its distribution on the pseudo-solution and distills itself on disagreeing completions to correct confident errors.
- In Qwen3 non-thinking mode, U-OPSD improved mathematical accuracy across five benchmarks (AIME24, AIME25, HMMT25, MATH500, AMC23) by 8.5% (4B) and 10.7% (8B), outperforming supervised OPSD.
- In thinking mode, U-OPSD performed on par with supervised OPSD (0.9% ahead at 4B, equal at 8B) and surpassed GRPO by 0.7% (4B) and 1.1% (8B).

**Why it matters:** It demonstrates that language models can effectively self-improve on complex reasoning tasks without relying on external annotations, reward signals, or larger supervisor models.

<small>Yijiang Li, Bingyang Wang, Yijun Liang, Yunjie Tian, Di Fu, Nuno Vasconcelos</small>

AdvFD introduces Adversarial Fréchet Distance, combining static feature targets with an adversarially learned representation and real-feature whitening to improve visual generator post-training without metric hacking.

- Static pretrained feature spaces in standard Fréchet Distance (FD) loss lead to Fréchet hacking, degrading alignment in alternative feature spaces.

### [Attention-Path Fragility as an Uncertainty Signal in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11138v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-08-11 · `arXiv:2608.11138v1`

Attention-Subnetwork Mutual Information (ASMI) is a training-free uncertainty estimator that measures prediction fragility by masking attention heads and evaluating mutual information across the resulting subnetworks using a semantic-agreement kernel.

- ASMI identifies confident-but-fragile predictions on grounded QA, adding error-predictive information beyond single-pass confidence and entropy to roughly halve retained errors.
- Sem-ASMI evaluates a single greedy response without stochastic generations, tying or beating Semantic Entropy on 10 of 12 grounded benchmark-backbone settings.
- The adaptive ASMI variant ties or leads the strongest baseline in 8 of 12 settings, showing statistically significant leads in 3 settings.
- ASMI's predictive utility is regime-graded: it provides strong uncertainty signals when answers route through provided context, but reverts to the Maximum Softmax Probability (MSP) baseline for parametric knowledge recall.

**Why it matters:** ASMI enables highly accurate uncertainty estimation for grounded QA tasks from a single inference pass, significantly reducing computational overhead compared to sampling-based baselines like Semantic Entropy.

### [ThinkRetrieve: Retrieval-Augmented Reasoning Traces for Test-Time Scaling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10928v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-08-11 · `arXiv:2608.10928v1`

ThinkRetrieve is a test-time scaling framework that dynamically injects retrieved solved problem exemplars directly into an LRM's intermediate reasoning trace at each step to prevent error compounding and drift.

- Mitigates diminishing returns, uncertainty, and error drift in long chain-of-thought traces generated by Large Reasoning Models (LRMs).
- Retrieves step-by-step solved exemplars from an external corpus and injects them into the thinking trace during intermediate inference steps.
- Evaluated across five LRMs (1.5B to 8B parameters) on GSM-8K, MATH-500, AIME 2025, and SciQ datasets.
- Achieves relative accuracy improvements over standard test-time scaling by up to 60% on AIME 2025.

**Why it matters:** It offers a method to scale test-time inference compute more effectively by providing models with procedural reasoning guidance rather than standard factual retrieval.

### [When Confidence Signals Disagree: Local and Global Confidence in Autoregressive Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16933v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-09-15 · `arXiv:2609.16933v1`

Comparing local confidence (greedy token probability) and global confidence (modal-answer frequency) in autoregressive language models reveals they are weakly correlated, not empirically interchangeable, and that their disagreement signals sampling instability.

- Local confidence shows little association with correctness on MMLU and ARC Challenge, whereas global confidence is moderately associated with correctness.
- On ARC Challenge, larger local-global confidence gaps correlate with higher answer entropy, more distinct sampled answers, and lower modal-answer concentration.
- The gap-entropy association persists when calculated from disjoint stochastic samples, ruling out shared finite-sample variation as the cause.
- Sampling instability is dataset-dependent, observed in ARC but present in only 4% of questions on MMLU.

**Why it matters:** Confidence cannot be treated as a single scalar property, meaning system oversight and downstream evaluation algorithms must explicitly define which confidence measurement they rely on.

<small>Julio C. Amador Diaz Lopez</small>

### [LimiX-2: A Contextual Mechanism Network Towards General Structured-Data Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17488v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-09-15 · `arXiv:2609.17488v1`

LimiX-2 is a scaled tabular foundation model using Contextual Mechanism Networks and Context-Conditional Masked Modeling to capture context-dependent joint data distributions rather than target-centric predictions.

- Adopts the Contextual Mechanism Networks (CMN) paradigm to learn the context-dependent joint distribution p(x, y \| D_context) instead of standard p(y \| x, D_context).
- Pretrained via Context-Conditional Masked Modeling (CCMM) on synthetic datasets derived from structural causal models (SCMs) with varied graph structures, functional mechanisms, and observation processes.
- Outperforms current dataset-specific models and tabular foundation models across TabArena, TALENT, and BCCO benchmarks.
- Demonstrates causal awareness where its feature attention mechanism directly encodes causal relationships, facilitating causal skeleton recovery.

**Why it matters:** By shifting from target-centric prediction to joint structural modeling, LimiX-2 advances general tabular intelligence and provides built-in causal skeleton recovery alongside superior predictive accuracy.

### [JustFit: 200K-Token LLM Serving on a 24 GiB Laptop with Just-in-Time State Management](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17475v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-09-15 · `arXiv:2609.17475v1`

JustFit is an MLX-based inference runtime that uses just-in-time state management to enable 200K-token context serving on a 24 GiB laptop.

- Integrates three core mechanisms: KVExec for compressed KV execution, PhaseSwap for component residency, and StateTrans for state-preserving serving transitions.
- Fuses state reconstruction with coordinate just-in-time materialization and release independently of model-weight quantization.
- Increases single-request context capacity on a 24 GiB M4 Pro MacBook running Qwen3.8-27B MXFP4 from 30,720 tokens (mlx-vlm baseline) to 212,992 tokens (6.93x expansion).
- Achieves 19.11 tokens/s on a 32K-input/64-output probe and maintains a median peak process memory footprint of 16,374 MiB on repeated 32K+6K workloads.
- Successfully answers 29 of 30 AIME 2026 problems using the integrated local runtime.

**Why it matters:** It enables consumer-grade laptops to run high-context, extended-reasoning LLM workloads locally without exceeding tight memory constraints.

<small>Yuhua Chen</small>

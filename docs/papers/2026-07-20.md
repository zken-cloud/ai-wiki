**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-15 · 👍 56 · ⭐ 56 · `arXiv:2607.14530`

- xHC addresses insufficient write-back information and cubically scaling residual-mixing cost, which limit prior Hyper-Connections (HC) methods to N=4.
- xHC combines temporal feature augmentation for richer write-back with a sparse residual-stream architecture that updates k=4 of N=16 streams while retaining dense access.
- xHC improves average downstream score by 4.0 points over mHC on an 18B MoE model with modest additional training FLOPs.

- xHC-Flash reduces per-sublayer memory traffic from 73.5C to 40C, comparable to mHC at N=4 (34C), making large-N expansion practical.

**Why it matters:** xHC enables effective and practical large-N residual-stream expansion for LLM pre-training, offering significant performance gains and compute efficiency.

### [On-Policy Delta Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15161)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-15 · 👍 38 · ⭐ 31 · `arXiv:2607.15161`

This paper introduces On-Policy Delta Distillation (OPD^2), a new method for on-policy distillation that uses a "delta signal" to transfer reasoning capabilities more effectively than conventional methods.

- On-policy distillation is a post-training method in reinforcement learning providing token-level supervision from a teacher model.
- The delta signal is defined as the difference between the teacher model and its base model prior to instruction tuning for reasoning capability.
- OPD^2 utilizes this delta signal as a distillation reward, providing a more direct signal for transferring reasoning capabilities.
- Experiments show OPD^2 consistently outperforms conventional on-policy distillation across mathematics, science, and code-reasoning benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** OPD^2 enables reasoning LLMs to achieve strong performance with a short post-training period, improving efficiency in model development.

### [Beyond Entropy: Correctness-Aware Advantage Shaping via Contrastive Policy Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14614)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-15 · 👍 13 · `arXiv:2607.14614`

Contrastive Policy Optimization (CPO) replaces traditional entropy with token-level contrastive disagreement between reference-guided and vanilla generation distributions to enable correctness-aware advantage shaping in reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards.

- Entropy-based advantage shaping fails to differentiate useful uncertainty from detrimental confusion, whereas CPO's contrastive disagreement reliably indicates token-level correctness.
- On-policy Distillation is demonstrated to be a special case of CPO where an external teacher model instantiates the posterior distribution.
- CPO addresses and resolves the zero-advantage problem inherent in standard methods.
- Optimal performance is achieved by balancing exploration supported by correct responses and exploitation supported by incorrect responses.

**Why it matters:** CPO improves the training efficiency and generalization of RLVR methods by providing a precise, correctness-aware signal that overcomes zero-advantage and entropy limitations.

### [Structured Output Collapses Answer Diversity Across 44 Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18476v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-07-20 · `arXiv:2607.18476v1`

Prompting language models to return structured formats like JSON or XML significantly reduces output diversity, collapsing answer distributions toward dominant modal responses across 44 tested models without requiring decoder-level schema enforcement.

- Prompting for JSON in the 'Pick a word' task increases modal answer frequency from 41% to 64%, reduces distinct answers from 52 to 36, and drops mean surprisal from 1.80 to 1.58 bits.

- JSON requests alter 53% of stable chat defaults, shifting them toward crowd consensus or instantiating new static defaults (e.g., Claude Fable answering 'cerulean' for color 100% of the time in JSON versus 0% in chat).

**Why it matters:** Software integrations relying on structured outputs interact with significantly more homogeneous model behaviors than standard chat-based evaluations and comparisons suggest.

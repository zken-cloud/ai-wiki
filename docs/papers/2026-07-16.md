ShortOPD is a novel short-to-long On-Policy Distillation schedule that significantly improves the recovery of pruned LLMs for free-form generation tasks by focusing training on non-repetitive, high-information tokens.

- Structured pruning often causes LLMs to collapse on free-form generation, with greedy pass@1 nearly vanishing, though pass@k recovers substantially.

### [Long-Context Fine-Tuning with Limited VRAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15105v2)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-07-16 · `arXiv:2607.15105v2`

Hierarchical Global Attention (HGA) combined with segment-wise backpropagation and tiered KV storage enables fine-tuning of large language models with long contexts on limited VRAM.

- HGA with segment-wise backpropagation and tiered KV storage allows training sequences up to 16,384 tokens on a 16 GB GPU, while dense attention fails at 4,096 tokens.
- Only the active segment remains differentiable in VRAM; older KV is detached to RAM or NVMe, and HGA loads a bounded set of exact historical tokens for each query block.
- On Qwen3-8B with 4-bit QLoRA, HGA achieves 16,384 tokens with 15.28 GB peak VRAM during training and 131,072 tokens during evaluation on a 16 GB Quadro RTX 5000.
- HGA-trained adapters achieve comparable perplexity (2.7405 nat) to dense-trained adapters (2.7383 nat) at 2K training length, outperforming the stock model (2.9541 nat).
- HGA training is marginally faster at 2K tokens/s (217.75 vs. 207.02 tokens/s for dense) and throughput ratio improves with context length due to constant attended historical set per token.

**Why it matters:** This method significantly extends the context length for fine-tuning large language models on commodity hardware, reducing VRAM requirements and improving training efficiency for long sequences.

### [VarRate: Training-Free Variable-Rate KV Cache Compression for Long-Context LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15498v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-07-16 · `arXiv:2607.15498v1`

VarRate is a training-free KV cache compression method for long-context LLMs that assigns variable low-rank budgets to tokens based on query salience, keeping all tokens at a non-zero rank.

- VarRate avoids token eviction, which causes 11-15 point accuracy collapse in token-selection methods under query-agnostic reuse.
- VarRate degrades by only 3.5-5.5 points where query-aware selection collapses, by keeping every token at a non-zero rank.

### [Relevant and Irrelevant: A Renormalization Group Analysis of Transformer Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15449v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-07-16 · `arXiv:2607.15449v1`

This paper applies Wilsonian renormalization group theory to Transformer attention, classifying it as a relevant or irrelevant operator based on data correlation length and deriving testable predictions for fixed-point geometry and perturbation decay.

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-07-16 · `arXiv:2607.14614v1`

Contrastive Policy Optimization (CPO) uses token-level contrastive disagreement between reference-guided and vanilla generation distributions for correctness-aware advantage shaping in reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR), outperforming entropy-based methods.

### [Answer-Conditioned Chains of Thought Degrade Verifiable-Reasoning Distillation in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14552v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-07-16 · `arXiv:2607.14552v1`

Answer-conditioned chain-of-thought generation degrades verifiable-reasoning distillation in LLMs, leading to lower accuracy, especially on difficult problems, due to backward rationalization.

- Training a strong instruction-tuned reasoning model on its own answer-conditioned chains sharply lowers its verifiable-reasoning accuracy.
- The accuracy loss grows with problem difficulty, reaching approximately 27 points on the hardest competition problems.
- Answer-conditioned chains exhibit backward rationalization, deriving from the shown answer rather than independently, with early final-answer statements as a measurable symptom.
- The degradation is a property of the data, not the generator, and transfers across teacher families, affecting eight thinking models from four families.
- The 'rationalize-toward' instruction, not just answer visibility, causes the degradation.

**Why it matters:** To maintain reasoning accuracy in LLM distillation, chains of thought should be generated answer-blind, as correctness filters cannot detect this data damage.

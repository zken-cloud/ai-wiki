### [Decoupled Predictive Decision Making: A Shared Conditional Predictor for Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02075)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-31 · 👍 113 · `arXiv:2611.02075`

Decoupled Predictive Decision Making (DPDM) improves agent decisions by combining a frozen language model semantic encoder with a shared, frozen conditional predictor (LimiX-2) that infers task targets from deployment evidence without parameter updates.

- DPDM uses a two-part architecture: language models (such as Qwen3.5-27B) generate semantic representations of evidence, while a single frozen LimiX-2 conditional predictor maps evidence and queries to choices using a unified interface.
- The system operates entirely via in-context empirical inference using deployment evidence, requiring zero neural parameter updates for either the semantic encoder or the conditional predictor.
- Evaluated across 50 benchmarks, DPDM with Qwen3.5-27B raised mean accuracy from 78.80% (few-shot prompting) to 84.92% at evaluated history budgets, and reached 82.33% when using identical histories and queries.
- On ToolPRMBench, DPDM achieved absolute accuracy gains of 34.26% on BFCL tasks and 28.87% on ToolSandbox tasks at respective history budgets.
- Across five reasoning-step assessment tasks and four encoders, DPDM increased mean balanced accuracy by an absolute 14.51% under identical imbalanced histories.

**Why it matters:** By decoupling semantic representation from empirical inference, DPDM allows LLM agents to continuously learn from deployment history and improve decision accuracy without costly model fine-tuning.

<small>Lang Mo, Xingxuan Zhang, Qi Wang, Mingchao Hao, Hao Yuan, Chunjing Gan, Wang Lu, Peng Cui</small>

### [VFlash: Fast, Accurate Visual Planning via Flash Primitive Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08449)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-11-04 · 👍 66 · `arXiv:2611.08449`

VFlash introduces Flash Primitive Decoding, a method using a lightweight drafter to predict structured visual primitives jointly in a single forward pass instead of token-by-token.

- Replaces autoregressive coordinate-by-coordinate decoding with a lightweight drafter that generates an entire visual primitive (boxes, points, trajectories) in one forward pass.
- Adapts its prediction layout dynamically based on the primitive's type, dimensionality, and variable length.
- Trains directly on annotated primitives and accepts valid structural predictions without requiring exact target-token agreement with the autoregressive model.
- Achieves ~25x faster generation for 3D point motion forecasting and improves spatial accuracy over standard autoregressive decoding.
- Boosts average success rates of a conditioned world action model from 62.6% to 70.2% across four MolmoSpaces task suites.

**Why it matters:** By drastically reducing generation latency while improving spatial accuracy, VFlash enables real-time visual goal and trajectory planning for physical AI systems like robotics and autonomous driving.

<small>Yu-Cheng Chou, Shihao Wang, Jin Huang, Chi-Pin Huang, Chiao-An Yang, Xianzhe Fan, Jing Wang, Zhiqi Li</small>

### [Harness-S: Learning from Sibling Harnesses for Generalizable Agent Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04277)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-11-02 · 👍 49 · ⭐ 20 · `arXiv:2611.04277`

Harness-S uses a fixed pool of specialized sibling harnesses to rescue failed policy attempts during training, distilling auxiliary execution capabilities into a single policy that executes under the original base harness.

- Directional Harness Evolution creates a fixed Sibling Harness Pool of specialized variants designed to repair different failure modes.
- Complementary Harness Rescue Training activates auxiliary harnesses only after initial attempts under the base harness fail, using the resulting successful trajectories for policy reinforcement learning.
- An Adaptive Harness Router selects the top-k harness variants using LLM voting and Harness Behavior Profiles, which are continuously re-anchored as the policy evolves.
- The method demonstrates performance gains across AppWorld, SWE-Gym, and DBBench when evaluated on the original base harness, with optional Rank-1 router reuse during inference.

**Why it matters:** It allows AI agents to internalize capabilities discovered through diverse, specialized execution environments without requiring complex or non-standard harnesses at deployment time.

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-11-03 · 👍 34 · ⭐ 3 · `arXiv:2611.06741`

TailSteer is a final-stage training framework that prevents multi-turn visual drift by concentrating preservation corrections at the tail of on-policy denoising trajectories.

- Targets long-term visual drift in multi-turn image editing where small unintended changes accumulate across repeated output reuses.

- Demonstrates substantial reduction in cumulative drift across 15-20 editing turns on FLUX, SenseNova, and Qwen model families without degrading single-turn editing or text-to-image benchmarks.

### [Learning Dynamics of On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08016v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-11-05 · `arXiv:2611.08016v1`

A mechanistic framework analyzes how on-policy distillation's feedback loop causes limited student responsiveness and generation degeneration, while showing offline distillation can successfully prepare models for effective on-policy training.

- On-policy distillation (OPD) dynamics are analyzed across two stages: converting distillation signals into parameter updates, and broadcasting updates across prefixes to alter rollout distributions.
- Student models can exhibit limited responsiveness despite high teacher-student disagreement due to the underlying update-conversion mechanism.
- OPD can cause generation degeneration when local updates aimed at increasing teacher agreement inadvertently raise the probability of unsuccessful continuations, leading to unreliable supervision.
- Offline distillation can act as a necessary pre-conditioning phase for OPD, yielding substantial downstream accuracy gains even if immediate held-out accuracy does not improve during the offline phase.

**Why it matters:** Understanding OPD's feedback mechanics helps practitioners avoid training degeneration and effectively sequence offline preparation to unlock downstream performance gains in post-training.

### [O$^2$PD: Overlap-Gated On-Policy Distillation on Reliable Prefix](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07731v1)

Overlap-gated On-Policy Distillation (O²PD) restricts distillation to the first 20% prefix tokens of student rollouts and uses an overlap-gated α-divergence to address top-k alignment failure modes.

- Applies token-level alignment exclusively to the 20% prefix tokens of student-generated rollouts to ensure teacher supervision remains reliable.

**Why it matters:** O²PD improves student model training efficiency and stability by filtering out unreliable off-policy teacher supervision and resolving support mismatches in top-k distillation.

### [RRC: Unlocking Generative Reward Models in LLM Reinforcement Learning via Ranking-Based Reward Construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06310v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-08-06 · `arXiv:2608.06310v1`

Ranking-based Reward Construction (RRC) bridges generative reward models and LLM reinforcement learning by deriving scalar rewards from relative preference rankings instead of absolute scoring paradigms.

- Identifies a fundamental mismatch between the comparative, ranking-based nature of generative reward models and the traditional scalar scoring paradigm in RL.
- Introduces self-competitive ranking, which generates reward signals by evaluating relative preference comparisons among sampled responses.
- Introduces anchor-guided ranking, which uses a small set of reference responses to enable scalable ranking-based reward construction.
- Demonstrates performance gains on open-ended chat and reasoning benchmarks compared to prior reward construction methods.

**Why it matters:** RRC enables generative reward models to be effectively utilized as reward functions in reinforcement learning, improving model alignment on chat and reasoning tasks.

### [Answer First, Reason Later: Commitment Order in Diffusion LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05687v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-08-06 · `arXiv:2608.05687v1`

Researchers demonstrate that unconstrained diffusion LLMs fail at reasoning by committing final answers prematurely before completing chain-of-thought tokens, a pathology resolved using frontier-gated commitment order.

- Unconstrained LLaDA-8B decoding on GSM8K commits final answers at 15-24% of the trajectory, causing up to 90% collapse to answer-only outputs as canvas size grows.
- Prompt-decoder analysis shows chain-of-thought benefits reasoning only under ordered token commitment, yielding a +34.8 percentage point interaction effect.
- Premature commitment stems from reachability rather than EOS pressure, as samplers act on termination beliefs at distant unmasked positions.
- Frontier-gated commitment causally recovers performance from 0.528 to 0.852 on GSM8K while maintaining up to 4x parallel decoding throughput.

**Why it matters:** Enforcing sequential token commitment order is required to prevent reasoning collapse in diffusion LLMs without sacrificing their parallel decoding capabilities.

<small>Jewon Yeom, Jaewon Sok, Seonghyeon Park, Jeongjae Park, Hwiyeong Lee, Taesup Kim</small>

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-08-06 · `arXiv:2608.06291v1`

BaKron accelerates neural network quantization using Kronecker-factored Hessians by combining anti-diagonal parallelism with recursive divide-and-conquer processing.

- Reduces total computational work for an m x n weight matrix from O(m^2 n^2) to O(mn(m+n)) using O(m+n) sequential steps.

- Extends two-sided adaptive-rounding formulations previously utilized in frameworks like BoA and YAQA.

**Why it matters:** It enables computationally efficient neural network quantization that leverages rich two-sided curvature information without exceeding the scaling limits of standard GPTQ approaches.

### [Visual Grounding in Zero-Shot Vision-Language Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06154v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-08-06 · `arXiv:2608.06154v1`

- A 32,874-call ablation battery across 15 models shows direct VLM controllers often rely on conservative priors, fail lane-axis reflection (LEFT/RIGHT transformations), or prove image-invariant.
- No tested local VLM met joint longitudinal and lateral grounding criteria, whereas an image-only deterministic control achieved 0.090 m MAE in lead-gap estimation with exact mirror equivariance.

**Why it matters:** Current vision-language models are unsuitable as monolithic zero-shot end-to-end controllers, but can safely serve as bounded, selective hazard assistants within modular control architectures.

### [When Self-Evolution Backfires: Pre-Commit Gating against Skill Contamination in LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05810v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-08-06 · `arXiv:2608.05810v1`

Researchers identify an irreversible skill-contamination phase transition in self-evolving LLM agents and introduce Verifier-as-Gatekeeper (VaG), a pre-commit gating framework that prevents defective skills from entering the decision context.

- Unconditional skill accumulation leads to cross-round contamination chains where descendant skills inherit flawed reasoning, making post-hoc skill removal structurally ineffective.
- Verifier-as-Gatekeeper (VaG) uses a progressive trust hierarchy of three heterogeneous critics (structural validity, behavioral harmlessness, semantic consistency) plus marginal-gain subset selection.
- On Terminal-Bench 2, VaG achieves 72% pass@1 while maintaining a skill pool roughly 5x smaller than unconditional accumulation.

- Ablations demonstrate that the three critics intercept largely disjoint classes of harmful skills, proving them complementary and mutually non-substitutable.

**Why it matters:** Preventing defective skills prior to integration is essential for LLM agents, as post-hoc skill deletion fails to reverse reasoning flaws inherited by downstream distilled skills.

### [Reasoning Errors Have a Region and a Direction in the Residual-Stream Trajectory of LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05660v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-08-06 · `arXiv:2608.05660v1`

A novel three-stream detector evaluates LLM reasoning accuracy by combining residual-stream motion (displacements) with state context (coarse region via vector quantization and fine direction via normalized multi-layer states) rather than relying on static states or decontextualized trajectories alone.

- Trajectory-only (displacement) methods omit originating state context, while full-state probing reintroduces shortcut-prone token information.
- The proposed three-stream architecture decouples representation into motion, a coarse region reader (vector quantization), and a fine direction reader (normalized multi-layer states).
- Outperforms displacement-only state-of-the-art by up to 12% and single-layer probing baselines by 21% on unseen reasoning benchmarks.
- Transfers zero-shot to factual completion and fact verification tasks, demonstrating the framework detects general model correctness rather than benchmark-specific reasoning styles.
- Ablation studies confirm motion, region, and direction components provide complementary signals for error detection.

**Why it matters:** It enables more reliable identification of LLM reasoning errors and factual hallucinations across diverse tasks without requiring full-state probing or task-specific retraining.

### [Feyospace-v1: How the Cyber Mercury Seven Trained Frontier Cyber Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08418)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-07 · 👍 73 · `arXiv:2609.08418`

A seven-person team developed Feyospace-v1, a data-centric framework using five specialized systems to train open-weight cyber agent models that achieve state-of-the-art performance for their parameter scales.

- Utilizes five complementary systems: Choulea (reasoning analysis), SkyReal (teacher-cost reduction), Hongzwang (API restriction bypass), PSBreakup (merging restoration), and Kreator (intervention-to-reasoning conversion).
- Constructed an audited dataset of 164,269 execution-verified trajectories from resettable environments including CTF, kernel-history, firmware, and device-backed setups.
- Improved starting models by an average of 23.76% on CyberGym and 10.49% across pooled CTF suites.
- Feyospace-s1 achieved a 63.24% verified success rate, ranking 10th overall on the CyberGym leaderboard and 1st among models at comparable parameter scales.

**Why it matters:** It demonstrates that small, independent teams can produce top-tier agentic cyber capabilities through data-centric post-training without requiring massive organizational scale.

<small>Zongjie Li, Alan Z. W, John Nicolas J, Walter H. F, Scott Donald L, Gordon Y. P, Deke X Jr</small>

### [Breaking the Vision-Action Shortcut: Latent Interface Training for Generalizable Robotics Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12641)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-10 · 👍 51 · ⭐ 24 · `arXiv:2609.12641`

Latent Interface Training (LIT) mitigates vision-action shortcuts in robot foundation models using a two-stage strategy that uses a pose-supervised latent interface to constrain visual conditioning to task-relevant spatial information.

- Stage 1 trains an action expert to predict action chunks conditioned on language, robot state, and the terminal SE(3) end-effector pose, avoiding visual reliance.
- Stage 2 replaces direct image conditioning with a latent interface that aggregates visual and semantic inputs and is supervised to reconstruct the Stage 1 terminal pose.
- Evaluated across four architectures (Pi0.5, MolmoAct2, FAST-WAM, and ImageWAM), LIT improved overall LIBERO-Plus success rates by 3.87 to 10.70 percentage points.
- Real-world testing demonstrated a 13.30 to 16.70 percentage-point success increase across three tasks with unseen lighting, distractors, and camera setups.

**Why it matters:** LIT prevents models from relying on task-irrelevant visual shortcuts, significantly improving robot policy generalization under visual and environmental distribution shifts.

<small>Jianman Lin, Shailesh Shailesh, Zhongyi Luo, Jiafei Duan</small>

### [SAS: Simple Attention Sparsification via End-to-End Optimization of Context Ranking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13141)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-10 · 👍 45 · ⭐ 3 · `arXiv:2609.13141`

Simple Attention Sparsification (SAS) optimizes context ranking end-to-end with language modeling loss by injecting continuous log-form selector scores directly into attention logits during training, bypassing hard Top-K gradient blocking.

- Injects continuous selector scores inside the attention softmax in log form during training to enable standard backpropagation.
- Uses normalized softmax gates to calibrate historical context against an always-retained current block, training the model on relative priorities.
- Implements a custom memory-efficient Triton kernel that integrates the SAS mechanism directly into FlashAttention-style computation.
- Outperforms layer-wise attention distillation baselines across reasoning, long-context, and agentic tasks, particularly under tight attention budgets.

**Why it matters:** It directly aligns attention budget allocation with language modeling performance rather than proxy layer-wise distillation, enabling higher accuracy under strict memory and compute constraints.

<small>Zhiwei Li, Lei Zhu, Hao Gu, Xiang Hu, Yan Wang, Haitao Mi, Sirui Han, Leo Liang</small>

<small>Code: [https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Simple-Attention-Sparsification](https://github.com/https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Simple-Attention-Sparsification)</small>

### [COBRA-Skills: Contextual Bandit-Guided Evolution for Agent Skill Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11682)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-09 · 👍 27 · ⭐ 5 · `arXiv:2609.11682`

COBRA-Skills optimizes LLM agent skill sets by framing optimization as contextual-bandit-guided prioritization paired with evidence-grounded skill evolution, reducing evaluation costs significantly.

- Formulates skill optimization as budgeted sequential optimization over a dynamically evolving candidate pool.
- Couples contextual bandit prioritization with execution-feedback-driven skill refinement to reduce evaluation overhead.
- Reduces optimization cost by 55% to 58% relative to SkillOpt while using only 50 unique optimization examples per benchmark.
- Demonstrates strong performance across six heterogeneous benchmarks, three target models, and different agent harnesses.

**Why it matters:** It enables cost-effective, data-efficient distillation and refinement of reusable LLM agent skills without requiring extensive task data or expensive brute-force execution evaluations.

### [StepAudio 3 Gen Technical Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12945)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-10 · 👍 26 · `arXiv:2609.12945`

StepAudio 3 Gen is a general-purpose discrete autoregressive audio generation model that unifies speech, vocals, sound effects, and music generation using a shared residual code space.

- StepAudio Tokenizer operates at 12.5 Hz in a 16x2048 residual code space, jointly quantizing semantic and acoustic features across all code layers.
- Dual-axis generation architecture models the first codebook autoregressively along the time axis, using a lightweight causal Transformer to predict the remaining 15 codebooks along the codebook axis.
- Applies a three-part training methodology featuring interference-aware progressive pretraining, multi-task instruction training, and supervised fine-tuning to protect text abilities while building audio generation capabilities.
- Uses an RVQ Adaptor to integrate multi-codebook acoustic representations directly into the discrete autoregressive backbone, replacing diffusion Transformer paradigms.

**Why it matters:** It demonstrates that a single discrete autoregressive architecture can achieve state-of-the-art TTS and voice design while natively generating diverse audio types like music and sound effects without relying on diffusion Transformers.

<small>Bin Lin, Bo Zhao, Boyang Wang, Boyang Zhang, Boyong Wu, Chao Yan, Chen Geng, Chen Wu</small>

### [SNAP3D: Physically Grounded 3D Parts for Assembly from a Single Image](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13146)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-10 · 👍 2 · ⭐ 1 · `arXiv:2609.13146`

SNAP3D introduces a physics-guided framework for single-image part-aware 3D generation that resolves part penetration, infers contact graphs, and optimizes parameterized connectors via simulation feedback for physical assembly stability.

- Resolves inter-part penetration and recovers a contact graph to map neighboring part interactions.
- Instantiates parameterized connectors at contact surfaces and optimizes their placement, orientation, and dimensions using physical simulation feedback.
- Introduces a physics-based evaluation protocol measuring assembly validity and stability under gravity alongside traditional geometric metrics.
- Validates physical realizability through successful 3D printing and real-world assembly of generated multi-part assets.

**Why it matters:** It enables the direct transformation of single images into physically stable, 3D-printable parts that can be assembled in the real world without collapsing or overlapping.

<small>Yu-Rou Tuan, Hao-Tang Tsui, Nicolas Ugrinovic, Kris Kitani, Xiaoxuan Ma</small>

### [Adaptive Compute in Latent World Models: When Depth Helps, Hurts, or Doesn't Matter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10203v3)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-07-11 · `arXiv:2607.10203v3`

Adaptive compute in latent world models shows depth can help, hurt, or not matter for prediction precision in autoregressive rollouts, depending on training and configuration.

- The shallow penalty (rho = err(shallowest-exit rollout)/err(full-depth rollout)) was used to assess depth's impact on prediction precision across nine DeepMind Control tasks.
- Three regimes were identified: depth helps (6/9 tasks, rho up to 8x), depth hurts (2/9 tasks, rho down to 0.87x), or depth barely matters.
- Depth inversion (where depth hurts) is training-induced; supervising early exits only at the first rollout step erases it (Delta=+0.28, n=8).
- The regime is predictable via a frozen dimensionality-only classifier, and the inversion reproduces under a transformer predictor but is configuration-dependent.
- Competent-policy data removes both inversion and intrinsic tradeoff, with loss unchanged, on two retrained tasks.

**Why it matters:** Understanding when and why adaptive compute benefits world models is crucial for efficient and effective planning in reinforcement learning.

<small>Achyuthan Sivasankar</small>

### [Structured Thoughts For Improved Reasoning And Context Pruning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10386v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-07-11 · `arXiv:2607.10386v1`

Structured Thoughts organizes LLM reasoning into alternating exploratory scratch work and distilled conclusions, improving reasoning performance by up to 8.08% and enabling 85% context pruning.

- Structured Thoughts framework organizes reasoning into alternating blocks for exploratory scratch work and blocks for distilled conclusions.
- A dataset of structured thoughts is created by segmenting reasoning traces and prompting an LLM to summarize steps into blocks.
- Fine-tuning foundation models on this re-formatted data yields up to 8.08% performance gains on reasoning benchmarks compared to standard SFT.
- Context pruning is enabled by discarding blocks after each / pair, retaining only conclusions.
- Proof-of-concept pruning achieves 85% memory/context savings with an 8.67% performance drop on mathematical tasks.

**Why it matters:** This method significantly reduces memory footprint and improves reasoning efficiency for LLMs by structuring thought processes and enabling context pruning.

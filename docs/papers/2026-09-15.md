### [How Lossless Is Lossless Speculative Decoding? The Role of Numerical Precision in Orthrus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15504)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-13 · 👍 15 · `arXiv:2609.15504`

An independent reproduction of the hybrid autoregressive-diffusion model Orthrus reveals that its speculative decoding exact-trajectory match drops to 43-45% under BF16 precision, achieving 100% exact matching only under FP32 precision.

- Orthrus achieves 100% exact trajectory matching under FP32 precision across evaluated prompts, confirming numerical precision dictates its exact losslessness.
- Under BF16 precision, exact trajectory matching falls to 45% on the authors' checkpoint and 43% on an independently trained model across 1,190 prompts from 12 domains.
- The probability of an exact trajectory match under BF16 correlates strongly with the response-conditional perplexity of the reference model.
- Trajectory divergence under lower precision does not cause systematic degradation on downstream lm-eval-harness benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** Evaluating losslessness in speculative decoding architectures requires separating strict output trajectory equivalence, which depends heavily on numerical precision, from downstream benchmark performance.

<small>Ilya Koziev, Leonid Sinev, Ivan Oseledets</small>

### [TimeLens2: Generalist Video Temporal Grounding with Multimodal LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17423)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-18 · 👍 166 · ⭐ 86 · `arXiv:2607.17423`

TimeLens2 is a generalist video temporal grounding model that predicts variable-cardinality sets of evidence intervals using a novel supervision and optimization strategy.

- TimeLens2 treats temporal evidence as an interval set for both supervision and optimization, addressing limitations of existing training strategies.
- TimeLens2-93K constructs multi-span supervision via caption-derived proposals, independent localization, cross-agent consensus, semantic verification, and boundary refinement.
- A temporal Wasserstein reward computes exact one-dimensional W1 between uniform distributions over merged interval supports, providing dense, matching-free feedback.

- The 2B, 4B, and 8B variants improve over their Qwen3-VL backbones by 14.2, 13.0, and 18.1 mIoU points, respectively.

**Why it matters:** This advancement enables more precise identification of specific events within videos, improving the utility of MLLMs for video analysis.

### [Group Entropy-Controlled Policy Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16850)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-17 · 👍 29 · `arXiv:2607.16850`

GEPO introduces group entropy-controlled asymmetric advantage shaping to address heterogeneous entropy regimes and biased advantage signals in RL for LLMs across diverse tasks.

- GEPO extends GRPO by using group entropy, estimated from grouped samples, for entropy-conditioned asymmetric advantage shaping.
- GEPO attenuates positive advantages in low-entropy groups to reduce over-exploitation and negative advantages in high-entropy groups to preserve exploration.
- Adaptive thresholds for advantage shaping are derived from historical entropy statistics.
- Experiments on two base models across thirteen benchmarks (mathematics, physics, science, code generation, instruction following) show GEPO outperforms GRPO and recent entropy-controlled methods.

**Why it matters:** GEPO improves reinforcement learning alignment of LLMs by balancing exploration-exploitation across heterogeneous tasks, leading to consistent cross-task performance gains.

### [DiffGI: Differentiable Geometry Images for High-Fidelity Thin-Shell 3D Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13365)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-14 · 👍 20 · `arXiv:2607.13365`

DiffGI introduces a differentiable geometry image framework using continuous 2D Truncated Signed Distance Functions for high-fidelity thin-shell 3D generation, overcoming limitations of implicit and discrete geometry image methods.

- DiffGI replaces discrete binary occupancy maps with a continuous 2D Truncated Signed Distance Function (TSDF) to encode boundary positions at subpixel precision, eliminating staircase artifacts.
- A differentiable Marching Squares algorithm, based on analytical linear interpolation, enables gradient propagation from 3D surface losses back to the 2D latent space.
- A DiffGI-VAE, augmented with a geometry-aware normal rendering loss, compresses complex 3D surfaces into a 32x32 latent space.
- A transformer-based latent diffusion model with a flow-matching objective is used for conditional 3D generation from the compact latent space.

**Why it matters:** This method enables superior reconstruction fidelity and boundary precision for thin-shell 3D objects like garments with significantly fewer computational resources.

<small>Eungjune Shim, Hansol Lee, Eunjung Ju</small>

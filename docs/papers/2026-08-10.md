### [SFT Conflicts, RL Coexists: A Theoretical and Empirical Analysis of Multi-Task Learning for LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03573)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-08-05 · 👍 20 · ⭐ 5 · `arXiv:2608.03573`

A theoretical and empirical analysis reveals that multi-task SFT suffers from gradient norm-limited task conflicts, whereas RL enables stable task coexistence through variance-limited, sparse, near-orthogonal parameter updates.

- Empirical parameter-level observations show RL induces sparse and approximately orthogonal updates across diverse tasks, unlike SFT which suffers from severe multi-stage training conflicts.
- Theoretical analysis demonstrates that gradient interference in SFT is norm-limited and scales with absolute gradient magnitude.
- Gradient interference in RL is variance-limited and bounded by the gradient variance from advantage normalization and on-policy optimization.
- The resulting small variance bound in RL produces near-orthogonal optimization directions across tasks, enabling a decoupled multi-task training paradigm called Parallel-RL.

**Why it matters:** Understanding RL's variance-limited gradient interference allows developers to decouple multi-task LLM training via Parallel-RL to improve training efficiency and flexibility without performance degradation.

<small>Kejian Zhu, Zhuoran Jin, Shangqing Tu, Hongbang Yuan, Yushi Bai, Kang Liu, Juanzi Li, Jun Zhao</small>

### [Diffusion LLMs as Targets and Adversaries: Mechanistic Safety Exploits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07430v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-08-07 · `arXiv:2608.07430v1`

Researchers exposed mechanistic safety vulnerabilities in Diffusion LLMs, showing that safety alignment is sparse, transferable from autoregressive base models, and exploitable via neuron pruning and a low-cost offline jailbreak method.

- DLLMs initialized from autoregressive models inherit the same safety neuron footprint, allowing self-pruning to increase Attack Success Rates (ASR) up to 73.8% on LLaDA and 86.6% on Dream.
- Cross-architecture transfer pruning using Qwen2.5 safety maps increases ASR to 73.2% on Dream and 86.3% on Fast-dLLM.
- SN-Guided Diffusion uses a weighted safety neuron loss to steer diffusion away from safety regions, achieving an AUROC of 1.0 for benign-vs-jailbreak prompt discrimination.

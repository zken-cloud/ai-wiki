### [Annotations as Rollouts: Efficient and Scalable Reinforcement Learning for Video MLLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20492)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-08-19 · 👍 64 · ⭐ 18 · `arXiv:2608.20492`

OraRL integrates ground-truth annotations as oracle rollouts during video MLLM post-training, using a decoupled advantage estimator and sign-balanced pruning to bypass slow chain-of-thought generation while improving performance.

- Directly integrating high-reward oracle rollouts causes 'advantage inversion' by raising group baselines and making positive policy advantages negative.
- OraRL uses a decoupled advantage estimator where policy rollouts define an oracle-free baseline, modulated by the oracle-policy gap via directional gain and a detached oracle advantage.
- Sign-balanced pruning retains only the oracle and strongest positive/negative rollouts, dropping step-time overhead to 2.2x SFT compared to 4.9x for GRPO with CoT.
- Without CoT, Video-ORA-9B reduces decoding latency from 4,780 ms to 130 ms while scaling effectively from 0.8B to 9B parameters across 100k prompts.
- Video-ORA-9B achieves state-of-the-art results: temporal mIoU of 66.0 (up from 62.5), tracking AO of 78.2 (up from 73.0), segmentation of 70.4 (up from 64.3), and 73.1 on VSI-Bench.

**Why it matters:** It enables highly efficient RL post-training for video MLLMs, dramatically cutting decoding latency while outperforming substantially larger commercial models like GPT-5 and Gemini-3-Pro on spatial and temporal intelligence benchmarks.

<small>Yunheng Li, Guohong Mu, Hao Li, Shengsheng Qian, Dingwen Zhang, Qibin Hou, Ming-Ming Cheng</small>

Researchers formalize prefix invariance and introduce a two-forward-pass gradient-free audit that localizes causal leaks across attention, state-space, and hybrid sequence architectures where attention-mask inspection fails.

- Prefix invariance requires that representations at position t remain strictly independent of future inputs at positions greater than t.
- The proposed lightweight audit requires only two forward passes with no training or gradient calculations to pinpoint where causality breaks.

**Why it matters:** Standard attention-mask checks are insufficient for ensuring causality, making empirical auditing essential for preventing future data leakage in complex or hybrid sequence models.

### [Beyond the Stability-Exploration Dilemma: Environmental Regularization for LLM Policy Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23311)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-08-23 · 👍 15 · ⭐ 7 · `arXiv:2608.23311`

Environment-Regularized Policy Optimization (ERPO) replaces action-side Policy-KL with input-side Query-KL to control query distribution shift without constraining LLM response exploration.

- Replaces standard Policy-KL regularization with a Query-KL (QKL) term that bounds query distribution shift from the pre-RL reference distribution.
- Applies a dataset-static, reference-derived per-query weight to bias policy updates toward queries typical of the reference distribution.
- Eliminates response score functions from the QKL term, allowing QKL gradients to flow strictly through query likelihood without direct gradient pressure on response distributions.
- Integrates into existing GRPO, PPO, and REINFORCE pipelines without requiring additional forward passes.
- Demonstrates higher accuracy and improved stability during long-horizon training and high-temperature decoding across six mathematical reasoning benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** ERPO resolves the stability-exploration trade-off in LLM reinforcement learning, preventing policy drift while maintaining full action-space exploration without computational overhead.

Parason introduces a framework to learn and execute both subtask and trial parallelism in LLM reasoning traces, accelerating inference on complex math benchmarks using Parallelism-Aware GRPO.

- Identifies two distinct parallel reasoning modes: Subtask Parallelism (task decomposition) and Trial Parallelism (simultaneous speculative attempts, verification, and hypothesis aggregation).
- Reveals that Trial Parallelism accounts for 65.5% of parallelizable reasoning steps in DeepSeek-V4 on the HLE benchmark, becoming more dominant on difficult problems.
- Converts sequential reasoning traces into structured parallel trajectories using a context-free grammar.
- Trains models with Parallelism-Aware Group Relative Policy Optimization (PA-GRPO) using a reward function that balances accuracy, latency, and parallelism ratios.
- Executes parallel structures at inference time via tool calls, achieving an average ~1.7x wall-clock speedup on AIME24 and AIME25 while preserving accuracy.

**Why it matters:** By unlocking trial parallelism alongside subtask parallelism, Parason significantly reduces inference latency for long test-time LLM reasoning traces without sacrificing problem-solving accuracy.

### [Effective Learning Rate Governs Loss Dynamics in Language Model Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24814v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-08-25 · `arXiv:2608.24814v1`

Language model pretraining loss dynamics collapse onto a single trajectory when runs match their effective learning rate (ELR), defined as the ratio of learning rate to parameter norm.

- Matching ELR produces identical loss trajectories across different learning rates and parameter norms, with mean collapse errors (a few x 10^-3) falling below seed-to-seed variance.
- ELR collapse precision is primarily governed by normalization design and the timescale of LR-norm variation.
- Weight decay and Hyperball influence loss dynamics predominantly through the ELR schedules they induce.
- Replacing standard learning rate with ELR allows a fitted functional scaling law (FSL) to transfer across norm-control methods and explains the delayed acceleration effect.

**Why it matters:** ELR serves as a unified coordinate that links learning rate scheduling, norm-control interventions, and scaling laws to simplify pretraining optimization.

### [WeMM-Embedding: WeChat Multi-Modal Embedding Technical Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24053v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-08-25 · `arXiv:2608.24053v1`

Tencent has released WeMM-Embedding, a family of 2B, 4B, and 9B universal multimodal embedding models supporting text, images, videos, visual documents, and interleaved inputs with flexible output dimensions.

- Model family consists of 2B, 4B, and 9B parameter variants trained via a large-scale alignment stage followed by a refined fine-grained supervision and cross-scale knowledge transfer stage.
- The 9B variant achieves a state-of-the-art score of 80.6 on MMEB-v2, while the 2B model outperforms previous 8B open-source baselines.
- Deployed at scale within WeChat applications, showing gains across a 26-task in-house benchmark and positive results across 14 online A/B tests.
- Model weights and code have been open-sourced on GitHub.

**Why it matters:** It offers open-source, production-proven multimodal embedding models that outperform larger existing baselines in retrieval, search, and recommendation tasks.

### [Curved Inference II: Sleeper Agent Geometry - Extending Interpretability Beyond Probes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24037v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-08-25 · `arXiv:2608.24037v1`

Curved Inference II introduces semantic surface area (A'), an unsupervised geometric metric in unnormalised residual space that detects naturalistic deceptive reasoning without reliance on backdoors, artificial triggers, or linear probes.

- Addresses limitations of linear probes, which rely on artificial backdoor insertion rather than naturally occurring deceptive alignment.
- Introduces semantic surface area (A') to quantify representational work by measuring both magnitude and directional change of meaning construction in residual space.
- Uses multi-turn context windows to simulate realistic deceptive reasoning, evaluating outputs via LLM consensus across five prompt strategies and two model families.
- Demonstrates that geometric measurement precision uncovers hidden semantic signatures, shifting statistical significance for certain strategies from p = 0.555 to p = 0.048.

**Why it matters:** It provides a scalable, unsupervised detection method for deceptive alignment based on the intrinsic geometric shape of inference, even when models suppress linear indicators.

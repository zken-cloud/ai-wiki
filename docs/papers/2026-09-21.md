### [Calibrating Teacher--Student Discrepancy for On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21619)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-17 · 👍 3 · `arXiv:2609.21619`

Calibrated On-Policy Distillation (Cal-OPD) filters out teacher self-deviations by calibrating the teacher-student discrepancy via positive and negative privileged interventions, retaining only the true capability gap for optimization.

- Standard on-policy distillation (OPD) incorrectly forces students to learn teacher-side likelihood shifts and self-deviations alongside the actual capability gap.
- Privileged OPD exacerbates teacher self-deviation due to larger likelihood shifts caused by privileged information.
- Cal-OPD isolates the teacher's self-deviation region using positive and negative privileged interventions, stripping it from the optimization target.
- Cal-OPD retains only 52-65% of the original teacher-student discrepancy signal while consistently outperforming standard OPD across mathematical reasoning benchmarks and model scales.

**Why it matters:** By removing noisy teacher self-deviations, Cal-OPD achieves higher mathematical reasoning performance while training on roughly half the signal volume of standard on-policy distillation.

### [On Repulsive and Attractive Teachers: Separating Correctness from Behavior in Self-Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21561v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-09-18 · `arXiv:2609.21561v1`

A study on self-distillation reveals that attractive and repulsive teachers induce opposing behavioral shifts, and proposes contrastive self-distillation to cancel these behavioral shifts and isolate correctness-relevant signals.

- Attractive self-distillation (moving toward a privileged teacher) suppresses exploratory reasoning and yields shorter, overly confident responses.
- Repulsive self-distillation (moving away from a privileged teacher) increases response length, causes unstable training, and can trigger unintended switches into latent thinking modes.
- Contrastive self-distillation combines attraction toward a correct-solution-conditioned teacher with repulsion from an incorrect-solution-conditioned teacher.
- Isolating contrastive self-distillation without GRPO causes the shared behavioral shifts of both teachers to cancel out, leaving a stable, token-level correctness signal.

**Why it matters:** It enables improved reasoning performance across non-thinking, instruct-only, and thinking LLMs while preventing training instability and unwanted length or behavioral drifts.

### [Abstention and Noise Filtering: Two Missing Primitives of Softmax Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22005v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-09-18 · `arXiv:2609.22005v1`

A study shows value-pathway gating in softmax attention provides two distinct missing primitives: abstention via learned per-head sink logits and noise filtering via value gates, with filtering benefits dominating as models scale to 350M parameters.

- Abstention (learned per-head sink logit) allows attention heads to output zero by bypassing the sum-to-one softmax constraint, while noise filtering (per-value gates) suppresses residual stream feature interference.
- Abstention accounts for almost all performance gains at 10M parameters but its benefit declines with scale, whereas noise filtering benefits increase with scale and drive most gains at 350M parameters.
- Models combining both abstention and noise filtering achieved the lowest validation loss across all tested scales (10M to 350M parameters).
- Controlled interference experiments confirm value gates remove superposed feature noise, though each tested gate variant exhibits a distinct blind spot.
- Adding both primitives requires negligible extra parameters and maintains compatibility with standard KV-caching.

**Why it matters:** Combining sink logits and value gates fixes fundamental limits of softmax attention to improve scaling performance without overhead or breaking KV-cache compatibility.

<small>Richard Zhe Wang</small>

### [Trading Depth for Time in Recurrent Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21605v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-09-18 · `arXiv:2609.21605v1`

Latent Recurrent Transformers (LRTs) trade physical depth for temporal steps by inserting latent thought tokens, achieving near-equivalent performance with 48% fewer parameters.

- Interleaves latent thought tokens between consecutive vocabulary tokens to pass hidden states through an L-layer backbone twice per token, executing 2L Transformer blocks total.
- Evaluated on 16- and 20-layer mixture-of-experts NanoChat backbones, comparing an L-layer model using one thought token against a 2L-layer model without thought tokens.
- One thought token brings the L-layer model within 0.006 and 0.004 bits per byte of the 2L-layer baseline, recovering 67% and 81% of the performance gains.
- Demonstrates that temporal recurrence can substitute for physical depth to improve parameter efficiency in recurrent Transformers.

**Why it matters:** Using temporal recurrence with latent tokens enables smaller, more parameter-efficient models to achieve language modeling performance comparable to significantly deeper backbones.

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-09-17 · `arXiv:2609.21096v1`

A single-pass method detects LLM hallucinations by using Forman-Ricci curvature to trace topological signatures of impaired context sharing in attention graphs.

- Analyzes Forman-Ricci curvature on attention graphs to identify structural information bottlenecks and over-squashing.
- Captures semi-local and global information-flow characteristics of attention heads to differentiate hallucinated responses.
- Identifies that hallucinated outputs correlate with self-attention over-reliance, diffused context retrieval, and information bottlenecks, particularly in the final transformer layer.
- Outperforms existing attention-based and multi-response baselines across two benchmarks without requiring multiple response generation passes.

**Why it matters:** This approach enables computationally efficient, single-pass hallucination detection across diverse LLM architectures without the overhead of generating multiple responses.

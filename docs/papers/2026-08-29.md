### [Luce: Relightable Gaussians for 3D Asset Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23943)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-08-24 · 👍 7 · `arXiv:2608.23943`

Luce unifies geometry and PBR materials into a voxelized multimodal Gaussian cloud using dedicated primitives, autoencoding them into a material-aware latent space for single-image relightable 3D asset generation via a rectified-flow transformer.

- Represents geometry and PBR modalities (albedo, metallic-roughness, surface normals) using dedicated Gaussian primitives within a voxelized multimodal Gaussian cloud.
- Uses a variational autoencoder to compress the multimodal representation into a unified material-aware latent space.
- Employs a rectified-flow transformer to generate the latent from a single image conditioned on multi-layer features from a pretrained image encoder.
- Decodes latents into relightable PBR Gaussians or an optional textured mesh containing a tangent-space normal map.
- Outperforms baselines on Toys4K by reducing FID by 28% and achieves a 0.8519 CLIP image-alignment score on an AI-generated image benchmark versus 0.8299 for the top baseline.

**Why it matters:** Luce enables the direct creation of relightable, high-fidelity 3D assets with accurate PBR materials and fine details like text from a single input image.

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-08-27 · `arXiv:2608.27260v1`

This paper presents a two-level framework that factorizes LLM agentic data and uses the Accuracy-Complexity-divErsity (ACE) lens to guide constrained data generation.

### [When Text Misleads: Inconsistent-Aware Reasoning for Audio-Grounded Dialogue](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27176v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-08-27 · `arXiv:2608.27176v1`

Researchers created ContraTalk, a benchmark measuring cross-modal transcript bias in spoken dialogue, and introduced an Audio Twin framework to expose localized acoustic evidence directly to LLM reasoning models.

- ContraTalk evaluates 501 questions across five discourse dimensions: interaction behavior, emotion state, dialogue act, social stance, and conversational intent.
- Text-only LLMs reach over 90% accuracy on consistent transcript-audio cases but drop to 33-48% on cross-modal conflict cases.
- Direct AudioLLMs select the transcript-biased incorrect option in roughly 30-40% of conflict cases.
- The Audio Twin framework converts speech into a text-readable representation of localized acoustic cues, which reduces trap selection and improves conflict-case accuracy.

**Why it matters:** The findings show that current audio-language models rely heavily on text shortcuts, demonstrating the need for explicit acoustic evidence extraction to achieve reliable speech-grounded reasoning.

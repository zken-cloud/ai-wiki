### [BirdDreams: Joint Perspective and BEV Video Generation for a Plausible, Reactive Driving World Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14815)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-11-09 · 👍 42 · `arXiv:2611.14815`

BirdDreams is an autoregressive driving world model that uses joint attention across dual video diffusion transformers to simultaneously generate perspective and schematic bird's-eye-view (BEV) video streams from a single camera image.

- Jointly predicts perspective video and a top-down schematic BEV video where roads and vehicles are simple shapes at a fixed metric scale.
- Uses joint attention between two dedicated video diffusion transformers (one per stream) to share motion and appearance information during generation.
- Implements ego-trajectory control by transforming the latest BEV frame along the target path as a soft geometric conditioning input.
- Operates at inference using only a single initial camera image, without requiring future vehicle trajectories, HD maps, or vehicle-state inputs.
- Outperforms perspective-only baselines and prior world models on PhysicalAI-AV and nuScenes in ego-trajectory compliance and surrounding-vehicle behavior.

**Why it matters:** By predicting surrounding scene layouts as part of the generated future, BirdDreams eliminates the need for privileged future trajectory inputs while improving geometric control and vehicle motion realism.

- SISS attack success scales with resolution reduction: achieving a 58.75% average success rate across four closed-source VLMs, which drops to 17.68% at 128x128, 6.85% at 224x224, and 5.60% at 336x336.

- Downstream web-agent tests showed increased instruction perception, though absolute behavioral compromise remained low at approximately 4%.
- A proposed consistency detector, ScaleGuard, lowered non-adaptive attack success from 61.4% to 24.3% and defense-aware success from 42.5% to 15.0% without false positives on clean controls.

**Why it matters:** Standard image preprocessing pipelines (like resizing and compression) can unintentionally act as an attack vector by exposing hidden prompt injections to vision-language models and web agents.

### [Unbundling Multimodal Vision Encoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17334v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-11-12 · `arXiv:2611.17334v1`

Controlled empirical experiments show that multimodal language models require dedicated vision capacity, but do not need pretrained visual priors or pre-fusion encoder placement.

- Disentangles vision encoding into three choices: visual prior from pretraining, dedicated vision capacity, and pre-fusion vs. in-fusion placement.
- Jointly training an encoder from scratch on common data achieves parity with using a pretrained vision encoder, eliminating the need for a separate vision pretraining stage.
- Encoder-free models underperform on visual question answering even when the backbone parameter count is scaled to match the encoder's parameters.
- In-fusion vision-only modules integrated into early backbone layers match pre-fusion encoder performance while reducing time to first token.

**Why it matters:** Architects can simplify multimodal model training pipelines and decrease inference latency by replacing separate pretrained vision encoders with jointly trained, early in-fusion vision modules.

### [Negative-Only Policy Optimization for One-Sided Verifiable Rewards](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17287v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-11-12 · `arXiv:2611.17287v1`

NOPO addresses one-sided verifiability in RLVR by treating verified positives as unlabeled and updating policies strictly through reference-relative suppression of verified negative samples.

- Formalizes One-Sided Verifiability (OSV), where cheap verifiers yield reliable negatives but introduce false positives that cause standard RLVR to reward shortcuts.
- Eliminates positive reward updates by treating verified positives as unlabeled and exclusively suppressing verified negative samples.
- Combines a reference-relative negative suppression loss with a group-level adapter that scales policy updates according to the ratio of non-falsified responses.
- Demonstrates improved pass@k across code, logic, and math benchmarks using unit-test, constraint-check, and LLM self-verifiers without access to oracle rewards.

**Why it matters:** It enables effective reinforcement learning optimization for complex reasoning tasks where evaluating errors is easy, but verifying absolute correctness is impossible or expensive.

### [Joint Null-Space Projection for Data-Free Model Merging](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17103v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-11-12 · `arXiv:2611.17103v1`

Joint Null-Space Projection (JNSP) is a closed-form, training-free model merging technique that projects each task's weight delta into the null-space of all other tasks' top right-singular input subspaces.

- JNSP addresses subspace interference by computing the top-r right-singular subspace of each 2D weight delta and removing components that fall into the union of other tasks' subspaces.
- The method requires no data, labels, gradients, or forward passes, delivering an order-independent closed-form projection directly from model checkpoints.
- On eight CLIP image-classification tasks, JNSP merged ViT-B/16 experts to 89.45% average accuracy, outperforming TIES-Merging (77.57%), Task Arithmetic (75.23%), and WUDI (88.47%).
- Performance improvements replicated across TALL backbones (+10.8 for ViT-B/16, +12.6 for ViT-B/32, +9.1 for ViT-L/14 over TIES), though JNSP underperformed plain task arithmetic on RoBERTa-large.
- Ablation controls showed that substituting a single shared subspace caused a 2.9-point loss, while a sequential projection variant collapsed accuracy to 69.6%.

**Why it matters:** JNSP enables highly effective multi-task vision model aggregation entirely offline, outperforming data-free baselines without requiring access to evaluation datasets or compute-heavy optimization passes.

<small>Tian Xia</small>

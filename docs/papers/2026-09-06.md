### [The Head Complexity of Boolean Functions in Single-Layer Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04046v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-09-03 · `arXiv:2609.04046v1`

A study on single-layer attention-only models establishes that $k$ heads are necessary and sufficient to compute $k$-bit parity, regardless of embedding dimension or numerical precision.

- Unconditional lower bounds show $k$ attention heads can compute $k$-bit parity but cannot compute $(k+1)$-bit parity, even with unbounded precision and embedding dimension.
- The parity lower bound relies on an alternating-sum obstruction where clearing softmax denominators leaves decision polynomial monomials missing at least one input bit.
- A compactness theorem proves any function computable by the model can be implemented with embedding dimension and precision bounded by head count, alphabet size, and sequence length.
- General $n$-bit binary functions require at most $2^n$ heads (one per monomial in multilinear expansion) and at least $\Omega(2^n/n^2)$ heads for almost all functions.
- The alternating-sum obstruction proof technique extends to establish lower bounds for multi-hop induction-head tasks.

**Why it matters:** It establishes fundamental computational limits of single-layer transformers, proving that increasing embedding dimension or numerical precision cannot compensate for a lack of attention heads.

<small>Rajmohan Rajaraman, Ravi Sundaram, Amanuel Tesfaye</small>

### [Two-Stage Reinforcement Learning for Sound and Adversarial Test Generation in Code LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03955v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-09-03 · `arXiv:2609.03955v1`

Test Cases Scaling (TCS) uses a two-stage reinforcement learning framework to auto-generate sound and adversarial test cases based on a code solver's evolving failure modes.

- TCS trains a test generator from a rolling policy-aligned buffer using a two-stage reinforcement learning approach.
- Stage 1 generates test cases that are consistent with the reference solution to ensure soundness.
- Stage 2 restricts the buffer to current failure modes to learn discriminative counterexample tests.
- The framework improves pass@1 performance and inference-time answer selection on the TACO and LiveCodeBench benchmarks.
- The resulting test generator can effectively select correct solutions among outputs produced by other LLMs.

**Why it matters:** TCS automates the creation of high-quality, discriminative test cases, improving code LLM evaluation, output selection, and overall model accuracy without relying on scarce human-written tests.

### [Doesn't Stop Reasoning: Analysis of Spurious CoT Termination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03633v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-09-03 · `arXiv:2609.03633v1`

Injecting an end-of-think token to early-exit reasoning in LRMs causes spurious CoT termination, where reasoning continues into the answering phase unless attention to the injected token is artificially boosted.

- Training-free early-exit methods use an injected end-of-think (EoT) token to force LRMs from the reasoning phase into the answering phase.
- Injecting an EoT token often fails to cause a clean transition; models exhibit spurious CoT termination where reasoning behavior continues in the answering phase until a second EoT is regenerated.
- The length of the spurious reasoning span in the answering phase scales proportionally with the number of reasoning tokens saved by the early exit.
- The authors introduce Exit-token Attention Biasing (EAB) to increase attention to the injected EoT, which reduces spurious CoT termination and answering-phase length across four LRMs and five benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** Matching an LRM's explicit think-block format via token injection is insufficient to control its internal state, requiring attention manipulation to reliably force early exits.

<small>Seunghee Koh, Sungjae Choi, Minchan Kwon, Sunghyun Baek, Junmo Kim</small>

### [High-Dimensional Learning Dynamics of Attention-Indexed Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03858v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-09-03 · `arXiv:2609.03858v1`

Researchers analyzed the high-dimensional learning dynamics of attention-indexed models, demonstrating how different attention parameterizations create implicit architectural biases that dictate optimization speed and sample complexity.

- In the high-dimensional limit, the population-loss landscape is defined by finite trace order parameters, whereas online SGD is governed by an infinite matrix-moment hierarchy that can be truncated with exponential precision.
- Direct optimization of a full attention matrix S in R^{d x d} can trap online SGD in uninformative states.
- Tied attention (S = W W^T) induces automatic symmetry breaking, enabling weak recovery in Theta(d^2 log d) samples.
- Untied attention (S = U V^T) exhibits a fast-slow timescale separation where pre-activation means evolve fast and overlaps evolve slowly; weak recovery requires the fast dynamics to break initial symmetry.

**Why it matters:** Understanding how attention matrix parameterization acts as an implicit bias provides a theoretical foundation for designing faster-converging and more sample-efficient transformer architectures.

<small>Yizhou Xu, Margarita Sagitova, Lenka Zdeborová, Florent Krzakala</small>

### [CORE: Improving Compositional Reasoning in MLLM Embedding via Reranker Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04083v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-09-03 · `arXiv:2609.04083v1`

CORE introduces a Rank-KL objective to distill fine-grained compositional judgments from a multimodal cross-attentive reranker into a dense MLLM embedding model.

- Synthesizes candidate retrieval lists spanning five compositional matching levels to capture subtle attribute-object binding differences.
- Uses a listwise Rank-KL distillation objective that forces the embedding model to mimic the reranker's multi-level probability distribution and ranking order.
- Evaluations show listwise Rank-KL and pairwise CoSENT utilize multi-level supervision better than standard contrastive learning.
- CORE-EMBED-8B achieves a top total average score of 0.666 among embedding models across COLA, SUGARCREPE++, and NEGBENCH benchmarks.
- CORE-RERANKER-8B reaches an 82.7% total average (outperforming Jina-Reranker by 10.7 points) and generalizes to MCMR without degrading COCO or Flickr30K performance.

**Why it matters:** It enables dense embedding models to resolve complex compositional logic and attribute bindings without resorting to computationally expensive cross-attention at retrieval time.

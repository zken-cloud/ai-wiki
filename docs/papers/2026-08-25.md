### [Beyond Imitation: Filtering On-Policy Distillation by Reasoning Progress](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19408)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-08-18 · 👍 5 · `arXiv:2608.19408`

R2-OPD improves on-policy distillation by using progress rewards to filter out teacher supervision that conflicts with actual reasoning progress.

- Standard On-Policy Distillation (OPD) treats all teacher feedback equally, leading to conflicts when valid student reasoning steps receive low rewards for deviating from teacher outputs.
- R2-OPD constructs two within-trajectory rankings of reasoning spans: one based on teacher-derived rewards and one based on independently estimated progress rewards.
- Distillation rewards are selectively suppressed when the teacher-derived ranking and the progress-reward ranking disagree.
- The method filters out conflicting supervision while retaining valid teacher guidance, leading to consistent performance gains in reasoning tasks.

**Why it matters:** It prevents models from penalizing correct reasoning paths during distillation simply because they diverge from the teacher's exact output generation.

<small>Chen Yang, Haiyuan Wan, Rengrong Xiong, Yize Chen, Danny H. K. Tsang</small>

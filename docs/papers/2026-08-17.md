### [Can We Defend Against AI-Generated Video Attacks on Real-World Crisis Events? A Systematic Evaluation of Detectors, Generators and Social Dissemination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14391)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-08-13 · 👍 43 · ⭐ 2 · `arXiv:2608.14391`

Researchers introduced RA-Bench, a benchmark of 17,886 videos across 10 social-risk categories, revealing that current AI video detectors fail to generalize and degrade further during social dissemination.

- RA-Bench pairs 1,830 real-video anchors with 16,056 synthetic clips generated by 4 open-source and 5 closed-source models across 10 social-risk categories.
- Evaluated 19 detection systems—7 traditional detectors, 10 zero-shot multimodal models under 3 review settings, and 2 fine-tuned MLLMs—finding none generalized consistently across RA-Bench instances.
- Detectability varies by generation quality and conditioning, while source-level detection patterns remain stable across sampling seeds.
- Human authenticity tests show that synthetic videos capable of fooling human perception are similarly difficult for automated detectors to classify correctly.
- Simulated social dissemination degrades overall detector reliability, exacerbating real-world failure rates.

**Why it matters:** Existing deepfake detection pipelines are unreliable for real-world crisis management because they fail to generalize to modern video generators and degrade under social media sharing conditions.

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-08-13 · 👍 6 · `arXiv:2608.14106`

Researchers identified forecast collapse in time-series foundation models on low-predictability targets and introduced CalibRank, an objective function that balances forecast calibration and cross-sectional ranking accuracy.

- Forecast collapse causes point predictions to become nearly flat and ruins cross-sectional ranking performance when target predictability is low, such as in hourly stock returns.
- Per-series loss functions like squared error force low-amplitude forecasts to maintain calibration, whereas cross-sectional correlation objectives improve ranking but inflate prediction amplitude by over 10x.
- CalibRank balances calibration and ranking loss, nearly tripling cross-sectional correlation on the Finance1K benchmark while maintaining target-level forecast amplitude across tested models.

**Why it matters:** Standard per-series evaluation metrics obscure cross-series structural failures, which can render foundation model forecasts unusable for downstream multi-asset decision-making.

- Identifies the interval when an agent executes an action and awaits environment observation as a reasoning idle window.

### [Scaling Domain Data Repetition in LLM Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14071)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-08-13 · 👍 3 · `arXiv:2608.14071`

A study on scaling domain data repetition shows that optimal repetition counts mildly increase with model size at a fixed tokens-per-parameter ratio and correlate negatively with a domain's final validation loss.

- At a fixed tokens-per-parameter (TPP) ratio, the optimal repetition count for high-quality domain data mildly increases as the model size scales.
- Optimal repetition count strongly correlates negatively with the domain's final validation loss, meaning lower-loss domains tolerate and benefit from higher repetition.
- The total volume of unique domain data shows only a weak relationship to its optimal repetition count.
- Optimal repetition counts tuned on smaller proxy models with identical TPP ratios can serve as practical estimates for larger LLMs.

**Why it matters:** Practitioners can safely reuse high-quality data repetition schedules optimized on smaller proxy models to prevent domain dilution in larger models without causing severe overfitting.

### [Twin: Playing an Unknown Game with a Test-Time Digital Twin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14490v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-08-14 · `arXiv:2608.14490v1`

Twin uses a frontier coding agent to generate and iteratively repair executable world models at test-time for zero-shot grid games, achieving a 93.3% score on the ARC-AGI-3 benchmark.

- Twin constructs world models by converting transition prediction mismatches into code counterexamples to repair the executable program.
- A replay validation harness prevents game actions until the generated model perfectly reproduces all previously observed game transitions.
- Twin cleared 179 out of 183 levels (97.8%), outperforming human efficiency on 88.3% of cleared levels.
- The system inferred the underlying goal prior to receiving any reward signal in 87.2% of cleared levels, using search to discover goals for the rest.
- Twin raised the base model benchmark score from 7.8% (and 61.1% with an off-the-shelf harness) to 93.3%, clearing 23 of 25 full games.

**Why it matters:** This approach demonstrates that frontier LLMs can automatically reverse-engineer executable environment dynamics and unseen goals purely through test-time interaction and code refinement.

### [Knowing When to Stop: Bayesian Optimal Stopping for LLM Evaluations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14425v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-08-14 · `arXiv:2608.14425v1`

The optstop framework uses precision-based Bayesian adaptive stopping to dynamically allocate LLM evaluation compute based on uncertainty rather than fixed sampling budgets.

- Employs hierarchical Bayesian inference across binary, ordinal, and continuous outcomes without needing a calibrated item bank.
- Keeps every benchmark item eligible for sampling while halting evaluation once estimates achieve sufficient precision or stability.
- Includes a low-performance safeguard that increases sampling caution as performance approaches zero to accurately capture rare successes.
- Achieved 57% to 97% reductions in planned evaluation trials across nine validation settings in a 200-item, 10-epoch test without altering conclusions.

**Why it matters:** It significantly reduces compute costs and runtime for LLM benchmarking without sacrificing statistical confidence or accuracy in the final results.

<small>Toby D. Pilditch</small>

### [Self Gradient Forcing: Native Long Video Extrapolation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20368)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-21 · 👍 34 · ⭐ 115 · `arXiv:2607.20368`

Self Gradient Forcing (SGF) is a two-pass training strategy for autoregressive video diffusion models that addresses the historical context-gradient gap by restoring supervision for memory-writing, enabling stronger long-video extrapolation.

- SGF introduces a two-pass training strategy to provide supervision for how earlier generated latents should be written into key-value caches for future video-latent generation, addressing the historical context-gradient gap.
- Pass 1 performs a no-gradient autoregressive rollout, recording self-generated context and noisy latents at a sampled denoising exit step.
- Pass 2 performs parallel context-gradient reconstruction for the recorded exit step, using the generated context as stop-gradient clean-latent input and recomputing context KV representations and future-to-context causal attention.
- SGF improves native long-video extrapolation, demonstrating stronger subject identity, background/layout consistency, and temporal stability compared to Self Forcing.
- SGF, trained on a 5-second window, can extrapolate videos lasting several minutes.

**Why it matters:** This method significantly extends the duration and consistency of generated videos, making autoregressive video diffusion models more practical for long-form content creation.

<small>Junhao Zhuang, Shiyi Zhang, Yuxuan Bian, Yaowei Li, Yawen Luo, Yijun Liu, Weiyang Jin, Songchun Zhang</small>

### [Beyond Euclidean Clipping: Overcoming Exploration Collapse in LLM RL via Riemannian Isometric Policy Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10169)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-10 · 👍 14 · `arXiv:2607.10169`

RIPO addresses PPO-Clip's exploration collapse in LLM RL by using Riemannian geometry for policy updates, ensuring isometric exploration and improved performance on benchmarks.

- PPO-Clip's exploration collapse stems from implicitly measuring policy discrepancy with a Euclidean metric, inconsistent with the policy Riemannian manifold's intrinsic geometry.
- This geometric mismatch causes conservative updates in low-probability regions and aggressive updates in high-probability regions, leading to exploration collapse.
- Riemannian Isometric Policy Optimization (RIPO) is proposed to correct this by guaranteeing isometric policy updates on the Riemannian manifold.
- RIPO achieves a favorable bias-variance trade-off, stabilizing optimization.
- RIPO significantly outperforms existing LLM RL algorithms, showing up to 60% improvement over GRPO on AIME24 across seven competition-level benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** RIPO's geometric correction to policy optimization can significantly enhance the reasoning capabilities of LLMs by improving RL algorithm stability and exploration efficiency.

<small>Zhicheng Cai, Xinyuan Guo, Hanlin Wu, Mingxuan Wang, Wei-Ying Ma, Ya-Qin Zhang, Hao Zhou</small>

Natural-language autoencoders for activation explanations are shown to be unfaithful to individual claims; RECAP, a method using co-trained auxiliary predictors, improves decodability and verifiability of internal content.

- RECAP improves verifiability: an independent probe scores true claims above false ones (AUC 0.96 vs 0.82 without RECAP) and maintains lie detection against adversaries (AUC 0.95 vs control's 0.51).

**Why it matters:** This work provides a method to make internal model content independently checkable for AI safety and improves the reliability of activation explanations.

### [Expanding Flow Maps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21585v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-07-23 · `arXiv:2607.21585v1`

Expanding Generative Flows (EFlows) and Expanding Flow Maps (EFMs) extend flow-based generative models to variable dimensions and sequence lengths by combining conditional state expansion with transport maps.

- EFlows define flows between distributions of increasing dimensionality using an expanding interpolant that augments the state with conditional noise.
- EFMs distill expanding interpolants into few-step models by factoring the transition into an expand operator and a transport map.
- The expand operator adds coordinates or tokens conditioned on the current state, while the transport map pushes the expanded state forward along the interpolant.
- Fixed-canvas flows and flow maps are recovered as special cases when the expand operator is the identity map.
- The framework extends to the discrete simplex to support variable-size graph generation and variable-length sequence generation.

**Why it matters:** It provides a unified, principled flow-matching framework that enables fast, few-step generation when output size or sequence length must be learned dynamically.

<small>Sophia Tang, Pranam Chatterjee</small>

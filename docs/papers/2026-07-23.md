### [Self Gradient Forcing: Native Long Video Extrapolation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20368)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-21 · 👍 34 · ⭐ 115 · `arXiv:2607.20368`

Self Gradient Forcing (SGF) is a two-pass training strategy for autoregressive video diffusion models that addresses the historical context-gradient gap by restoring supervision for memory-writing, enabling stronger long-video extrapolation.

- SGF introduces a two-pass training strategy to provide supervision for how earlier generated latents should be written into key-value caches for future video-latent generation, addressing the historical context-gradient gap.
- Pass 1 performs a no-gradient autoregressive rollout, recording self-generated context and noisy latents at a sampled denoising exit step.
- Pass 2 performs parallel context-gradient reconstruction for the recorded exit step, using the generated context as stop-gradient clean-latent input and recomputing context KV representations and future-to-context causal attention.
- SGF improves native long-video extrapolation, demonstrating stronger subject identity, background/layout consistency, and temporal stability compared to Self Forcing.
- SGF, trained on a 5-second window, can extrapolate videos lasting several minutes.

**Why it matters:** This method significantly extends the duration and consistency of generated videos, making autoregressive video diffusion models more practical for long-form content creation.

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-10 · 👍 14 · `arXiv:2607.10169`

RIPO addresses PPO-Clip's exploration collapse in LLM RL by using Riemannian geometry for policy updates, ensuring isometric exploration and improved performance on benchmarks.

- PPO-Clip's exploration collapse stems from implicitly measuring policy discrepancy with a Euclidean metric, inconsistent with the policy Riemannian manifold's intrinsic geometry.
- This geometric mismatch causes conservative updates in low-probability regions and aggressive updates in high-probability regions, leading to exploration collapse.
- Riemannian Isometric Policy Optimization (RIPO) is proposed to correct this by guaranteeing isometric policy updates on the Riemannian manifold.
- RIPO achieves a favorable bias-variance trade-off, stabilizing optimization.
- RIPO significantly outperforms existing LLM RL algorithms, showing up to 60% improvement over GRPO on AIME24 across seven competition-level benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** RIPO's geometric correction to policy optimization can significantly enhance the reasoning capabilities of LLMs by improving RL algorithm stability and exploration efficiency.

### [Windowed-MTP: Removing the Full-Context Draft-KV Tax at Million-Token Context](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21535v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-07-23 · `arXiv:2607.21535v1`

Windowed-MTP applies a StreamingLLM-style sliding window and attention sink to the draft head of Multi-Token-Prediction (MTP) speculative decoding, bounding KV cost and improving performance at million-token contexts.

- MTP draft heads typically run full attention over the entire KV cache, causing draft cost to grow linearly with context and dominate at million-token contexts.
- Windowed-MTP applies a StreamingLLM-style sliding window plus attention sink to only the draft's attention, leaving the full-attention target verification intact.
- This approach is training-free, drop-in, and lossless, bounding the draft's KV working set to a constant and dropping ~99% of KV entries at 1M context.
- Windowing cuts per-decode-step cost by +28% to +44% across Qwen GDN-MoE 35B/122B and Mamba2-hybrid NoPE 120B at 1M context on a single GPU in SGLang.
- The unread draft KV (7.7-11% of total KV at 1M) is reclaimed via a compact ring buffer without affecting acceptance or quality.

**Why it matters:** This method significantly reduces the computational overhead of speculative decoding at long contexts, making it more efficient for large language models.

<small>Alagappan Valliappan</small>

### [Learning What Matters: Supervising Global Context Pruning with Causal Evidence Sets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21692v2)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-07-23 · `arXiv:2607.21692v2`

This paper introduces supervising global context pruning with causal evidence sets, demonstrating that attention does not reliably indicate causal dependence for context selection in retrieval tasks.

- Attention and causal dependence disagree on which context blocks are essential, with selectors distilled from attention inheriting this discrepancy.
- Teachers attend to outdated facts and exhibit varying attention patterns across training runs, even with identical evidence.
- In two-step reference tasks, attention at the answer position can skip intermediate steps, with skipping rates varying from 36% to 98% across teachers.
- Selectors trained on causal evidence sets achieve 99% or better routing accuracy, outperforming attention-distilled selectors, and these causal sets can be recovered by masking alone.
- Pretrained models like Qwen2.5-3B and Gemma-2-9B show similar conflicts, with Gemma-2-9B's accuracy increasing from 56% to 99% when restricted to causally relevant sentences.

**arXiv cs.CR** · 2026-07-23 · `arXiv:2607.20860v1`

IRIS is a black-box auditing method for LLM gateways that detects model substitution and routing dilution using only returned text, fingerprints backends, and estimates routing fractions with a self-sizing query budget.

- IRIS fingerprints LLM backends by asking endpoints to generate random numbers or strings, requiring only the returned text.

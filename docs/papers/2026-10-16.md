### [Waypoint-OPD: Transferring RL-Induced Policy Shifts via Gated On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21601)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-14 · 👍 181 · ⭐ 2 · `arXiv:2610.21601`

Waypoint-OPD improves policy-shift distillation by using directional agreement between early and late teacher RL shifts to gate endpoint shifts on student trajectories.

- Identifies that endpoint policy shifts can mask inconsistent local action preferences where early and late teacher shifts reinforce or cancel out.
- Introduces Waypoint-OPD, which evaluates agreement between early and late teacher shifts at student-visited prefixes via an intermediate RL checkpoint.
- Gates the endpoint shift to suppress updates when early and late shifts point in opposing directions, requiring only one extra frozen-teacher scoring pass.
- Outperforms endpoint-only transfer across all six domain-student settings using RL-trained Qwen3-4B teachers and Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-14B, and Qwen3-30B-A3B students.

**Why it matters:** It enables more accurate knowledge transfer from RL-trained teachers to students by filtering out noisy or inconsistent policy shifts without heavy computational overhead.

### [Each Reward to Its Relevant Tokens: Reward-Specific Token-Level Credit Assignment for Multi-Reward RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21439)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-15 · 👍 14 · ⭐ 1 · `arXiv:2610.21439`

MRTPO improves multi-reward RL fine-tuning by using Jensen-Shannon distance to assign individual reward advantages to relevant tokens without requiring auxiliary models or token-level annotations.

- Addresses the limitation of methods like GDPO, which normalize distinct rewards separately but still spread advantages evenly across all response tokens.
- Calculates token relevance by replaying responses with and without a specific reward's verifier information and measuring the per-token Jensen-Shannon distance between the two resulting next-token distributions.
- Reassigns advantage values on a per-token basis specifically for the corresponding reward based on this calculated relevance metric.
- Demonstrates faster convergence and higher performance across text-to-SQL, multi-constraint instruction following, and tool-calling tasks.

**Why it matters:** It enables more precise credit assignment in multi-reward reinforcement learning for language models without requiring manual token annotations or extra reward models.

### [Knowledge That Is Linear Before Fine-Tuning Generalizes After It](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22300v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-10-15 · `arXiv:2610.22300v1`

A study across 11 language models demonstrates that how linearly readable a relation's facts are in pretrained representations strongly predicts post-fine-tuning generalization to held-out subjects.

- Distinguishes 'active facts' that support generalization from 'inert facts' that can only be recalled without enabling task generalization.
- Tested generalization by fine-tuning models on synthetic composite relations (e.g., mapping a subject to an arbitrary label assigned to its original attribute, such as Paris -> Europe -> crab) and evaluating on held-out subjects (e.g., Munich -> crab).
- Pretrained linear probe readability correlates strongly with post-fine-tuning generalization performance across relations (Pearson r = 0.87 to 0.92).
- The correlation holds when controlling for pretraining frequency, fine-tuning dataset size, original model accuracy, label reassignment seeds, and 13 semantic label categories.
- Incorrect predictions by fine-tuned models map back to original answers that align more closely with the linear probe's top outputs than with the pretrained model's raw generative outputs.

**Why it matters:** Evaluating the linear readability of pretrained internal representations allows practitioners to predict whether a model will successfully generalize downstream fine-tuning tasks across related entities.

<small>Faridoun Mehri, Hinrich Schütze</small>

### [Towards Continual Learning: Mitigating Forgetting With Subspace Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22293v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-10-15 · `arXiv:2610.22293v1`

Subspace Tuning (SST) mitigates catastrophic forgetting in LLM fine-tuning by restricting weight updates to the bottom singular subspace, preserving principal singular directions.

- Identifies that SFT causes catastrophic forgetting by updating principal singular directions of weight matrices, whereas SST restricts updates strictly to the bottom singular subspace.
- Combines dense gradient estimation, a subspace refresh mechanism, and tight protection of up to 99% of the weight subspace.
- Reduces KL drift by 2x to 4x compared to baseline recipes, keeping fine-tuned models closer to their base distribution.
- Integrates as an optimizer-agnostic method compatible with Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and self-distillation, with retention gains scaling alongside model and dataset size.

**Why it matters:** SST improves the learning-forgetting Pareto frontier, allowing LLMs to acquire new target-task skills while nearly eliminating catastrophic forgetting in large-scale models.

Training-free on-policy distillation aggregates fine-tuned specialist models into a single base model using layer-wise least-squares regression over input second moments gathered from forward passes on rollouts, eliminating gradient steps.

### [Human Interventions as Labels: Localizing, Steering, and (Sometimes) Editing the Computations Behind LLM Agent Failures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21708v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-10-15 · `arXiv:2610.21708v1`

Researchers use human intervention events as diagnostic labels to localize, steer, and weight-edit the internal computation driving LLM agent over-asking failures across five open model families.

- Steering vectors derived from intervention labels causally flip 72-100% of held-out over-asking decisions at specific residual-stream depths, whereas random control directions flip at most one episode.
- Mechanistic probing reveals that in four of five evaluated model families (Qwen2.5-3B, gemma-2-2b, OLMo-3-7B, Mistral-Nemo-12B, Phi-4), the model internally represents that the prompt is unambiguous yet still generates unnecessary clarifying questions.

### [Evaluation Awareness Beyond English: Do LLMs Act Differently When Tested in Indic Languages?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22333v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-10-15 · `arXiv:2610.22333v1`

A study across four instruction-tuned LLM families shows evaluation awareness increases safety refusals in five Indic languages by an average of 14.4 percentage points, matching English refusal levels while elevating benign over-refusals.

- Evaluated four instruction-tuned LLMs across five Indic languages and English using prompts with monitoring and safety-test cues.
- Refusal rates for harmful requests increased in all tested models and across all six languages when explicit evaluation cues were present.

**Why it matters:** Standard safety evaluations in Indic languages may artificially overestimate real-world model alignment due to evaluation awareness altering default generation behavior.

KnowGPO improves search agent reinforcement learning by grouping trajectories and calculating process rewards based on atomic knowledge state transitions rather than surface-level state observations.

- Decomposes target query answers into atomic knowledge conditions to track task-relevant information gain.
- Groups trajectories sharing identical knowledge states to enable within-group baseline comparisons.

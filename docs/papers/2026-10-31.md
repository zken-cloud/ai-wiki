### [Proving and Improving the Platonic Representation Hypothesis with JEPAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39302)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-28 · 👍 55 · ⭐ 26 · `arXiv:2610.39302`

Researchers proved the Platonic Representation Theorem for Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPAs) and showed that positive view choice, rather than scale alone, drives cross-modal representation alignment.

- The Platonic Representation Theorem proves separately trained JEPAs converge to identical representations up to a single rotation when positive pairs observe the same scene ('good views') and misalignment is bounded.
- Next-token prediction acts as a JEPA with 'bad views' (document segments), explaining why language models require scale to align with vision models.
- A 109M-parameter text JEPA aligns better with DINOv2 and CLIP image encoders than evaluated language models up to 72B parameters, requiring 42x fewer training samples than next-token prediction.
- Aligning separately trained encoders via a single rotation yields an image captioner achieving 0.838 CIDEr on COCO and 64% top-5 pair-free zero-shot accuracy on ImageNet-100.

**Why it matters:** Using JEPAs with high-quality positive views offers a mathematically proven, sample-efficient alternative to scaling language models for cross-modal alignment.

### [Multi-Token Prediction Done Right](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38331)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-27 · 👍 11 · ⭐ 32 · `arXiv:2610.38331`

Soft-MTP fixes a flaw in standard Multi-Token Prediction training by isolating the useful KL self-distillation signal from a noisy gradient residual using a straight-through gradient-routing trick.

- Identifies that standard MTP cross-entropy gradients decompose into a beneficial KL self-distillation term and a low-signal-to-noise ratio residual that degrades optimization at scale.
- Introduces the KL gradient-recovery trick, using straight-through gradient routing to exact-recover the KL divergence gradient from combined cross-entropy gradients without materializing vocabulary-sized logit matrices.
- Compatible with standard fused cross-entropy kernels, DeepSeek-style MTP, and Meta-style MTP while adding zero parameters and zero measurable computational overhead.
- Demonstrates performance gains across 167M to 1.2B scales, including lower next-token prediction perplexity, higher zero-shot accuracy, faster speculative decoding, and improved downstream math and code reasoning.

**Why it matters:** Soft-MTP provides a zero-overhead, parameter-free optimization fix for multi-token pre-training that improves downstream reasoning capabilities and speculative decoding speed.

### [AB-Attention: Accurate Block Attention for Accelerating Video Diffusion Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39663)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-28 · 👍 5 · ⭐ 24 · `arXiv:2610.39663`

AB-Attention is a training-free sparse attention method that accelerates Video Diffusion Transformers via local query clustering, probability-weighted block sampling, and fine-grained GPU tensor-core tile gathering.

- Intra-Block Clustering Selector (IBCS) computes cheap, accurate block importance by clustering queries strictly within each query block without global token permutation.
- Sampling with Inclusion-Probability Weighting (SIP) samples attention blocks proportional to importance estimates and reweights them by inverse inclusion probability to unbiasedly approximate dense attention.
- A custom kernel gathers fine-grained key/value blocks into full tensor-core tiles to refine selection without incurring extra tensor-core overhead.
- Demonstrates 89-92% attention reduction, up to 2.23x end-to-end inference speedup, and up to 4.4 dB PSNR improvement over top baselines on Wan2.1 and HunyuanVideo.

**Why it matters:** It enables significantly faster video diffusion generation at high sparsity levels without degrading visual fidelity or requiring costly retraining.

### [PIVOT: Progressive Intervention via Online Teacher Correction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40576v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-10-29 · `arXiv:2610.40576v1`

PIVOT combines trajectory-level interventions with token-level KL supervision by inserting teacher-generated corrections directly into student rollouts during online reinforcement learning from verifiable rewards (RLVR).

- Addresses the sparse reward problem in RLVR where groups of entirely failed rollouts provide zero learning signal.
- Combines trajectory-level intervention (to access reward-bearing paths) with dense token-level KL supervision on teacher-corrected segments alongside standard RLVR objectives.
- Demonstrates that applying trajectory-level intervention or token-level supervision alone is insufficient for optimal student learning.
- Increases mean avg@8 performance on hard logic games from 19.5% (best baseline) to 35.6%.

**Why it matters:** PIVOT enables language models to learn complex logic and mathematical reasoning tasks where standard RLVR fails due to extremely sparse rewards.

<small>Zhihao Yang, Boyuan Zheng, Puhui Tan, Yukun Chen, Kuan Zhang, Longze Chen, Jiaming Li, Renke Shan</small>

### [Every Token Needs Its Own Teacher: Multi-teacher Adaptive On-policy Self-distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40574v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-10-29 · `arXiv:2610.40574v1`

MTA-OPSD introduces token-level adaptive selection from a multi-teacher pool for on-policy self-distillation, allowing every token to select its preferred teacher dynamically during training.

- Existing OPSD methods keep the teacher fixed across training or within responses, failing to address token-by-token variance in teacher preference.
- A pilot study demonstrates that optimal teacher preference varies at the token level and can be identified using majority voting and KL-based selection.
- MTA-OPSD creates a multi-teacher pool with candidate teachers that provide token-specific adaptive supervision during training.
- Empirical evaluation using Qwen3 models on mathematical reasoning tasks demonstrates performance improvements up to +5.9 over the base model.

**Why it matters:** Adapting teacher supervision at the individual token level significantly improves the performance of large language models during post-training self-distillation.

<small>Yifu Luo, Chuang Ma, Zeyu Chen, Enkelejda Kasneci, Qi Chen, Gjergji Kasneci</small>

### [Incentivizing Self-Learning in Large Language Models via Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39671v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-10-29 · `arXiv:2610.39671v1`

A reinforcement learning approach using a custom interface incentivizes LLMs to autonomously acquire, process, and apply knowledge from long-form textbooks to solve complex mathematical problems.

- Introduces the BookNavigator interface to enable LLMs to navigate long-form reference texts, learn principles, and generate proofs on the fly without reference solutions.
- Uses reinforcement learning (RL) with fine-grained rewards to train models in domain-agnostic self-learning strategies.
- Improves overall proof accuracy of Intern-S2-Preview-35B from 51.5% to 79.5%, outperforming textbook-free and fixed-retrieval RL baselines.
- Demonstrates generalizability to out-of-domain tasks, boosting base model accuracy on long-context benchmarks BrowseComp-Plus (+1.7 percentage points) and CL-bench (+2.3 percentage points).

**Why it matters:** Reinforcement learning over autonomous reference navigation allows LLMs to handle unfamiliar domains and long-context problem solving without relying on preassembled contexts or static retrieval.

<small>Lingkai Kong, Yuzhe Gu, Haiteng Zhao, Zijian Wu, Youqing Fang, Zhouqi Hua, Yanan Sun, Kai Chen</small>

### [Raincheck: Deferred Multimodal Prefill for Efficient Multi-Turn MLLM Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39562v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-10-29 · `arXiv:2610.39562v1`

Raincheck introduces deferred multimodal prefill for MLLMs, progressively processing pruned visual tokens during idle decode capacity and scheduling gaps to restore multi-turn accuracy without increasing TTFT.

- Decouples initial token pruning from permanent information loss by background-prefilling unselected tokens in source order during decode steps and post-answer idle gaps.
- Uses attention visibility control and affine state mapping to update context dynamically after generation starts in full-attention and hybrid recurrent models.
- Employs prefill-decode fusion to mitigate repeated model weight reads during simultaneous decoding and background prefilling.
- Evaluated on Qwen3-VL-8B and Qwen3.5-9B at 30% initial token retention, recovering 77.7% (MultiVerse) and 60.4% (ConvBench) of quality lost by hard pruning.
- SGLang implementation cuts mean first-turn TTFT by up to 24.1% compared to full prefill, while increasing mean TPOT by less than 0.5%.

**Why it matters:** It enables MLLMs to achieve the low initial latency of aggressively pruned prefill while recovering lost accuracy for subsequent conversation turns at virtually no TPOT overhead.

<small>Wenjie Huang, Jun Zhang, Hongyaoxing Gu, Haoyang Huang, Tianqi Xu, Xinyue Hao, Xinyi Hu, Xinle Du</small>

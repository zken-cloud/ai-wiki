### [IdeaAMBIG: Benchmarking Implementation-Critical Gaps in Research-Idea Specifications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10539)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-08 · 👍 22 · `arXiv:2609.10539`

Researchers introduce IdeaAMBIG, a 660-instance benchmark designed to evaluate LLMs on detecting and resolving implementation-critical specification gaps in research methods.

- IdeaAMBIG consists of 660 evidence-grounded instances, comprising 163 real-world gaps from GitHub issues and reproducibility reports, alongside 497 controlled synthetic gaps.
- The benchmark evaluates three tasks: codification-readiness assessment, defect localization (given only the specification), and clarification action generation (given the annotated defect).
- Across 13 evaluated LLMs, top performance reached only a 9.6% Macro Defect Recovery Rate on real-world instances, but achieved an 80.6% Macro Clarification Action Success Rate when defects were pre-identified.
- An oracle study demonstrated that providing gold resolutions increased downstream codification-ready rates from 14% to 98%, highlighting defect localization as the main bottleneck.

**Why it matters:** Identifying methodological gaps in research papers is currently the primary failure point preventing autonomous coding agents from successfully reproducing scientific codebases.

<small>Yiling Ma, Yilun Zhao, Sihong Wu, Manasi Patwardhan, Arman Cohan</small>

### [ActReview: Rebuttal-Guided Training Data and Rubric Rewards for Actionable Peer Review Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09076)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-07 · 👍 21 · `arXiv:2609.09076`

ActReview is a rebuttal-guided post-training framework that uses review-rebuttal threads to train models to generate actionable peer reviews consisting of diagnostic claims and grounded revision suggestions.

- Deconstructs actionable peer-review generation into diagnostic claim generation and revision suggestion generation.
- Constructs ActReview-40K from OpenReview threads by mapping reviewer concerns to author rebuttals and paper-localized evidence.
- Post-trains Qwen3-8B-Base via multi-task supervised fine-tuning (SFT) followed by Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) using candidate-aware, weakness-specific rubric rewards.
- Introduces ActReview-Bench, a human-curated benchmark of 1,000 instances designed to evaluate diagnostic quality and revision usefulness.

**Why it matters:** Using author rebuttals as latent supervision enables LLMs to generate more actionable, evidence-grounded revision feedback for pre-submission paper refinement.

<small>Yiling Ma, Yilun Zhao, Sihong Wu, Ziyu Chen, Manasi Patwardhan, Arman Cohan</small>

### [The Semantic Bottleneck: Leveraging Semantic Representations for Non-Invasive Speech Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10296)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-08 · 👍 5 · `arXiv:2609.10296`

Brain2Semantics2Text introduces a non-invasive speech decoding method that maps sentence-level MEG signals into an intermediate semantic embedding space before converting them into natural language text.

- Leverages a semantic bottleneck to reconstruct high-level sentence meaning without requiring word-level temporal alignment.
- Maps sentence-level MEG responses directly into a continuous semantic manifold, which is then inverted to generate text.
- Targets high-level semantic representations rather than low-level acoustic or lexical features to handle the low signal-to-noise ratio of non-invasive neural recordings.
- Demonstrates improved sentence-level text reconstruction performance compared to prior non-invasive Brain2Text approaches.

**Why it matters:** Focusing on slower-evolving semantic representations allows non-invasive neural interfaces to overcome low signal-to-noise ratios and decode meaningful continuous text from MEG recordings.

<small>Gilad D. Landau, Dulhan Jayalath, Oiwi Parker Jones</small>

### [PlannerForge: LLM Agents for Scenario-Based Testing of Motion Planners in Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08965)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-07 · 👍 5 · ⭐ 4 · `arXiv:2609.08965`

PlannerForge is a unified LLM-agent framework covering the end-to-end scenario-based testing pipeline for autonomous driving motion planners, including generation, retrieval, modification, assessment, enhancement, and benchmarking.

- Outperforms Scenario Factory 2.0 by generating 193 executable scenarios out of 200 from natural language queries, realizing 92-96% of specified road, city, and vehicle attributes.
- Beats BM25 on rank-1 scenario selection (92.0% vs. 67.5%) and exceeds From-Words-to-Collisions on physically valid scenario edits (>=94% vs. 31%).
- Open-source 20-35B parameter backends like Qwen3.6:35B match commercial APIs on three out of five tasks, with end-to-end module chaining retaining 78-83% of seed queries.
- Cost-tuning at N=400 improves planner success from 50.4% to 70.2% and decreases collision rates from 19.0% to 8.4% without requiring domain-specific fine-tuning.

**Why it matters:** It replaces fragmented, multi-tool testing workflows with a unified, automated LLM framework that improves planner safety without domain-specific model fine-tuning.

<small>Yuan Gao, Sebastian Müller, Mattia Piccinini, Marc Kaufeld, Yuchen Zhang, Finn Rasmus Schäfer, Qunying Song, Johannes Betz</small>

### [Reference-Based Bias Detection in LLMs via Relative Representations of Hidden States](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10060)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-08 · 👍 3 · `arXiv:2609.10060`

A reference-based auditing method measures Representational Bias Shift (ΔB) in LLM hidden states via relative representations against anchor sentences, detecting internal bias changes without needing generated text or judge models.

- Encodes sentences via similarities to fixed anchor sentences, allowing direct comparison of hidden-state geometries before and after fine-tuning.
- Correlates with output-level bias shifts in 15 of 18 tested settings, achieving up to \|r\| = 0.84 (p < 0.001) under full fine-tuning.
- Detects checkpoints with increased bias at ROC AUCs between 0.65 and 0.99, outperforming a SEAT-based baseline on WildGuardMix and DecodingTrust.
- Completes an audit in approximately three minutes, consuming 3x to 50x less compute than traditional output-level benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** It enables fast, low-compute auditing of fine-tuned model checkpoints for bias shifts without relying on expensive text generation or task-specific evaluation datasets.

<small>Marek Jeliński, Jan Dubiński, Maciej Chrabaszcz, Sebastian Cygert</small>

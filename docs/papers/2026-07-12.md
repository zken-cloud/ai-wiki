### [Sticky Jump Diffusions: A Unifying View of Masked, Continuous, and Hybrid Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10951v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-07-12 · `arXiv:2607.10951v1`

Sticky Jump Diffusions (SJDs) are introduced as continuous-time Markov processes unifying masked, continuous, and hybrid diffusion models through a novel time reversal mechanism coupling a score-driven SDE with a sticky jump kernel.

- SJDs are continuous-time Markov processes on R^d where discrete anchors (token embeddings) release mass at a hazard rate, which then diffuses in continuous space.
- Time reversal in SJD couples a score-driven SDE with a sticky jump kernel, whose rate and destination are determined by flux balance with the forward law.
- The score and per-anchor reverse hazards are estimated using Denoising Hazard Matching, a hazard analogue of denoising score matching, with simulation-free cross-entropy training.
- SJD recovers masked diffusion, continuous diffusion, and hybrid diffusion as limiting cases, explaining their inherent features through flux balance.
- The unsticking kernel becomes a design space, allowing for cross-position blending to incorporate inductive biases like spatial locality or constraint graphs, improving performance on CIFAR-10, Text8, and Sudoku.

**Why it matters:** This framework provides a unified theoretical foundation for various diffusion models, enabling new design spaces for corruption processes and potentially improving model performance across diverse data types.

<small>Pascal Jutras-Dubé, Patrick Pynadath, Jeremy Lu, Yuan Gao, Ruqi Zhang</small>

### [Diagnosing and Mitigating Thinking Collapse in On-Policy Self-Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10805v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-07-12 · `arXiv:2607.10805v1`

On-Policy Self-Distillation (OPSD) can degrade LLM performance in complex reasoning due to "Thinking Collapse"; Adaptive Dual-Perspective OPSD (AD-OPSD) mitigates this by dynamically moderating the distillation objective.

- Thinking Collapse is identified as a sharp decline in a model's native intermediate reasoning behavior, measured by epistemic-token density (ET per 1k), occurring in OPSD.
- Collapse is triggered by aggressive teacher gradients at high-student-entropy decision forks, suppressing student epistemic tokens into teacher non-epistemic targets, concentrated in high pointwise student-teacher divergence regions.
- AD-OPSD is proposed as a robust control framework that dynamically moderates the self-distillation objective.
- AD-OPSD selectively anchors high-suppression-risk sandboxed tokens to a reference prior from the frozen base model via an asymmetrical pointwise divergence gate.
- AD-OPSD improves over standard OPSD by up to +4.1% absolute average accuracy across mathematical benchmarks and diverse model scales.

**Why it matters:** This work provides a method to improve the reliability and performance of LLMs in complex reasoning tasks when using self-distillation techniques.

<small>Keqin Peng, Chen Li, Yuanxin Ouyang, Yancheng Yuan, Liang Ding</small>

- Pass@k inversion occurs because RLVR training may eliminate rare correct trajectories present in the base model, especially for 'boundary prompts'.

- AWA-RL boosts absolute precision by up to 10.3% and overall RA-F1 by 2.9% compared to non-abstaining baselines, with marginal raw accuracy sacrifice.

**Why it matters:** This method improves the reliability of search-augmented LLMs by reducing hallucinations, making them more trustworthy for open-domain QA tasks.

### [Distributed Denial of Science: How Indirect Data Poisoning of AI Systems Can Industrialize Scientific Fraud](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10712v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-07-12 · `arXiv:2607.10712v1`

A new indirect data poisoning attack corrupts open datasets, leading AI systems to generate fraudulent scientific conclusions, with a 49.56% success rate and 6.0% detection rate across tested frontier AI models.

- The indirect data poisoning attack involves corrupting an open dataset and uploading it to a public repository, which autonomous research agents then retrieve and process.
- Across five topics, three frontier AI systems (Claude Code with Claude Opus 4.7, Codex with GPT-5.5, Gemini CLI with Gemini 3.1 Pro), and 450 experimental runs, poisoning succeeded in 49.56% of cases, with only 6.0% detection.
- The attack does not require topic-specific trigger words, agent access, indirect prompt injection, or fabricated papers, relying solely on the open data ecosystem and misleading metadata.
- A 'scientist persona' mitigation reduced poisoned conclusions to 16.67%, while a data provenance audit with five checks (referencing papers, social markers, statistical anomalies, related datasets, poisoning caution) reduced attack success to zero.

**Why it matters:** This attack vector could enable scientific fraud at an unprecedented scale by weaponizing AI in research, necessitating robust data auditing mechanisms.

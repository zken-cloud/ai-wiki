### [NCP-ArchPreview Technical Report: Moving towards Latent Space Language Models through Next Concept Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10715)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-08 · 👍 105 · `arXiv:2609.10715`

NCP-ArchPreview introduces Next Concept Prediction (NCP) alongside token prediction, scaling latent-space language modeling to 8.9B parameters and 5.73T tokens using product-quantized concept vocabularies derived from hidden states.

- Combines Next Token Prediction (NTP) with Next Concept Prediction (NCP) trained jointly end-to-end, using a product-quantized concept vocabulary constructed from internal hidden states.

- Injecting concept representations into a DFlash2 drafter increases the mean accepted token length by 4.17% with negligible overhead.

**Why it matters:** It demonstrates that integrating multi-token latent concept prediction into autoregressive models significantly improves sample efficiency and downstream performance at scale compared to standard token-only models.

### [Why Does Post-Training Quantization Work?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11716v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-09-10 · `arXiv:2609.11716v1`

A new study reveals two mechanisms explaining post-training quantization robustness in pretrained LLMs: layer-wise error cancellation via residual interactions and LM-head geometry that protects high-confidence token probabilities.

- Pretrained models accumulate hidden-state error significantly slower than randomly initialized models during quantized forward passes.
- Newly introduced quantization error in a given layer tends to oppose inherited input error, causing partial cancellation across residual connections.
- The counteracting residual interaction mechanism develops naturally during the pretraining process rather than through quantization-aware training.
- LM-head geometry preferentially preserves the logits and probabilities of top-ranked, high-confidence tokens, mitigating output distribution shifts.

**Why it matters:** Understanding the intrinsic mechanisms behind quantization tolerance helps explain why LLM compression preserves accuracy without needing retraining.

### [ChronoReplay: One Answer Interface Is Not Enough for Streaming Spatial Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34283)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-24 · 👍 216 · ⭐ 7 · `arXiv:2610.34283`

ChronoReplay addresses interface lock-in in streaming spatial VLMs by replaying heterogeneous spatial supervision as chronologically ordered episodes with source-native targets and balanced family weighting.

- Interface lock-in occurs when optimizing a spatial VLM for one readout fails to transfer to or degrades performance on other spatial tasks.
- ChronoReplay improves multi-benchmark evaluation, achieving a 13.41-point gain on OST-Bench and outperforming a shifted-order control by ~2.41 OST points.
- Ablations show family-balanced learning outperforms ordinary joint SFT, while answer serialization redistributes gains across OST-Bench and UCS-Bench.
- Adaptive frame selection applied to the fixed model provides further inference-time performance gains at lower visual compute cost.

**Why it matters:** It demonstrates how to train a single spatial VLM checkpoint that excels across diverse spatial tasks without sacrificing performance on unaligned spatial readouts.

### [MuonGS: Pretraining LLMs with Relaxed Weight-Norm Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37647v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-10-27 · `arXiv:2610.37647v1`

MuonGS introduces Gram-Schmidt projection into the Muon optimizer to remove radial update components, relaxing weight-norm constraints to enable faster accumulated effective learning rate growth and improved LLM pretraining performance.

- Analyzes norm control via the accumulated effective learning rate $C_T=\sum_{t<T}\eta_t/\\|W_t\\|_F$, demonstrating vanilla Muon accumulates logarithmically while MuonW/MuonH guarantee linear accumulation.
- Proposes MuonGS, which uses Gram-Schmidt projection to remove radial update components while allowing weight norms to grow at a rate bounded by $O(\sqrt{t})$.
- Proves MuonGS guarantees a sustained accumulation rate of $C_T=\Omega(\sqrt{T})$ and establishes an $O(T^{-1/2})$ stationarity guarantee for smooth scale-invariant objectives.
- Demonstrates lower validation perplexity over MuonH in pretraining tests across 160M, 400M dense models and a 135M active/1B total parameter MoE model, yielding up to a ~15% token-equivalent speedup.

**Why it matters:** Relaxing strict weight-norm constraints allows optimizers to sustain exploration late in training, providing up to a 15% token efficiency gain over state-of-the-art Muon variants.

<small>Athanasios Glentis, Dawei Li, Mingyi Hong</small>

### [The Structure Gap: When More Data Cannot Replace Structured Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37523v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-10-27 · `arXiv:2610.37523v1`

The paper introduces the "structure gap," proving mathematically and empirically that finite data cannot match the finite-resource accuracy, extrapolation, and certification of methods incorporating known domain structure.

- Defines "structure-encoded problems" as tasks constrained by explicit structures like physical laws, graph topologies, symmetry groups, or optimization objectives.
- Proves the structure gap exactly for a Gaussian inverse problem, showing the known-operator Wiener solver achieves Bayes risk while a dense linear empirical learner suffers persistent expected excess risk.
- Establishes mathematical bounds via a cell-coverage lower bound and an unobserved-orbit non-identifiability result.
- Demonstrates the phenomenon using controlled experiments in known-operator inversion, sparse recovery, and graph reachability with size extrapolation.

**Why it matters:** It demonstrates that scaling dataset size alone cannot replace explicit domain constraints for certified generalization and extrapolation in structured tasks.

### [Collective Dynamics of Self-Reinforcement: How Cross-Task Coupling Drives Policy Collapse in Unsupervised RLVR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37553v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-10-27 · `arXiv:2610.37553v1`

Researchers show that policy collapse in label-free RLVR is a collective instability driven by cross-task coupling, predicting and proving a critical dataset size threshold N_c beyond which shared parameter perturbations outpace per-problem sharpening.

- Policy collapse in label-free RLVR occurs when cross-task coupling causes a shared perturbation mode to grow at a rate proportional to \lambda^2, independent of training set size N.

### [Watermark Basins: Black-Box Detection of Distilled LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37266v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-10-27 · `arXiv:2610.37266v1`

WM-Basin is a black-box detector that identifies distilled LLMs by detecting a persistent, key-specific geometric signal left in the parameter space by watermarked teacher traces.

- Watermarked teacher outputs create a persistent 'watermark basin' in parameter space orthogonal to ordinary task-specific fine-tuning subspaces.
- WM-Basin operates via API queries without needing student model weights or logits, mapping generated text to gradients using a clean probe model and projecting out task directions to analyze residual gradient alignment.
- Evaluated on student architectures from 135M to 8.2B parameters, WM-Basin achieves detection rates of 79% (n-gram=1) and 45% (n-gram=3) at a 0.1% false-positive rate, outperforming token-level baselines (29% and 1.3%).
- The parameter-space watermark signal persists through post-distillation modifications such as quantization, pruning, and reinforcement learning post-training.

**Why it matters:** It provides model providers with a robust method to prove unauthorized API-based distillation of their proprietary models, even when students sample at low temperatures or apply post-processing.

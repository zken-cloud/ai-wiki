### [See2Think: Do Multimodal Models Really Use Intermediate Visual States?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26769)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-28 · 👍 21 · ⭐ 3 · `arXiv:2607.26769`

See2Think introduces a unified evaluation framework, See2ThinkBench and Visual Action-of-Thought (VAoT), to assess how multimodal models utilize intermediate visual states in reasoning across 1,200 visually dependent problems.

- See2ThinkBench comprises 1,200 open-ended, visually dependent problems across 12 task categories, covering 2D structured, 3D scene, and real-world reasoning.
- VAoT records textual thoughts, visual actions, rendered states, and subsequent reasoning under four controlled inference settings.
- Evaluation of representative multimodal models shows visual reasoning is strongly model- and environment-dependent, with no single setting consistently dominating.
- Process analysis indicates models select relevant visual operations, but faithful rendering is a bottleneck; high feedback uptake does not guarantee accuracy gains.
- Models exhibit behavioral dependence on visual states, with accuracy dropping over 10 percentage points under task-relevant corrupted visual feedback.

**Why it matters:** Understanding how multimodal models use intermediate visual states is crucial for developing more reliable and interpretable visual reasoning systems.

### [β-OPSD: Deriving with Policy Optimization, Training with Self-Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28582)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-29 · 👍 17 · `arXiv:2607.28582`

β-OPSD generalizes on-policy self-distillation (OPSD) by introducing β as a controllable regularization parameter, enabling efficient approximation of policy optimization through logit mixing and outperforming vanilla OPSD in reasoning tasks.

- Vanilla OPSD is identified as the β=1 member of a policy-optimization family, where β weights the KL penalty to a reference policy.
- β-OPSD's optimal policy is derived as a geometric interpolation between a reference policy and a privileged teacher.
- The closed-form solution of β-OPSD is approximated by mixing token-level logits from the reference and teacher policies, avoiding direct, high-variance reinforcement learning optimization.
- Return-to-go credit assignment is incorporated to align token updates with sequence-level objectives.
- Experiments on mathematical reasoning benchmarks demonstrate β-OPSD consistently outperforms vanilla OPSD in stability and performance.

**Why it matters:** This method provides a more stable and performant approach to self-distillation for reasoning language models, reducing engineering effort and improving practical applicability.

<small>Jiawei Xu, Minghui Liu, Juzheng Zhang, Tom Goldstein, Furong Huang</small>

### [Chimera: Designing and Chinchilla-Scaling Hybrid Visual Diffusion Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28611)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-29 · 👍 16 · `arXiv:2607.28611`

Chimera introduces a hybrid visual diffusion backbone with Kimi Delta Attention, Multi-head Latent Attention, and Sparse Mixture-of-Experts, scaled using HeteroP for compute-optimal performance in high-resolution image and long video generation.

- Chimera processes text, image, and video tokens in a single raster-ordered stream without positional embeddings, utilizing Kimi Delta Attention (O(N) complexity), interleaved Multi-head Latent Attention, and modality-aware short convolutions.
- The architecture incorporates Sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) layers to expand capacity while controlling activated compute.
- HeteroP, a module-wise scaling scheme, transfers hyperparameters across width and depth based on functional fan-in and model depth, enabling Chinchilla-style compute-optimal scaling laws.
- An 11B-parameter Chimera with 2B activated parameters demonstrated 7.3x compute efficiency compared to a full-attention Wan-2.1 2B baseline in pretraining diffusion loss.
- Chimera extrapolates zero-shot from 5-second training clips to 30-second videos with only 6.5% FID degradation in the last five seconds, without length-specific fine-tuning.

**Why it matters:** This work provides a foundation for designing and scaling efficient long-context diffusion architectures, enabling high-resolution image and long video generation with reduced computational cost.

### [ShadowDancer: Teaching Video World Models Any Action by Learning Unified Dynamics Representations from a Video and Its Shadow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28362)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-07-29 · 👍 16 · ⭐ 18 · `arXiv:2607.28362`

ShadowDancer enables frame-level control of video world models by learning unified dynamics representations from video pairs that replay the same dynamics with independently resampled appearances, improving action transfer and long rollout.

- ShadowDancer uses "shadow pairs," video pairs constructed at scale by a "Shadow Library," which replay identical dynamics with independently resampled appearances.
- It employs "cross-shadow prediction" to learn actions by predicting one shadow from the other, discarding appearance variations and preserving dynamics.
- This approach yields a unified dynamics representation that drives a block-causal world model, allowing any demonstrated clip to be a reusable action asset.
- Experiments show improved action transfer and long action rollout compared to latent-action and interactive world model baselines, with an 86% blinded win rate in rollout comparisons.

**Why it matters:** This method allows for precise, frame-level control of video world models using demonstrated actions without requiring action labels, motion estimators, or fine-tuning in new environments.

### [Tokenizer-Agnostic Engram Module](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29065v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-07-31 · `arXiv:2607.29065v1`

A modified Engram module replaces token-level N-gram XOR hashing with general polynomial hashing over byte sequences, enabling tokenizer-agnostic embedding reuse without sacrificing performance.

- Engram originally relies on token-level N-gram XOR hashing, tightly coupling embeddings to a specific tokenizer.
- The proposed method treats N-grams as sampled byte sequences from all possible token byte sequences using a joint embedding space across N.
- Polynomial hashing achieves hash equivalence for any byte-equivalent token sequences, regardless of the underlying tokenizer.
- Empirical results show the tokenizer-agnostic substitution delivers performance comparable to the original architecture.

**Why it matters:** It allows pre-trained Engram memory embeddings to be reused directly across models that employ different tokenizers, avoiding the need to retrain embeddings from scratch.

### [TransMem: Transforming Hidden States into Memory for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29032v1)

**arXiv cs.CL** · 2026-07-31 · `arXiv:2607.29032v1`

TransMem is a lightweight inference-time module that transforms sparse historical hidden states from a frozen LLM into reusable memory representations without repeatedly re-encoding past context.

- Uses a lightweight gating network to dynamically apply latent interventions to current hidden states.
- Employs evidence-conditioned self-distillation where a memory-augmented student matches the predictive distribution of an evidence-only teacher sharing the same frozen backbone.

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-21 · 👍 13 · ⭐ 10 · `arXiv:2610.31651`

### [Diffusion Language Models Change Speed Across Tasks and Languages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25835)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-19 · 👍 9 · `arXiv:2610.25835`

Analysis of two diffusion language models reveals that inference cost per token varies significantly by task, language tokenization density, and answer correctness, rather than remaining constant.

- DiffusionGemma requires over three times as many forward passes per token for open-ended story writing compared to input-heavy tasks like grammar correction.
- Per-token generation cost increases when outputs contain novel text relative to the input, rely on task-rare words, or involve highly tokenized multilingual scripts.
- Within reasoning traces, final answers that repeat prior chain-of-thought steps are computationally cheap, whereas introducing new content increases required forward passes.
- Incorrect solutions to math problems require more passes per token than correct ones, enabling a decoding-speed candidate weighting method that outperforms majority voting by up to 4.2 points without extra compute.

**Why it matters:** Understanding task-dependent computation in diffusion language models allows developers to predict inference costs more accurately and leverage decoding speed as a signal to improve model accuracy.

<small>Mohsen Fayyaz, Ali Modarressi, Daniel Israel, Lucas Bandarkar, Nanyun Peng</small>

### [A Fix for Softmax and Cross-Entropy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31642v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-10-22 · `arXiv:2610.31642v1`

A novel implementation of log-softmax, softmax, and cross-entropy fixes two numerical bugs—Softmax Collapse and Softmax Cancellation—improving gradient stability during training and distillation.

- Softmax Collapse rounds target logit gradients to zero when one logit dominates, causing gradients to violate the zero-sum property.
- Softmax Cancellation produces false non-zero gradients when student and teacher logits match during distillation.
- The proposed fix eliminates the Slingshot phenomenon, accelerates grokking with weight decay, and prevents accuracy collapse without weight decay.
- Bound logit growth post-memorization is established via an equilibrium between weight decay and Adam's epsilon hyperparameter.

**Why it matters:** Fixing these fundamental numerical instabilities prevents model drift during knowledge distillation and stabilizes long-term neural network training dynamics.

<small>Jonathan T. Barron, Dan Gnanapragasam, Michael Tschannen</small>

### [Same Luck, Different Choice: Twin-World Credit Assignment for Agentic Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31544v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-10-22 · `arXiv:2610.31544v1`

TWICE introduces twin-world credit assignment for agentic RL by re-using Gumbel noise across counterfactual rollouts to reduce advantage estimation variance.

- Identifies that deterministic elements in agentic search (retriever, corpus, grader) make twin worlds realizable by keying Gumbel sampling noise by turn and line.
- Derives an unbiased turn-level advantage estimator whose variance scales by 1-ρ (where ρ is outcome correlation), reducing required rollouts to resolve advantage Δ from Θ(Δ⁻²) to Θ(Δ⁻¹).
- Uses a square-root budget allocation rule to spend twin rollouts where outcomes are predicted to disagree per unit computational cost.
- Improves GRPO average exact match by 5.3 points on Qwen2.5-3B and 4.5 points on Qwen2.5-7B across seven QA benchmarks while using 68% fewer auxiliary tokens than VinePPO.

**Why it matters:** By isolating decision impact from downstream stochastic luck, TWICE significantly improves agentic RL performance while cutting the auxiliary compute costs of counterfactual credit assignment.

- CMAS evaluates typed graph edits against an incumbent via paired shadow replay using common random numbers and interventional component credit.
- Promotion requires a variance-penalized score above a set margin, followed by a simulated canary stage with pre-specified rollback mechanisms.

### [SpectralCanary: Embarrassingly Simple Low-Frequency Traces for T2I Dataset-Use Auditing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31659v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-10-22 · `arXiv:2610.31659v1`

SpectralCanary is a proactive text-to-image dataset-use auditing method that blends a low-frequency canary image into training data using a smooth low-pass mask without requiring auxiliary models or per-image optimization.

- Uses a smooth low-pass mask to embed a fixed canary image into dataset images while leaving text captions untouched.
- Achieves a 91.1% average per-generation TPR at 1% FPR and 94.8% model-level accuracy with 30 generations on the Pokemon dataset across two architecture families.
- Maintains high image fidelity across five diverse domain datasets using SD3.5, recording an SSIM of 0.989, LPIPS of 0.022, and a mean ΔFID of 0.05.
- Ablation studies confirm low-frequency signal placement transfers to model outputs more reliably than mid- or high-frequency placements.
- Resists adaptive removal attacks, which require aggressive image modifications exceeding 25 ΔFID points to successfully erase the signal.

**Why it matters:** It enables model owners and creators to reliably audit whether proprietary image collections were used to fine-tune text-to-image models with minimal impact on image quality.

### [BeNice! Closing Looping's Inference-Memory Gap via KV-Sharing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31633v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-10-22 · `arXiv:2610.31633v1`

BeNice! is a two-stage method combining pretraining stochastic depth with post-training KV-cache broadcasting to enable depth-wise KV-sharing in looped language architectures without losing model quality.

- Uses stochastic depth during pretraining to induce contractive residual dynamics that drive representations toward a common limit across loop recurrences.
- Applies BeNice! post-training to broadcast the final recurrence's KV-cache into the first recurrence, training it to produce compatible residuals.
- Matches standard finetuning unshared validation perplexity while eliminating the linear KV-cache memory growth of looped models.
- Composes with low-precision quantization (FP8, MXFP8, NVFP4) and efficient sequence mixers like Multi-Latent Attention and Kimi Delta Attention.
- Improves simulated low-memory long-context performance, raising cache hit rates by up to 31.4%, boosting throughput by up to 23.6%, and reducing p90 time-to-first-token by up to 83.6%.

**Why it matters:** It eliminates the linear KV-cache memory overhead inherent to looped parameter-efficient architectures, significantly reducing latency and memory usage during long-context inference.

<small>Hayden Prairie, Jacob M. Ryan, Taylor Berg-Kirkpatrick, Daniel Y. Fu</small>

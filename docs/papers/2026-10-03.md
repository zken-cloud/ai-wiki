### [On-Policy or Off-Policy Learning? A Systematic Study of Distillation Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35259)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-27 · 👍 130 · `arXiv:2609.35259`

A controlled study on Llama3 and Qwen2.5 shows rollout policy is less critical than token-level KL direction for task performance, while learning rate drives forgetting and update sparsity.

- Forward KL is highly robust to rollout policy shifts, maintaining stable performance across the student-teacher policy spectrum.
- Reverse KL is significantly more sensitive to the rollout policy and performs better when using student-generated rollouts.
- Learning rate, rather than on-policy data, primarily governs catastrophic forgetting and the sparsity of parameter updates.
- On-policy data enhances generalization on harder Countdown arithmetic variants for both KL directions, but the advantage wanes after subsequent RLVR.

**Why it matters:** The findings challenge the assumption that on-policy rollouts are inherently superior, helping practitioners optimize distillation pipelines by prioritizing KL direction and learning rates over complex policy data generation.

<small>Julianna Piskorz, Antonin Berthon, Mihaela van der Schaar</small>

### [Transformers Stop Thinking Too Early, and a Tiny LoRA Fixes It](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36585)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-28 · 👍 55 · ⭐ 2 · `arXiv:2609.36585`

A task-trained rank-8 LoRA applied to a single early layer enables frozen pretrained transformers to process significantly longer in-context reference chains by initiating a hidden state relay across middle layers.

- Pretrained transformers default to following only 1.4-3.6 lines of context references, and adding pretrained loops yields minimal gain.
- Applying a rank-8 LoRA to one early layer improves Qwen3-8B's exact accuracy on 24-line chains from 15.5% to 99%, with longer training extending performance to 50 lines.
- Ouro-1.4B with the LoRA edit processes 60 lines after four loops and at least 160 lines after eight loops.
- The edit triggers a relay where middle layers pass chain identity and frozen heads attend progressively further up the chain; removing parent-line attention breaks the mechanism.
- A frozen-model measurement successfully predicts the last effective intervention layer within tolerance across three of four held-out models.

**Why it matters:** A minimal, single-layer parameter edit unlocks deep contextual reasoning capabilities that remain latent and underutilized in default pretrained transformers.

<small>Zehao Jin, Ruixuan Deng, Junran Wang</small>

### [Decoding Looped Transformers Better for (Almost) Free](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02185)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-30 · 👍 19 · `arXiv:2610.02185`

LoopCD is a training-free contrastive decoding framework for looped Transformers that uses intermediate recurrent states as guidance to improve inference quality while reducing compute.

- Leverages inherent weak-and-strong prediction pairs from early and late recurrent passes without requiring auxiliary models or external training.
- Offers two implementation variants: LoopCD-Logits (contrasts predictions in logit space with one extra output pass) and LoopCD-Hidden (contrasts in hidden-state space with zero output overhead).
- LoopCD-Logits increases Ouro-2.6B-Thinking's AIME 2024 pass@1 from 61.88% to 73.33%, while LoopCD-Hidden improves Huginn's HumanEval pass@1 from 22.56% to 31.71%.
- Allows halving the number of recurrent loops to match or exceed full-depth unguided baselines, reducing forward FLOPs by 22.5% to 48.2%.

**Why it matters:** It improves LLM reasoning performance and significantly cuts inference FLOPs for parameter-efficient looped architectures without requiring additional model training.

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-09-29 · 👍 10 · `arXiv:2609.39687`

Neighborhood On-Policy Self-Distillation (N-OPSD) improves mathematical reasoning models by using local parameter perturbations to create a pool of frozen expert teachers that supervise student-sampled prefixes.

- Offline pool selection uses greedy optimization to pick frozen experts based on filtered reference-token gains beyond the pool's current best at each position.
- Online routing separates anchor selection via MaxPeak from support-level selection using quantile choice among experts matching the anchor token.
- Students learn the selected expert's full next-token distribution using OPSD's clipped forward-KL objective, retaining standard single-model inference.

Group Mass Capping GRPO (GMC-GRPO) mitigates stale-rollout bias in asynchronous LLM post-training, reducing the fourth-order delay term's threshold dependence from O(ε⁻⁴) to O(ε⁻²).

- Derived a GRPO convergence bound that isolates the tradeoff between the second moment of trajectory-level importance-weighted estimators and bias from clipping or rescaling.

- Blind-trained RL models using test images recover 50% (at 3B) to nearly 80% (at 7B) of real-image benchmark gains, proving standard benchmarks permit language-only shortcuts.

### [FERPO: Forward Entropy-Regularized Policy Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02198v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-10-01 · `arXiv:2610.02198v1`

Forward Entropy-Regularized Policy Optimization (FERPO) is an on-policy maximum entropy RL algorithm that updates actors using critic values directly, avoiding action-derivative critic gradients.

- Eliminates the need to differentiate the critic with respect to actions by deriving an optimal target action distribution from entropy- and KL-regularized policy improvement.
- Fits the actor to the target distribution by minimizing a forward Kullback-Leibler (KL) objective using self-normalized importance sampling (SNIS).
- Uses KL regularization to restrict target distribution deviation from the rollout policy, maintaining well-behaved importance weights.
- Leverages forward-KL to cover multiple high-value action modes and enhance exploration, contrasting with reverse-KL mode-seeking behavior.
- Demonstrates improved sample efficiency on MuJoCo Playground and ManiSkill, with faster actor updates than Relative Entropy Pathwise Policy Optimization (REPPO).

**Why it matters:** FERPO avoids unreliable policy updates caused by inaccurate critic action-derivatives while offering faster execution and better multi-modal exploration in continuous control tasks.

### [Local Support Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02126v1)

**arXiv cs.AI** · 2026-10-01 · `arXiv:2610.02126v1`

Local Support Learning (LSL) prevents catastrophic forgetting in large pre-trained models without using prior data by pairing weight adapters with Gaussian Mixture Model gating functions that restrict updates to local input activation distributions.

- Models catastrophic forgetting as a geometric problem in the input space of individual weight matrices.
- Pairs a standard trainable weight adapter with a Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) gating function.
- Uses the GMM's rapid likelihood decay away from current training data to keep the adapter closed on inputs from prior learning phases.
- Successfully retains pre-trained and fine-tuned capabilities across multiple phases in LLMs up to 7B parameters without relying on past data.

**Why it matters:** LSL enables continual post-training of 7B-parameter LLMs while preserving past capabilities without the compute and storage overhead of retaining prior dataset copies.

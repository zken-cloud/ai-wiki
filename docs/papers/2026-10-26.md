### [Reading, Writing, Sharing, and Interacting with the World through Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31668)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-21 · 👍 193 · ⭐ 20 · `arXiv:2610.31668`

A new survey proposes world modeling as a unifying framework to categorize video foundation model literature across four core operations: reading, writing, sharing, and interacting.

- Frames video as a partial, lossy, temporally ordered projection of an underlying latent temporal world.
- Categorizes literature into reading (inferring world properties), writing (synthesizing video from conditions), sharing (unifying generation and understanding), and interacting (updating states via actions).

- Traces a domain shift from clip-level visual recognition and perceptual fidelity toward persistent state, controllable evolution, and causal/physical validity.

**Why it matters:** Structuring video models around latent world states rather than surface pixel dynamics guides the development of models capable of robust physical reasoning and interactive downstream decision-making.

### [Every Weight Is a Path of Shared Discrete Decisions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31931)

**Hugging Face Daily Papers** · 2026-10-21 · 👍 15 · ⭐ 2 · `arXiv:2610.31931`

TWIG is a neural network weight representation where every weight is a path of inherited discrete decisions, reducing circuit addition costs and unifying compression techniques into a single optimization problem.

- Represents network weights as paths of shared, inherited discrete decisions including signs, power-of-two shifts, zero gates, and shared codebook indices.
- Unifies pruning, quantization, low-rank factorization, codebooks, and weight sharing into edits of a single tree-structured object optimized by a single optimizer.
- Formulates discrete decision selection as a higher-order Boltzmann machine ground-state problem compatible with classical annealers and quantum optimizers.
- Replaces general multipliers with circuits composed primarily of wires and adders when using power-of-two decisions.
- Outperforms GPTQ on five LLM weight matrices at 3- and 4-bit accuracy, using 9-15% fewer additions on typical matrices and 20-55% fewer additions on matrices with strong outliers at equal layer error.

**Why it matters:** TWIG simplifies hardware deployment on chips with fixed weights by eliminating dedicated multipliers and provides a unified framework for model compression.

<small>Ammar Ali, Stamatis Lefkimmiatis</small>

- Mathematical proof shows Lipschitz location encoders are constrained to an intrinsic output dimension of at most 2, causing contrastive loss to exceed its optimum and collapsing embedding covariance.

**Why it matters:** LoCA provides a theoretically grounded method to map single geospatial coordinates to diverse multimodal targets, significantly improving downstream performance across broad earth observation and climate tasks.

This study analyzes Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) as a singular statistical model, evaluating its learning complexity via the real log canonical threshold (RLCT) and forward-backward covariance geometry.

### [Direction-Aware Token Credit Assignment with Reward-Model Gradients](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32772v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-10-23 · `arXiv:2610.32772v1`

Direction-Aware Token Credit Assignment (DATC) improves GRPO by mapping frozen reward-model gradients into policy logit space to weight token updates based on their alignment with the reward gradient direction.

- GRPO applies a uniform response-level advantage across all tokens, which can create token-level updates that do not align with local reward increases.
- DATC projects frozen reward-model gradients into policy logit space to measure alignment between the reward direction and GRPO's signed sampled-action update direction.
- The method allocates token-specific weights based on this directional alignment while maintaining the original sign and mean of the response-level advantage.
- DATC achieved higher performance than standard GRPO across four experimental settings evaluated on two model families.

**Why it matters:** DATC provides a more mathematically aligned token-level credit assignment mechanism for reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) without altering the global response advantage structure.

### [From Parameter Rebasin to Functional Transfer Across Heterogeneous Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32581v1)

**arXiv cs.LG** · 2026-10-23 · `arXiv:2610.32581v1`

Ariadne is a training-free method that transfers task-specific behavior across heterogeneous Transformers by modeling local activation changes rather than transporting parameters.

- Replaces parameter transport by characterizing fine-tuning as activation changes induced at matched transformer blocks.
- Aligns activation effects to a target base model using local Procrustes maps.
- Synthesizes native target model parameter updates directly using closed-form ridge regression without resizing source models.
- Reduces time and memory overhead compared to previous cross-architecture rebasin approaches across model expansion, reduction, and language-model transfer.

**Why it matters:** It enables efficient reuse of task-specific fine-tuning updates across Transformers of different widths and depths without expensive retrainings or complex parameter-resizing pipelines.

A new conditional autoencoder framework achieves principled nonlinear concept erasure with formal guardedness guarantees while enabling concept steering without target-task labels or extra training costs.

- Proves that under specific assumptions, population-level independence combined with perfect reconstruction guarantees perfect nonlinear guardedness.
- Provides theoretical representation preservation guarantees when operating in imperfect-reconstruction regimes.
- Outperforms supervised variants of competing erasure methods on utility-erasure trade-offs without requiring target-task labels.

**Why it matters:** It enables safer AI representations by removing sensitive attributes with theoretical guarantees against nonlinear probes while simultaneously allowing representation steering without sacrificing utility.

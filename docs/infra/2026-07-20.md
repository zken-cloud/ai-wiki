### [Bristol Myers Squibb Building Life Science Industry’s Most Advanced AI Factory on NVIDIA Vera Rubin](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/bristol-myers-squibb-building-life-science-industrys-most-advanced-ai-factory-on-nvidia-vera-rubin/)

**NVIDIA Blog** · 2026-07-20

Bristol Myers Squibb (BMS) is deploying a second NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD, built on eight DGX Vera Rubin NVL72 systems, to create the life science industry's most advanced and energy-efficient AI cluster.

- BMS's new NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD utilizes eight DGX Vera Rubin NVL72 systems, offering up to 10x performance per megawatt compared to previous infrastructure.
- The platform includes NVIDIA BioNeMo Agent Toolkit for biological AI, supporting predictions, model training, and agentic workflows across drug discovery.
- AI is used for target identification, expanding CELMoD compound libraries for blood cancer treatment, and lead optimization via a "Predict First" methodology.
- The new system aims to provide limitless compute access to all scientists, addressing current saturation from large-scale predictions and foundational model building.

**Why it matters:** This expansion aims to accelerate drug discovery by enabling faster cycles, exploring larger chemical spaces, and allowing researchers to focus on scientific problems rather than resource logistics.

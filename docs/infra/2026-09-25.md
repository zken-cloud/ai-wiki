### [Efficient MoE Training for Biological Foundation Models](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/efficient-moe-training-for-biological-foundation-models/)

**NVIDIA Developer Blog** · 2026-09-24

Mixture-of-experts (MoE) architectures enable scaling of biological foundation models by activating only a small subset of expert subnetworks per token, reducing computational costs compared to dense transformers.

- Dense transformer scaling increases computational costs for both training and inference as all tokens pass through every layer.
- MoE architectures scale efficiently by utilizing many specialized subnetworks (experts).
- MoE models route individual tokens to a small subset of available experts rather than processing them through the full network.

**Why it matters:** Adopting MoE architectures allows biological foundation models to scale up capabilities without incurring the prohibitive computational overhead of traditional dense transformers.

### [Watermarking in vLLM](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-09-24-watermarking-in-vllm)

**vLLM Blog** · 2026-09-24

vLLM has integrated a distribution-preserving Gumbel-max text watermarking mechanism using efficient GPU kernels, speculative decoding support, and statistical detection to track LLM output provenance without altering model generation quality.

- Text watermarking works by substituting fresh random sampling values with reproducible, keyed random values derived from recent context and candidate tokens.
- The implementation leverages the Gumbel-max trick to alter the generation process without modifying the expected output distribution or favoring specific writing styles.
- Detection requires minimal metadata, reconstructing the context-derived keyed values to statistically verify alignment over a sequence.
- The engineering design addresses throughput and latency overheads in high-throughput serving environments, even for small models or large vocabulary sizes, while maintaining speculative decoding compatibility.

**Why it matters:** This allows high-throughput LLM serving systems like vLLM to provide robust, undetectable text provenance and tracking without sacrificing generation quality or inference performance.

### [Speaker-labeled transcription with WhisperX on SageMaker AI](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/speaker-labeled-transcription-with-whisperx-on-sagemaker-ai/)

**AWS Machine Learning Blog** · 2026-09-24

AWS has introduced a maintained WhisperX Deep Learning Container (DLC) on Amazon SageMaker AI, enabling GPU-accelerated speech-to-text with word-level timestamps and speaker diarization.

- Integrates OpenAI Whisper with batched inference, wav2vec2 forced alignment for per-word timestamps, and speaker diarization without requiring a Hugging Face token.
- Follows SageMaker AI serving contracts on port 8080 (POST /invocations, GET /ping) using multipart/form-data for audio and parameters like language, diarize, and response_format.
- Supports multiple output formats including json, verbose_json, srt, and vtt for analytics and video captioning pipelines.
- Offers deployment flexibility via real-time endpoints for short interactive clips (under the 60-second limit) and asynchronous endpoints for long audio files.

**Why it matters:** The pre-packaged DLC allows enterprise teams to deploy production-ready, highly accurate speaker-labeled transcription pipelines on SageMaker without building custom GPU container images.

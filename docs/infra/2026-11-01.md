### [Managing Amazon Bedrock costs end to end: A playbook for FinOps and platform teams](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/managing-amazon-bedrock-costs-end-to-end-a-playbook-for-finops-and-platform-teams/)

**AWS Machine Learning Blog** · 2026-10-30

AWS outlines a five-stage operational framework for FinOps and platform teams to manage and optimize Amazon Bedrock costs across scaling and agentic AI workloads.

- Agentic AI workflows scale token consumption by context size and task autonomy rather than simple user turns, necessitating architectural cost controls.
- Granular cost attribution automatically maps Amazon Bedrock inference spending to the caller's IAM principal ARN via the line_item_iam_principal column in AWS CUR 2.0.
- The framework follows a sequential five-stage loop: Attribute, Observe, Enforce, Optimize, and Operate.
- Platform teams centrally mitigate waste through model routing, prompt caching, and token limits, while FinOps leads attribution and budget enforcement.

**Why it matters:** It enables organizations to systematically attribute, observe, and cap generative AI spending down to individual IAM principals before scaling usage creates uncontrolled cost overruns.

### [NVIDIA Vera Rubin Maximizes Intelligence per Dollar for Post-Training Workloads — a Key Metric for Agentic AI](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-vera-rubin-post-training-intelligence-per-dollar/)

**NVIDIA Blog** · 2026-07-17

NVIDIA introduces "intelligence per dollar" as a key metric for agentic AI, emphasizing continuous post-training for models that adapt to dynamic environments, distinct from traditional cost per token for inference.

- Agentic AI requires continuous post-training to adapt to shifting environments, emerging edge cases, and changing tools, unlike generative models responding to static prompts.
- Post-training builds intelligence through reinforcement learning (RL) techniques, involving forward passes (attempts), scoring, and backward passes (weight updates) across millions of iterations.
- NVIDIA NeMo open libraries, including NeMo Gym for training environments and NeMo RL for distributed post-training, provide infrastructure for scalable post-training.
- The NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra, a 550-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts (MoE) model, achieved 71.7% on the SWE-bench coding benchmark using a disclosed post-training recipe on NeMo RL.

**Why it matters:** This shift in focus to continuous post-training and the "intelligence per dollar" metric is crucial for developing and evaluating agentic AI systems that can operate effectively in real-world, dynamic conditions.

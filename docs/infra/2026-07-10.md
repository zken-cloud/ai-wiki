### [Towards Free Normalization: Fusing Normalization into GEMM and Attention Kernels](https://pytorch.org/blog/towards-free-normalization-fusing-normalization-into-gemm-and-attention-kernels/)

**PyTorch Blog** · 2026-07-10

Novel kernel fusion techniques for LayerNorm and RMSNorm significantly reduce memory-IO overhead by fusing normalization operations into GEMM and attention kernels, achieving up to 90% latency hiding for normalization and 35% kernel speedup for attention.

- Normalization operations, such as LayerNorm and RMSNorm, are highly memory-bound and can account for 10-20% of total training latency in LLMs and recommendation models.
- Techniques like Lazy Pre-Norm and Multi-CTA Norm Fusion hide up to 90% of normalization kernel latency by fusing them with GEMMs.
- The FlashNormAttention algorithm fuses multiple normalizations around an attention kernel (GDPA), achieving up to 35% kernel speedup.
- Kernel DSLs TLX (Triton extensions) and Helion (high-level DSL) were used for implementation, with benchmarks performed on NVIDIA B200 GPUs using bfloat16 data type.
- Elementwise affines were disabled in benchmarks due to significant performance overhead with marginal model quality effects.

**Why it matters:** These fusion techniques improve hardware compute utilization by reducing the memory-IO bottleneck of normalization operations, leading to faster training for large language models and recommendation systems.

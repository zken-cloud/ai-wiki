### [Migrating vLLM to the LibTorch Stable ABI](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-11-06-stable-abi-cuda)

**vLLM Blog** · 2026-11-06

vLLM migrated its CUDA build and C++ dependencies to the LibTorch Stable ABI as of version 0.33.0, enabling PyTorch upgrades without rebuilding vLLM.

- The LibTorch Stable ABI relies on a three-layer architecture: a C shim exposing selected APIs, C++ wrapper functions, and header-only utilities in torch::headeronly.
- Unsupported ops during the migration, such as is_pinned(), were executed in an ABI-stable manner using the general-purpose torch_call_dispatcher C shim to call ATen ops through the dispatcher.
- PyTorch added a native stable C++ is_pinned() method in version 2.14 based on feedback from vLLM's migration.
- Using the Stable ABI introduces a small amount of additional host-side overhead.

**Why it matters:** Users and package distributors can swap or update PyTorch wheels directly without undergoing the time-consuming process of rebuilding and relinking vLLM.

### [Zero Mismatch, Simple Async RL: REINFORCE Zero, DeepSeek-V4.1 and Kimi K3.1](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-11-06-zero-mismatch-async-rl)

**vLLM Blog** · 2026-11-06

NVIDIA Research and Inferact introduced REINFORCE Zero, demonstrating that bit-for-bit kernel alignment combined with unclipped importance sampling enables stable asynchronous RL under weight lags up to lag 64.

- Logprob discrepancy in async RL splits into weight lag (log π_θk − log π_θv) and kernel mismatch (log π_θv − log μ_v).
- Weight lag changes smoothly over updates and can be corrected with importance sampling (IS), whereas kernel mismatch fluctuates due to batch composition and execution details.
- Unclipped importance sampling reweights tokens by π/μ, leveraging exact kernel alignment (zero mismatch) to isolate and eliminate non-deterministic kernel variance.
- REINFORCE Zero was demonstrated on DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B, handling weight lags of 2 and 8 on math reasoning, and up to lag 64 on agentic tool use.

**Why it matters:** Eliminating kernel mismatch ensures that importance sampling accurately corrects for asynchronous weight lag, preventing training instability in high-throughput off-policy RL.

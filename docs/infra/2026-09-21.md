### [PD Serving of Qwen3.8-2.4T](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-09-21-qwen38-pd-serving)

**vLLM Blog** · 2026-09-21

vLLM achieved 5,000 total tokens/s per GPU throughput and 180 generated tokens/s per user interactivity serving the Qwen3.8-2.4T model using Prompt-Decode disaggregation on a GB300 NVL72 cluster.

- Evaluated on an 8K/1K workload, demonstrating the full Pareto frontier for both high-throughput and low-latency scenarios.
- Qwen3.8-2.4T architecture features 92 total layers (69 GDN layers and 23 Full-Attn layers) with NVFP4 MoE blocks (92 × 512 experts) and BF16 attention layers.
- Decoupled state sizing requires accounting for token-dependent Full-Attn KV cache (2 KiB per layer per token) and static per-request GDN state (4,216 KiB total per request).
- Provides reproducible srt-slurm recipes and a step-by-step decision framework for tuning KV cache block allocations and concurrency limits.

**Why it matters:** It establishes benchmark performance metrics and a reusable optimization methodology for serving trillion-parameter hybrid MoE models efficiently using disaggregated serving architectures.

### [Inside Petal: Building the World’s First Petabit-Class Transoceanic Subsea Cable](https://engineering.fb.com/2026/09/21/connectivity/petal-petabit-transoceanic-subsea-cable/)

**Meta Engineering** · 2026-09-21

Meta announced Petal, a 7,000 km transatlantic subsea cable planned for 2029 that uses 2-core multi-core fiber to achieve a record 1 Pbps capacity.

- Petal connects France and the US over ~7,000 km, doubling the 0.5 Pbps capacity of the 24-fiber-pair Anjana cable to achieve 1 Pbps (1,000 Tbps).
- The system deploys 2-core fiber technology across 24 fiber pairs, delivering optical throughput equivalent to a 48-fiber-pair system.
- The multi-core fiber maintains a standard 125 µm outer diameter, manufactured by Sumitomo Electric from preforms stretched from 20 cm wide to thousands of kilometers long.
- Built in partnership with NEC, Sumitomo Electric, and Orange, the cable avoids doubling physical infrastructure, materials, and power demands to scale capacity.

**Why it matters:** Petal introduces multi-core fiber at scale to bypass the Shannon Limit, allowing transoceanic networks to double data throughput without increasing physical cable size or environmental footprint.

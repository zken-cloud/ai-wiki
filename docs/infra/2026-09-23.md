**PyTorch Blog** · 2026-09-22

vLLM is introducing new hardware-agnostic layers to maintain portability across non-frontier GPUs and accelerators while native model definitions shift toward flat, hardware-specific optimizations that break fullgraph torch.compile compatibility.

- vLLM is shifting toward hardware-specific 'flat' models in vllm/models/ using custom fusions instead of torch.compile to maximize performance on newer architectures like NVIDIA Blackwell and GB300 NVL72.
- The refactoring of underlying layers and ops to support flat models makes them fundamentally incompatible with fullgraph torch.compile tracing and Dynamo.
- Hardware-agnostic layers allow vLLM to preserve model execution portability for out-of-tree accelerators, older GPUs, and non-standard architectures.
- On NVIDIA H100 GPUs, the hardware-agnostic layers achieve total token throughput within 3.4% (geometric mean across three recent models) of the native implementation.

**Why it matters:** This architectural split allows vLLM to achieve peak performance on cutting-edge hardware without abandoning support for older GPUs and alternative AI accelerators.

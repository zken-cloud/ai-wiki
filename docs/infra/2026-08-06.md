### [From User Sequences to Scaling Laws: A Multi-Stage Architecture for Meta’s Ads Ranking](https://engineering.fb.com/2026/08/05/ml-applications/from-user-sequences-to-scaling-laws-a-multi-stage-architecture-for-metas-ads-ranking/)

**Meta Engineering** · 2026-08-05

Meta introduced a multi-stage sequence model for ads ranking that decouples offline transformer user modeling from online ranking and applies dense tokenization with target-aware attention to establish predictable LLM-style scaling laws.

- Decouples heavy offline user sequence modeling from lightweight online ad ranking, transferring cached embeddings via asynchronous processing.
- Combines dense tokenization and target-aware attention to learn feature interactions directly from data, reducing reliance on manual feature engineering.
- Eliminates scaling ceilings and lossy knowledge transfer inherent in traditional hybrid sequence/sparse ranking models.
- Achieved a 6% conversion lift on Instagram, a 3% conversion lift on Facebook, and a 3.5% ad click lift on Facebook as part of Meta's GEM platform.

**Why it matters:** This architecture enables ads recommendation platforms to scale sequence transformer lengths and model parameters predictably without incurring millisecond-level online latency penalties.

### [NVIDIA Vera Rubin Driving Performance Per Watt, Lowest Token Cost for Partners Worldwide](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/vera-rubin/)

**NVIDIA Blog** · 2026-07-21

NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 production is ramping up, offering 10x more throughput per megawatt than Grace Blackwell NVL72, driven by extreme codesign across seven chips and five rack trays.

- Vera Rubin NVL72 achieves 10x more throughput per megawatt than Grace Blackwell NVL72, as benchmarked by CoreWeave on DeepSeek-R1.
- The platform is built with extreme codesign across seven chips (Vera Rubin NVL72, Vera CPU, Groq 3 LPX, Spectrum-6 SPX, Vera BlueField-4 STX) and five rack trays.
- The Vera CPU features custom Olympus cores, delivering 2x single-threaded performance, 3x core-to-core bandwidth, and 40% lower memory latency compared to competing chiplet designs.
- Networking includes sixth-generation NVLink scale-up with 2x throughput, 3x lower latency, and 10x higher packet rates, and Spectrum-X Ethernet with 1.6x higher RDMA bandwidth via 102.4T Spectrum-6 switches and 1.6T ConnectX-9 SuperNICs.
- The Vera Rubin NVL72 system design eliminates cables, fans, and hoses in the tray, reducing assembly time to one minute and enabling chiller-free dry-cooler operation with 45-degree Celsius liquid cooling inlet.

**Why it matters:** This advancement provides significantly higher performance per watt and lower token cost, addressing critical power and cost constraints for AI factories and enabling large-scale AI infrastructure deployment.

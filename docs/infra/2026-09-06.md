### [NVIDIA to Acquire Hugging Face](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-to-acquire-hugging-face/)

**NVIDIA Blog** · 2026-09-03

NVIDIA has agreed to acquire open-source AI platform Hugging Face for $12.93 billion while maintaining its platform neutrality and multi-hardware support.

- NVIDIA will purchase Hugging Face for $12,930,300,000.
- Hugging Face currently hosts over 3 million models, 500,000 datasets, and 1 million applications, serving 18 million users and 200,000 companies.
- NVIDIA is the platform's largest contributor, having released over 500 models and 250 open datasets on Hugging Face.
- The platform will remain open and vendor-neutral, continuing to support multi-cloud, multi-accelerator deployment without requiring NVIDIA hardware.

**Why it matters:** The acquisition combines NVIDIA's infrastructure with Hugging Face's developer ecosystem while explicitly preserving open-weight model availability and non-NVIDIA hardware compatibility.

### [VeRL-Omni v0.2.0: Faster Diffusion RL and Stable Omni Training](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-08-20-verl-omni-v0-2-0)

**vLLM Blog** · 2026-08-20

VeRL-Omni v0.2.0 accelerates diffusion RL and stabilizes omni-modal training by introducing request-level batching in vLLM-Omni and a V1 trainer architecture.

- Replaced serial B≈1 DiT rollout forwards with request-level batching, packing multiple complete requests into single transformer forwards.
- Integrated a V1 trainer path for diffusion RL, laying the foundation for decoupled rollout and training execution.
- Increased GPU utilization from ~80% to ~100% on the Qwen-Image FlowGRPO LoRA recipe, reducing isolated generation time from 226s to 108s (a 52% reduction).
- Resolved correctness issues involving request-batched diffusion log-probs, async rollout semantics, rank-local LoRA weight updates, and rollout-correction hooks.

**Why it matters:** The performance and architectural upgrades significantly reduce rollout latency and compute costs for high-dimensional diffusion post-training and RLHF workloads.

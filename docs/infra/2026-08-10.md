### [Firebird Launches CIS Region’s Largest AI Factory in Armenia](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/firebird-ai-factory-armenia-blackwell-rubin-dsx/)

**NVIDIA Blog** · 2026-08-08

Firebird launched the CIS region's largest AI factory in Armenia using NVIDIA accelerated computing and Dell infrastructure, with plans to deploy over 70,000 NVIDIA Rubin and Blackwell GPUs by late 2027.

- Targets 300 MW of capacity in Armenia by 2027, built on the NVIDIA DSX platform to run up to 40% more GPUs per footprint.
- Part of a broader 2-gigawatt infrastructure roadmap spanning Armenia, Kazakhstan, and other frontier markets.
- Utilizes Schneider Electric medium/low-voltage switchgear, three-phase UPS, and Vertiv TrimCooler chilled-water architecture with iCOM CWM controls.
- Backed by an earlier investment from CoreWeave and a planned investment from NVIDIA, with early client demand from Perplexity.

**Why it matters:** The deployment establishes localized, high-density AI compute capacity in the CIS region to support sovereign AI model development and enterprise workloads.

### [Generate Trajectories, Reasoning Traces, and Auto-Labels with NVIDIA Alpamayo 2 Super](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/generate-trajectories-reasoning-traces-and-auto-labels-with-nvidia-alpamayo-2-super/)

**NVIDIA Developer Blog** · 2026-08-04

NVIDIA has released Alpamayo 2 Super, an open 34-billion-parameter reasoning vision model designed to unify trajectory generation, intent prediction, scene understanding, and data labeling for autonomous vehicle development.

- Alpamayo 2 Super features 34 billion parameters to handle multi-task AV workloads within a single framework.
- The unified architecture replaces separate, disparate models for scene understanding, auto-labeling, intent prediction, and trajectory generation.
- Consolidating these features enables shared representation reuse and simplifies the investigation of model behavior across the AV development pipeline.

**Why it matters:** By unifying disparate autonomous vehicle tasks into a single open 34B model, developers can streamline data labeling and improve output consistency across reasoning and trajectory generation workflows.

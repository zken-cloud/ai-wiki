### [Developing Healthcare Robotics with GPU-Native Medical Physics Simulation](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/developing-healthcare-robotics-with-gpu-native-medical-physics-simulation/)

**NVIDIA Developer Blog** · 2026-07-28

Healthcare robotics development faces challenges due to limited data collection and real-world experimentation, unlike autonomous driving or industrial robotics.

- Healthcare robotics cannot rely on internet-scale data collection.
- Unlimited real-world experimentation is not feasible for healthcare robotics.
- Each demonstration in healthcare robotics requires specialized equipment, clinical expertise, and patient/laboratory access.
- A fundamental challenge for developers is the data gap in training modern robotic policies.

**Why it matters:** The unique constraints of healthcare robotics necessitate alternative development methodologies to overcome data and experimentation limitations.

### [FP8 Training on AMD GPUs with TorchTitan and TorchAO: Upstreaming Performance Improvements](https://pytorch.org/blog/fp8-training-on-amd-gpus-with-torchtitan-and-torchao-upstreaming-performance-improvements/)

**PyTorch Blog** · 2026-08-13

AMD has upstreamed Primus-Turbo FP8 training optimizations into PyTorch's TorchAO and TorchTitan, enabling direct, high-performance FP8 execution on AMD Instinct GPUs.

- Delivered a 13.4% training throughput gain over BF16 on dense Llama3-8B models using rowwise FP8 with weight-gradient GEMMs retained in BF16.
- Integrated native hardware auto-detection for AMD's e4m3fnuz FP8 format (max value 240, no NaN/Inf) to resolve gradient corruption and clipping.
- Built a Triton fusion pipeline for DeepSeek-V3 671B MoE shapes that recovered 89% of FP8 quantization overhead, including a 6.2x speedup per MoE layer via colwise scales coalescing.
- Achieved a 4.2x backward pass acceleration on DeepSeek-MoE-16B by removing backward transposes and fusing kernels.

**Why it matters:** The upstreamed changes allow AI practitioners to leverage out-of-the-box, highly optimized FP8 training on large-scale AMD Instinct MI300X clusters without relying on external, out-of-tree libraries.

**NVIDIA Blog** · 2026-08-12

- B200 Blackwell cloud capacity commands premium rates ranging from approximately $5.30 to $7.05 per GPU-hour.
- NVIDIA DSX AI factories provide fungible architecture capable of running language, vision, speech, biology, physical AI, and robotics workloads across multiple clouds and operators.

**Why it matters:** Institutional financing platforms treat AI compute as a long-term, redeployable asset class, enabling AI clouds and enterprises to scale data center infrastructure without traditional capital constraints.
